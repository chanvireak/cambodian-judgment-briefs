# Corrections and contributions

This corpus is machine-generated and will contain errors. Corrections are welcome.

## Reporting a mistake in a brief

Open an issue with:

1. the doc id (the number in `briefs/<id>.md`),
2. the section and the `[Para N]` cite you are disputing,
3. what the judgment actually says at that line,
4. if you can, a short quote of the Khmer text.

A correction that cites the judgment beats one that cites another summary.

## Sending a pull request

- Edit only `briefs/<id>.md` or `reports/synthesis.md`; `reports/digest.md` and `reports/index.md` are
  generated.
- Keep the six-section shape and the `[Para N]` citation style. Every section needs at least one cite.
- Do not expand a name the OCR reduced to an initial, and do not resolve a contradiction between two parts
  of a judgment — list both in section 6.
- If you change the law stated in a brief, say in the PR which paragraph supports your version.

## Disagreeing about method

Issues about the citation scheme, the outcome labels or the digest's groupings are welcome too. Say what you
would do instead and why.
