# Mr D v Mr K (Civil Judgment No. 829 "ម", Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 4 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Pouy Sao) · first instance · Doc ID 142327

## 1. Facts
On 3 December 2015 Mr K signed a loan contract with Mr D, a Japanese national resident in Kyoto, for 53,782 USD (fifty-three thousand seven hundred and eighty-two), repayable by 30 November 2021, the money taken as capital for an agricultural venture. [Para 11][Para 12] The venture did not progress and communication broke down; on 2 March 2018 Mr D served written notice terminating the contract and demanding repayment of the whole sum. [Para 14][Para 15] Mr K signed for the notice on 8 March 2018, ignored the later notices and repaid nothing. [Para 38] Mr D sued for the principal, 5% annual interest from 2 March 2018 and 5,000 USD in legal service costs. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 10]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the "I OWE YOU NOTE" of 3 December 2015 is a valid and enforceable loan contract although written in a foreign language and lacking the detail of an ordinary loan agreement? [Para 42][Para 54]
2. Whether the lender could terminate the contract and reclaim the principal before the 30 November 2021 repayment date, and whether the termination notice reached the borrower? [Para 42][Para 70][Para 75]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr D, lender):** the denial of the contract is contrary to the facts — the note records the principal, the repayment period, the date and the identity of both parties, bears their signatures and thumbprints, and so expresses true consent under Article 311; the borrower signed receipt of the termination notice on 8 March 2018 and knew of the follow-up notices, so the termination took effect from then and his conduct is evasion. [Para 33][Para 35][Para 38]
**Defendant (Mr K, borrower):** he does not admit the loan or the exercise of rights over the debt, and says the claim is premature because repayment is not due until 30 November 2021; a genuine loan would have been drawn as a loan letter stating interest, purpose and term and accompanied by a receipt, and on Articles 578 and 591 of the Civil Code nothing is payable yet; he asks that case files 1173 and 1174 be joined. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 31]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** claims partly allowed — Mr K is ordered to repay 53,782 USD (fifty-three thousand seven hundred and eighty-two) and 2,000,000 (two million) riel in damages; the claim for 5% annual interest is dismissed; provisional enforcement is refused; the defendant bears the costs; all other claims are dismissed. [Para 84][Para 85][Para 86][Para 87][Para 88]
The court first satisfied itself that the file was formally and legally regular and could be taken up under Articles 7, 8, 9 and 75 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 42][Para 43] On the contract it held the note sufficient: it identifies the lender and the borrower and records that Mr K received 53,782 USD, so the mutual consent required by Article 311 is made out and the instrument is a genuine loan contract. [Para 67][Para 68][Para 69] On prematurity it rejected the borrower's objection, finding that he borrowed to fund a planting business which did not prosper, leaving a high risk that he could never repay, so the lender was entitled to terminate and reclaim the principal without waiting for the contractual date. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73] On notice it found the 2 March 2018 letter received and signed for on 8 March 2018, with further notices afterwards, so the termination had taken effect. [Para 75][Para 76] On interest it found the contract stipulated none and that neither party had any intention or true will to pay interest at the time of contracting, and dismissed that head of claim. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 80] Damages were assessed on the facts, the law and the actual loss at 2,000,000 riel, and provisional enforcement was refused to avoid the complications of later restitution. [Para 82][Para 83][Para 85]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A written acknowledgement of debt that names the lender and the borrower, states the principal and the repayment period and bears both parties' signatures and thumbprints is a valid loan contract under Article 311 of the Civil Code, even if written in a foreign language and lacking the detail of a standard loan agreement; and where the business the loan funded has failed so that repayment is at high risk, the lender may terminate and sue for the principal before the contractual due date. [Para 69][Para 73]

**Obiter dicta:** that the two case files were related and triable together under Article 99 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and that provisional enforcement was refused for fear of restitution complications rather than on any ground of the merits. [Para 31][Para 83]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The principal is written in words as "fifty-three thousand seven hundred and eighty-seven" (ប៉ែតសិប ប្រាំពីរ) in the claim and the judgment's recital, against the figure 53,782 and the words "eighty-two" used elsewhere in the same document. [Para 8][Para 11] Party names survive only as initials — the plaintiff as "ឈ្មោះ ដ" and the defendant as "ក" — and are reported as OCR redactions, not names. [Para 4][Para 6] Passages of the parties' submissions are corrupted by stray Chinese characters (售, 甘, 包) and broken words, which obscure sentences in the account of the notices. [Para 18][Para 21][Para 49][Para 51]
