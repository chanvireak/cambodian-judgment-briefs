# Bank D v Ms C1, Mr C2 and Ms C3 (Civil Judgment No. 859 "ជ១", Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 12 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Ki Manera, Deputy President) · first instance · Doc ID 142328

## 1. Facts
On 16 November 2015 Ms C1 and Mr C2 borrowed 500,000 USD (fifty-ten thousand) from Bank D under a loan agreement dated 26 November 2015, at 8% a year, with a penalty of twice the loan rate on late payment, interest on arrears running from 15 days after the due date, and a 1% annual fee for the seven-year term. [Para 12] Two mortgage agreements of 17 November 2015 charged eight immovable properties, with Ms C3 as guarantor by property. [Para 13] The borrowers stopped paying from May 2017; by 31 August 2018 the bank's credit department statement put the debt at 492,245.56 USD, being 461,000 USD principal, 13,317.78 USD interest, 4,610.00 USD in fees and 13,317.78 USD in penalties. [Para 15][Para 19] The bank sued for the principal, 8% interest, the 1% fee, the 8% penalty and 100,000 USD in damages, and none of the three defendants appeared at any stage. [Para 6][Para 10][Para 30]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the defendants' default under the 26 November 2015 loan agreement entitles the bank to the principal with 8% annual interest, the 1% fee and the 8% penalty as stipulated? [Para 33][Para 43]
2. Whether Ms C3, who is not a named borrower but charged her property as guarantor, is jointly liable for the whole debt without the bank first pursuing Ms C1 and Mr C2? [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Bank D, creditor):** it stands by its claim of 26 October 2018; the agreement of 26 November 2015 records the 500,000 USD advance at 8% with the penalty and fee clauses, the borrowers paid only until May 2017 and then defaulted despite reminders, and the debt statement of 19 September 2018 proves the arrears. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 20]
**Defendants (Ms C1, Mr C2, Ms C3, borrowers and guarantor):** no submissions — summoned to each procedural step and signing for the summonses, they never appeared and gave no reason, so the court proceeded in their absence under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 26][Para 30]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed — Ms C1, Mr C2 and Ms C3 are ordered jointly to repay 461,000 USD (four hundred and sixty-one thousand) with 8% annual interest on that principal from 23 May 2017 and 1% annual fee and 8% annual penalty from the same date, all until full payment, plus 20,000 USD (twenty thousand) damages; costs fall on the defendants; points 1.1 to 1.3 are provisionally enforceable. [Para 49][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55]
Since the defendants never appeared, the court tried the case in their absence under Article 201 and reminded itself of the duty under Article 4 to conduct civil litigation fairly and speedily while parties act honestly. [Para 30][Para 31] On the agreement it found the signatures genuine and the 500,000 USD advance proved, so each party was bound to perform and the borrowers had not. [Para 32][Para 33] The mortgage agreements of 17 November 2015 showed the three defendants charged the properties, and though Ms C3 was not a party to the loan agreement, clause 2-1 of the mortgage contract guaranteed all obligations under it. [Para 34][Para 35] Applying Article 908(a), paragraph 1, of the Civil Code — a person jointly liable with the principal debtor may not require the creditor to proceed against that debtor first, nor escape liability by asserting the debtor's solvency — the court held Ms C3 jointly liable with the other two. [Para 36][Para 37] Default since May 2017 being documented, principal and 8% interest were allowed as claimed, and the 1% fee and 8% penalty as agreed in clauses 6.01 and 6.03. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 43][Para 44] The 100,000 USD damages claim was cut to 20,000 USD because no evidence fixed the extent of the loss, leaving the court to award a reasonable amount. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 53] Provisional enforcement was granted because the claim is a money claim over property. [Para 47][Para 55]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A person who guarantees a debt by charging immovable property is jointly liable with the principal debtors for the whole obligation, and under Article 908(a) paragraph 1 of the Civil Code may not insist that the creditor first sue the principal debtors or show they are unable to pay, even where she is not a named party to the loan agreement, the mortgage contract guaranteeing all obligations under it. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37] Where the amount of damages claimed is not proved, the court awards only such sum as the evidence shows to be reasonable rather than the sum pleaded. [Para 46][Para 53]

**Obiter dicta:** that a properly summoned defendant who signs for the summons and then stays away may be tried in absentia under Article 201, and that a money claim over property is of its nature fit for provisional enforcement. [Para 30][Para 47]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The 1% annual fee is claimed from 23 November 2018 in the prayers but awarded and reasoned from 23 May 2017. [Para 8][Para 22][Para 50][Para 51] The borrowing is dated 16 November 2015 in the summary but 23 November 2015 in the plaintiff's own statement, both under an agreement dated 26 November 2015. [Para 12][Para 18] The number of mortgaged properties is eight in the summary and nine in the mortgage reasoning. [Para 13][Para 19][Para 34] The 8% rate is glossed in words as "five three percent" in one place, and the debt statement's principal is written "four hundred and sixty thousand" against the figure 461,000. [Para 7][Para 12] Party names survive only as C1, C2 and C3 and the bank as "ធនាគារ ដ", and the operative paragraph is garbled where the defendants' names should appear. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 49]
