# Bank D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 888 "ឆ១", Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 22 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Horm Meng Se, Deputy President) · first instance · Doc ID 143248

## 1. Facts
On 21 December 2016 Mr C1 and his wife Ms C2 signed a loan contract with Bank D for 20,000 USD (twenty thousand) at 14.4% a year over 60 months, charging a 12.50 m by 20 m residential plot as security. [Para 9][Para 12][Para 21] They paid principal and interest for twelve months, amortising 3,091.25 USD of principal, then stopped: the bank pleads default on principal and interest from 5 December 2017 and penalty interest from 5 February 2017. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 22] Clause 2.3 of the contract let the bank enforce against the mortgaged property on any failure to pay to schedule. [Para 13] The bank sued for 16,908.75 USD principal, 14.4% interest from 5 December 2017, penalty interest at twice the contract rate from 5 February 2017, 4,000 USD in damages and costs; the defendants, served by newspaper publication, never appeared. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 8][Para 32]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the court may uphold the claim on the bank's documents alone where the defendants, duly summoned by public advertisement, failed to appear at any stage? [Para 33][Para 39][Para 40]
2. Whether contractual interest at 14.4% and penalty interest at twice that rate are recoverable on the outstanding principal from the dates pleaded, the borrowers having serviced the loan for a year? [Para 33][Para 45][Para 46]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Bank D, creditor):** it maintains its claim of 18 January 2019 and the preparatory note of 22 April 2019; the contract, the 8 January 2019 amortisation schedule and the mortgage undertaking of 21 December 2016 prove the advance, the terms and the default, and clause 2.3 entitles it to enforce and to ask for provisional execution. [Para 14][Para 26][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** no submissions — they were summoned by publication in the Rasmei Kampuchea newspaper and by posting at the commune office and did not attend at any stage. [Para 8][Para 32][Para 38]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed — the defendants must repay 16,908.75 USD (sixteen thousand nine hundred and eight dollars and seventy-five cents) with 14.4% annual interest on that sum from 5 December 2017 and penalty interest at twice the contract rate from 5 February 2017, both until full payment, plus 4,000,000 riel (four million riel) for loss of time and mental distress; the remaining claims are dismissed, the principal is provisionally enforceable, and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50]
The court noted that service had been effected in the manner prescribed for absent parties, that the defendants had nonetheless stayed away, and that it could therefore hear the case in their absence. [Para 8][Para 39] It found the bank's submissions appropriate and its documents — the contract, the amortisation schedule and the mortgage undertaking — clear, consistent and credible, and so decided the claim in accordance with them. [Para 36][Para 40] It scaled the damages claim to a moderate sum on the evidence rather than the 4,000 USD pleaded. [Para 37][Para 47] Finding the case uncomplicated, it sat as a single judge. [Para 41] Costs followed the event. [Para 42][Para 50]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A defendant who has been summoned by the substituted service the law allows — publication in a newspaper and posting at the commune office — and who fails to appear at any stage is treated as offering no defence, and the court may decide the claim on the plaintiff's documents alone where those documents are clear, mutually consistent and credible; interest and penalty clauses in the loan contract are then enforced for the periods claimed. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 45][Para 46]

**Obiter dicta:** that an uncomplicated claim may be tried by a single judge, and that a claim for damages unsupported by proof of its extent is cut down to a moderate sum. [Para 37][Para 41]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The principal is written as 16,908.75 USD but glossed in words as "sixteen thousand nine hundred and eight dollars and seventy-five cents" throughout, so figure and words disagree by ten cents. [Para 1][Para 44] Penalty interest is claimed and awarded from 5 February 2017 although default on principal and interest is pleaded only from 5 December 2017 and twelve months of instalments were paid. [Para 11][Para 46] Damages of 4,000 USD are claimed but 4,000,000 riel awarded on the same numerals, with no exchange rate stated. [Para 18][Para 47] Party names survive only as C1 and C2 and the bank as "ធនាគារ ដ", and the OCR repeatedly interrupts the text with page markers and the "not a model judgment" rubric, splitting sentences mid-clause. [Para 7][Para 38][Para 52]
