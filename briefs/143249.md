# Mr D v Mr C (Civil Judgment No. 820 "ជ១", Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 1 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Ki Manera, Deputy President) · first instance · Doc ID 143249

## 1. Facts
On 3 June 2016 Mr C borrowed 2,000 USD (two thousand) from Mr D under a written loan contract, promising to repay by 3 September 2016 with interest at 3% a month. [Para 9][Para 14] Mr C paid three months' interest, 180 USD in all, then missed the repayment date; afterwards he paid 500 USD and then 300 USD, leaving 1,200 USD of principal outstanding, and then avoided the creditor. [Para 9][Para 15] Mr D sued on 24 January 2017 for the 1,200 USD, interest at 5% a month from September 2016, 2,000,000 riel in damages and costs. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 12]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the borrower's assertion that he repaid 1,000 USD through a third party and a further 800 USD is proved, where the only witness he named did not attend despite being summoned? [Para 16][Para 17]
2. Whether the contractual rate of 5% a month is enforceable, given the ceiling fixed by Prakas No. 128 ក.យ.ប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011? [Para 21][Para 26]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr D, lender):** he stands by the plaint — 2,000 USD was lent on a proper contract repayable on 3 September 2016, only 800 USD came back in two instalments of 500 and 300, and the balance of 1,200 USD with 5% monthly interest and 2,000,000 riel in damages is due. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 18][Para 15]
**Defendant (Mr C, borrower):** he admits borrowing the 2,000 USD and paying interest duly, but says he repaid 1,000 USD at the end of 2016 through Mr S C, a friend's elder brother, and then 800 USD more, leaving only 200 USD, and offers 200 USD principal with 100 USD interest — 300 USD in all — asking that Mr S C be called as his witness. [Para 10][Para 16]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is partly allowed — Mr C must repay 1,200 USD (one thousand two hundred) principal, with interest at 1.5% a month from September 2016 until full payment, and 1,000,000 (one million) riel in damages; the claim for 5% monthly interest is dismissed and replaced by that lawful rate; costs fall on the defendant. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]
The court first held the plaint of 24 January 2017 formally proper and within its jurisdiction under Article 7 of the Code of Civil Procedure, reminding itself of the Article 4 duty of fair and speedy process. [Para 12][Para 13] On the contract it found the instrument of 3 June 2016 genuine and the borrowing admitted by the defendant himself. [Para 14] On the repayments it set the plaintiff's account — 800 USD paid, 1,200 USD remaining — against the defendant's claim of 1,800 USD paid; since the defendant had asked for Mr S C to be called, the court issued and served a summons, but the witness never appeared, so there was no evidence on which to accept the alleged payments. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17] The outstanding principal was therefore 1,200 USD. [Para 20] On interest, the court held the 5% monthly rate inconsistent with the lawful rate under Prakas No. 128 ក.យ.ប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011, which caps interest at 18% a year, and substituted 1.5% a month from September 2016. [Para 21][Para 26] The 2,000,000 riel damages claim was reduced to a reasonable 1,000,000 riel on the facts. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 27]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A stipulation for interest above the ceiling of 18% a year fixed by Prakas No. 128 ក.យ.ប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011 is not void in whole: the court strikes down the excessive rate and awards interest at the lawful maximum — 1.5% a month — running from the date pleaded. [Para 21][Para 26] A borrower who asserts repayment must prove it, and where the sole witness he names fails to attend despite a summons served at his residence, the assertion stands unproven against the creditor's account. [Para 17]

**Obiter dicta:** that a claim for a money sum is a claim of right over property, and that damages pleaded at a round figure will be scaled to what the facts make reasonable. [Para 20][Para 23]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The plaintiff's account makes the borrower's 180 USD of interest payments, but the sentence attributes the payment to the plaintiff himself ("ដើមចោទ"), and the opening summary of the loan is garbled so that the words "two" and the date of 3 June 2016 run together. [Para 7][Para 9] The prayer claims 5% "from September 2016" without stating a period, while the reasoning treats it as 5% a month. [Para 4][Para 21] The two accounts of repayment cannot be reconciled — 500 + 300 USD against 1,000 + 800 USD — and the judgment resolves it only by the missing witness, without saying whose figures it accepts. [Para 15][Para 16] The witness is named variously "សច" and "ស ច", the parties survive as "ដ" and "ច", and page markers and the "not a model judgment" rubric interrupt paragraphs mid-sentence. [Para 8][Para 10][Para 17][Para 30]
