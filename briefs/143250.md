# Institution D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 822 "ជ", Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 1 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Phi Manera, Deputy President) · first instance · Doc ID 143250

## 1. Facts
On 11 August 2017 Mr C1 and Ms C2 borrowed 150,000 USD (one hundred fifty thousand) from Institution D for 120 months at 16% a year, with penalty interest of 24% a year on arrears, and on the same day signed a hypothec over a plot held under a certificate issued on 6 September 2016. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 19] They paid nothing to schedule, so that 149,553.97 USD of principal stood outstanding by the time of suit, and default in principal, interest and penalty is pleaded from 12 September 2017. [Para 10][Para 11] The institution sued on 1 November 2017 for that principal, 16% interest and 24% penalty, 8,500 USD in attorney fees, 5,000,000 riel in damages, provisional execution and costs. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 27]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the absent defendants are liable for the outstanding principal with the contractual interest of 16% and penalty of 24% running from 12 September 2017? [Para 21][Para 28][Para 35]
2. Whether the institution must prove the extent of its claimed attorney fees and lost profit, or may recover them at the sums pleaded? [Para 29][Para 30]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Institution D, creditor):** it stands by the plaint of 1 November 2017; the contract, the hypothec of the same date, the ownership certificate and the payment schedule prove the advance and the terms, the borrowers took the money and then paid nothing, and clause 1.3 and clause 2.1 entitle it to the principal, interest, penalty, fees and damages claimed. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 17][Para 28]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** no submissions — they were summoned at their place of residence and by public advertisement and did not appear at any procedural step. [Para 16][Para 21]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is substantially allowed — the defendants must pay 149,553.97 USD (one hundred forty-nine thousand five hundred and fifty-three and ninety-seven cents) principal, with 16% annual interest and 24% annual penalty from 12 September 2017 until full payment, plus 4,000,000 (four million) riel in damages; the principal alone is provisionally enforceable; costs fall on the defendants. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38]
The court first recorded that service had been attempted at the defendants' residence and by public notice and that they still did not appear, so it could proceed in their absence under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure, subject to the Article 4 duty of fairness and speed. [Para 21][Para 22] On the contract it found the borrowers had genuinely contracted with the institution and received the money. [Para 23][Para 26] Since each party must perform the agreed terms and the borrowers had not, their failure to pay the instalments was a serious breach. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26] The principal, 16% interest and 24% penalty were therefore allowed as claimed, the evidence being clear and the defendants having failed their obligations under clauses 1.3 and 2.1. [Para 27][Para 28] The 8,500 USD fees and 5,000,000 riel damages were cut to 4,000,000 riel because no evidence fixed the extent of the loss, leaving the court to award a reasonable sum on the facts. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 36] Provisional execution was confined to the principal. [Para 32][Para 37]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan contract and its hypothec are proved and the borrowers default, the creditor recovers the whole outstanding principal with contractual interest and penalty interest running from the date of default as pleaded; but a claim for attorney fees and lost profit, being unliquidated, is not recoverable at the sum pleaded and is reduced to a reasonable amount on the evidence. [Para 28][Para 30][Para 35]

**Obiter dicta:** that a claim of right over a debt of a fixed sum is of its nature fit for provisional execution, though the court confined that relief to the principal and not to the running interest. [Para 32][Para 37]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The 16% rate is glossed in words as "fifteen-one percent" (ដប់ ប្រាំ មួយ ភាគរយ) at every appearance, against the figure 16%. [Para 4][Para 12][Para 35] The 8,500 USD attorney fees claimed are never dealt with in the operative order, which awards only 4,000,000 riel against the 5,000,000 riel sought, so it cannot be told whether the fees were refused or merged into the damages. [Para 5][Para 29][Para 36] The institution, the borrowers and the hypothec certificate are reduced to initials and stray digits — "គ្រឹះស្ថាន មី ដ", "ឈ្មោះ ៧", "លេខ!" — and the names of the parties in the evidence list are garbled beyond reconstruction. [Para 17][Para 23][Para 24] The copy stamp is dated 9 August 2019, five weeks after the judgment it certifies. [Para 40]
