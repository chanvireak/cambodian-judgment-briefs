# Mr D v Mr C (Civil Judgment No. 828 "ម", Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 4 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Ouy Sao, Deputy President) · first instance · Doc ID 143251

## 1. Facts
On 3 December 2015 Mr C signed a loan contract with Mr D, a Japanese national living in Japan, for 53,782 USD (fifty-three thousand seven hundred and eighty-two), repayable by 30 November 2021, the money taken as capital for a beekeeping business. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 65] The venture did not prosper and communication failed, so on 2 March 2018 Mr D served written notice terminating the contract and demanding repayment in full. [Para 14][Para 15] Mr C signed for the notice on 8 March 2018 and refused the follow-up notices. [Para 35] Mr D sued for the principal, 5% annual interest from 2 March 2018 and 5,000 USD in legal service damages. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 10]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the "I OWE YOU NOTE" of 3 December 2015 is a valid and enforceable loan contract although made in a foreign language and lacking the detail of an ordinary loan? [Para 33][Para 37][Para 49]
2. Whether the lender could terminate and reclaim the principal before 30 November 2021, and whether the termination notice reached the borrower? [Para 64][Para 67][Para 69]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr D, lender):** the denial of the contract contradicts the facts — the note states the principal and repayment date, bears both parties' signatures and thumbprints, and satisfies Article 311, so the consent is genuine and there is no forgery; the borrower signed for the termination notice on 8 March 2018, knew of the later notices, and is merely evading his duty. [Para 30][Para 32][Para 35][Para 36]
**Defendant (Mr C, borrower):** he does not admit the loan contract, the exercise of rights over the debt, or any knowledge of the termination notice or the follow-up notices; the claim is premature because repayment is not due until 30 November 2021, and under Articles 578 and 591 of the Civil Code nothing is payable yet — a real loan would have been drawn as a loan letter stating interest, purpose and term with a receipt. [Para 24][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28][Para 29]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** claims partly allowed — Mr C must repay 53,782 USD (fifty-three thousand seven hundred and eighty-two) and 2,000,000 (two million) riel in damages; the claim for 5% annual interest is dismissed; provisional execution is refused; all other claims are dismissed and the defendant bears the costs. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 80][Para 81][Para 82][Para 83]
The court first held the file formally and legally regular and triable under Articles 7, 8, 9 and 75 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 37][Para 38] On the contract it found the note sufficient: it identifies lender and borrower and records receipt of 53,782 USD, so the mutual consent required by Article 311 is established and the instrument is free of forgery. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63] On prematurity it held the objection baseless: the money was borrowed for a beekeeping business which did not prosper and left a high risk that the borrower could never repay, so the lender was entitled to terminate and reclaim without waiting for the contractual date. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67] On notice it found the 2 March 2018 letter received and signed for on 8 March 2018, with further notices after that, so the termination had taken effect. [Para 69][Para 70] On interest it found the contract stipulated none and that neither party had any intention or true will to pay interest, and dismissed that head. [Para 72][Para 73][Para 74] The 5,000 USD damages claim was assessed on the facts and the actual loss at 2,000,000 riel, and provisional execution was refused to avoid later restitution complications. [Para 76][Para 77][Para 80]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A written acknowledgement of debt naming the lender and borrower, stating the principal and repayment period and bearing both signatures and thumbprints is a valid contract under Article 311 of the Civil Code despite being in a foreign language and less detailed than a standard loan; and where the funded business has failed so that repayment is at high risk, the lender may terminate and sue for the principal before the due date, but interest claimed at 5% a year cannot be added where the contract stipulated none. [Para 63][Para 67][Para 72][Para 74]

**Obiter dicta:** that provisional execution is refused where granting it might create complications of restitution later, not because the claim itself is doubtful. [Para 77]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The summary of the transaction in the opening section is badly broken, so that purpose, the parties' cooperation failure and the "not a model judgment" rubric run into one another and the sentence naming the borrower is missing entirely. [Para 13][Para 14] In the money-lending reasoning the borrower is named "C" throughout, but the operative paragraph garbles the party line as "ឈ្មោះ ចោទ ឈ្មោះ ច" and "ដើម ដ .". [Para 78][Para 80] The plaintiff's own submissions repeat the loan date and the amount twice inside one sentence, mixing "with Mr D" and "with him", and the notice paragraph inserts a stray numeral "7" before the borrower's name. [Para 21][Para 35] Party names survive only as initials, and the plaintiff is "ឈ្មោះ ដ", the defendant "ច", with the judge's name rendered as both "អូយ សៅ" and "ក្មួយ សៅ" in the same document. [Para 4][Para 6][Para 85]
