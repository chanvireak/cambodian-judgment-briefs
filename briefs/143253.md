# Bank D v Mr C1, Ms C2 and Mr C3 (Civil Judgment No. 943(ខ), Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 6 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Ros Sameidy) · first instance · Doc ID 143253

## 1. Facts
On 29 March 2018 Bank D's Phnom Penh branch signed a long-term credit agreement, dated 30 March 2018, lending the defendants 450,000 USD (four hundred fifty thousand) over 120 months at 8.9% a year with damages of 25% a year. [Para 9][Para 11][Para 19] To secure it the defendants charged immovable property and attached movables by a hypothec agreement of the same date, registered with the municipal land management department. [Para 20][Para 25][Para 26] They stopped paying on 5 October 2018, leaving 436,456.49 USD of principal, and the bank sued in case file 404 of 5 March 2019 for that sum, 131,942.41 USD in interest and penalty to 22 January 2019, overdue interest at 13.35% a year thereafter, 50,000 USD in damages, a declaration of its hypothec, provisional execution and costs. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 10][Para 12]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the bank's hypothec over the defendants' immovables is valid and enforceable, having been created by written agreement and registered with the land department under Article 844 of the Civil Code? [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27]
2. Whether the bank may recover the interest, penalty and damages claimed, the defendants admitting the loan but disputing the balance and the incidental sums? [Para 13][Para 14][Para 23]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Bank D, creditor):** the credit agreement of 30 March 2018 and the hypothec of the same date are proved; the defendants drew the 450,000 USD, defaulted from 5 October 2018, and seriously prejudiced the bank's interests and solvency; it claims the outstanding principal, interest and penalty at 8.9% and 13.35%, 50,000 USD in damages for lost time, a declaration of its hypothec right and provisional execution. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 23][Para 24][Para 27]
**Defendants (Mr C1, Ms C2 and Mr C3, borrowers and mortgagors):** they admit signing the 450,000 USD loan but say they cannot confirm the 436,456.49 USD claimed and will supply documents and a statement to the court; they dispute the interest, the penalty, the damages and provisional execution, and ask that the other claims be dismissed. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 18]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed — the three defendants must jointly pay 436,456.49 USD (four hundred thirty-six thousand four hundred fifty-six dollars and fifty-nine cents) in principal, 131,972.41 USD in interest and penalty to 22 January 2019, overdue interest at 13.35% a year from 23 January 2019 and 40,000,000 (forty million) riel in damages; the bank's hypothec over the charged property is declared to exist; point 1(a) is provisionally enforceable; the remaining claims are dismissed and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35]
The court found the credit agreement and the hypothec both dated 30 March 2018 proved, the 450,000 USD drawn and the hypothec properly created under Article 844 of the Civil Code and registered with the land department, so the bank's right over the charged property existed and secured the debt. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] The defendants' failure to pay principal and interest to schedule was a serious breach. [Para 22] On the disputed balance, their plea that they could not confirm the figure, unaccompanied by any document, did not displace the bank's records. [Para 18] Interest and penalty were allowed at the contractual 8.9% with 13.35% overdue interest, computed to 22 January 2019. [Para 23] The 50,000 USD damages claim was held proper in principle because the bank had lost time in the proceedings, but was scaled to 40,000,000 riel. [Para 24][Para 33] Provisional execution was confined to the principal, and costs followed the event under Article 64 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 34]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A hypothec created by written agreement between creditor and debtor, and registered with the competent land authority, is valid and enforceable under Article 844 of the Civil Code; the court will declare the mortgagee's right over the charged property to exist and to secure the debt, and the charge survives the borrowers' default as the creditor's lawful security. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] A borrower who admits the loan but says he cannot confirm the outstanding balance, without producing any document, does not raise a triable dispute against the creditor's records. [Para 18]

**Obiter dicta:** that loss of time in litigation may justify damages in principle, though the sum pleaded will be reduced to a reasonable figure, and that provisional execution is confined to the fixed principal and not extended to interest. [Para 24][Para 28][Para 34]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The interest and penalty figure is 131,942.41 USD in the claim but 131,972.41 USD in the operative order, a difference of thirty dollars left unexplained. [Para 3][Para 31] The principal appears as 436,456.49 USD and once as 436,456.48 USD. [Para 2][Para 18] The damages award is written "40,000,000$ (forty million riel)", mixing the dollar sign with a riel sum, and the loan itself is written "450,000 ៛ (four hundred fifty thousand US dollars)". [Para 19][Para 33] The defendants agree on the principal in the list of undisputed points yet dispute the very same figure in their statement. [Para 13][Para 14] The parties are reduced to initials and the wife's and son's names are omitted altogether, the bench line is corrupted by stray Latin and Thai characters ("អបInហារ ៦៦. สวนบทเบาเบา ๆ"), and the judge's name is printed "រស់ សាម៉ឺឌី", "រស់ សាម៉ី ឌី" and "រស់ សាម៉ឺឌី" in successive lines. [Para 1][Para 37][Para 38]
