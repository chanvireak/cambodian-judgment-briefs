# Mr D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 1126 "S", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 9 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Theam Chan Piseth) · first instance · Doc ID 143260

## 1. Facts
On 8 May 2008 Mr C1 and his wife Ms C2 signed a mortgage contract over a plot held under a certificate of possession dated 10 July 2007 and borrowed 170,000 dollars from Mr D, promising interest at 3% a month. [Para 10][Para 18] Four further loans followed on receipts: 30,000 dollars on 3 June 2008, 100,000 dollars on 8 February 2009, and 27,000 dollars on each of 8 May 2009 and 8 August 2009 — 354,000 dollars in all, on which no interest was ever paid. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 12][Para 13][Para 19] Mr D sued for the principal, statutory interest from May 2009 to the date of filing, 50,000 dollars in damages, costs and provisional enforcement. [Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 9]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a court may award interest at the contractual rate where the contract stipulated 3% a month, or must it apply the ceiling fixed by Prakas No. 128 of 21 December 2001? [Para 18][Para 21][Para 26]
2. Whether a lender who recovers principal and statutory interest may also recover 50,000 dollars for distress, lost time and legal service? [Para 7][Para 22]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr D, lender):** the mortgage contract of 8 May 2008 and the four receipts prove 354,000 dollars lent, with 3% monthly interest promised; nothing was ever paid in interest, so he claims the principal, statutory interest from May 2009 to filing, and 50,000 dollars for distress, lost time and legal service. [Para 14]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** they admit owing the 354,000 dollars and ask for one further year to arrange repayment, while asking the court to dismiss every other head of the claim. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the defendants are ordered to pay 354,000 dollars (three hundred and fifty-four thousand dollars) principal, with interest at 18% a year on that sum from 8 May 2009 to 2 March 2019; the claim for 50,000 dollars in distress, lost time and legal service is dismissed; costs fall on the defendants, and head 1 of the order is provisionally enforceable. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28][Para 29]
The court found from the mortgage contract and the receipt of 8 May 2008 that the defendants had borrowed 170,000 dollars against the property, promising 3% a month, and from the receipts of 3 June 2008, 8 February 2009, 8 May 2009 and 8 August 2009 that they had received a further 184,000 dollars. [Para 18][Para 19] The claim for 354,000 dollars therefore rested on sufficient evidence, and the defendants admitted it. [Para 20] On interest, the court held that a claim for statutory interest conformed to a contract that attached interest, and that the rate was to be fixed by reference to the content of the Prakas on interest rates, capped by Prakas No. 128 K.Y.Prak/11 of 21 December 2001 of the Ministry of Justice. [Para 21] The order then set that rate at 18% a year on the principal from 8 May 2009 to 2 March 2019. [Para 26] The 50,000 dollar claim for distress, lost time and legal service had no basis on which the court could grant it. [Para 22] Provisional enforcement was ordered under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and costs under Article 64. [Para 23][Para 24]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan contract stipulates monthly interest and the lender claims interest "at the statutory rate", the court does not enforce the contractual rate but applies the maximum permitted by Prakas No. 128 of 21 December 2001 — 18% a year on the principal — running from the date of lending to a fixed date; and a general damages claim pleaded on top of principal and interest, without any separate basis, is refused. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 26]

**Obiter dicta:** that a borrower's bare request for further time to pay does not affect the award where the debt is admitted. [Para 15]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The reasoning nowhere states the rate it applies; the 18% figure appears for the first time in the operative order. [Para 21][Para 26] Interest is claimed "until the date of filing" but awarded only to 2 March 2019, two months before the claim was lodged on 9 May 2019, and the judgment does not explain the cut-off. [Para 6][Para 26] The Prakas is cited as "128 K.Y.Prak/11" and the Ministry's name is OCR'd as "ក្រសួង យុត៌្តធម"; a stray party initial is spliced into the dollar total in the receipts paragraph. [Para 19][Para 21] The date of the certificate of possession (10 July 2007) is split across two paragraphs. [Para 18][Para 19] Parties survive only as initials (Mr D, Mr C1, Ms C2); these are reported as redactions, not names.
