# Bank (name redacted) v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 852 K1, Phnom Penh First Instance Court, 10 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Ouk Sovannarith) · first instance · Doc ID 143262

## 1. Facts
On 06 January 2016 the plaintiff bank lent Mr C1 and Ms C2 USD 70,000 (seventy thousand US dollars) under a written credit contract at 1.1% per month, repayable in 60 instalments running from 06 January 2016 to 15 January 2021, with a 24% per annum penalty rate. [Para 15] The borrowers paid principal and interest for 24 months only and stopped on 15 March 2018; a final demand letter dated 15 June 2018 did not produce payment, and the bank sued on 09 April 2019. [Para 16][Para 43][Para 18]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a defendant who fails to appear at the oral argument without valid reason is deemed to have admitted the plaintiff's statement under Article 201(a) paragraph 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure? [Para 47][Para 57]
2. Whether a bank may recover the contractual penalty of 24% per annum in addition to 1.1% monthly interest, and whether an unproven claim for moral damages must be refused? [Para 55][Para 65]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the bank, creditor):** it maintained its plaint of 09 April 2019, amending only item 3 from USD 600.94 to USD 4,769.51 by request of 07 May 2019; the borrowers had signed and thumbprinted the contract and had acknowledged the debt in their own final demand letter. [Para 19][Para 50][Para 51]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** they did not appear; the summons was served through the court process server, who left a copy at their address, and they filed no documentary evidence and called no witnesses. [Para 31][Para 41][Para 44]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is accepted as lawful; the two defendants are ordered jointly to pay USD 40,850 (forty thousand eight hundred and fifty US dollars) principal, USD 4,879.69 (four thousand eight hundred and fifty-nine and sixty-nine cents US dollars) interest and USD 4,769.51 (four thousand seven hundred and sixty-nine and fifty-one cents US dollars) penalty for the period 15 March 2018 to 15 March 2019, plus 1.1% monthly interest and 24% annual penalty from 15 March 2019 until payment in full; the moral damages claim is dismissed and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 64][Para 65][Para 67]
The court first held the plaint procedurally regular, then recorded that the defendants were absent without valid reason and that the minutes of the hearing had been served on them. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48] On the documents it found the loan contract free of any defect in the expression of consent, bearing the thumbprints of both borrowers and of two witnesses, and found the final demand letter to be an acknowledgment of the debt owed to the bank; the authorised counsel's submissions were consistent with both documents. [Para 49][Para 51][Para 50][Para 52] Principal and accrued interest were therefore awarded, the continuing 1.1% monthly interest was granted under Articles 584 and 585 of the Civil Code, and the 24% penalty was allowed on the unpaid sums pro rata to the days of default. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55] Provisional enforcement was ordered under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and costs were charged to the defendants under Article 64. [Para 56][Para 58]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a borrower has signed and thumbprinted a loan contract and later acknowledged the debt in a written final demand, and then defaults and fails without valid reason to appear at the hearing, the court may treat the plaintiff's statement as admitted under Article 201(a) paragraph 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure and award principal, accrued interest and the contractual penalty; a claim for moral damages that is pleaded but not evidenced is nonetheless refused. [Para 57][Para 50][Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that provisional enforcement rests on Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure and that costs follow the unsuccessful party under Article 64. [Para 56][Para 58]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The plaint claims interest and penalty "up to 15 March 2019" without a start date, while the operative order computes both for the year 15 March 2018 to 15 March 2019. [Para 8][Para 61] The written-out words for USD 4,879.69 read "fifty-four cents" at one point and "fifty-nine cents" elsewhere. [Para 34][Para 61] Item 3 of the plaint was originally pleaded at USD 600.94 and amended to USD 4,769.51, so the two figures both appear in the record. [Para 9][Para 19] The loan term is stated as 60 months from 06 January 2016 yet ends 15 January 2021. [Para 15] Party names survive only as the redacted bank name, the digits "ច១"/"ច២" and an empty ID-card number; they are reported as OCR losses, not expanded. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 60]
