# Mr D v Mr C (Civil Judgment No. 991 N, Phnom Penh First Instance Court, 19 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Yich Cheana Vi) · first instance · Doc ID 143263

## 1. Facts
On 29 August 2014 Mr C borrowed USD 3,000 (three thousand US dollars) from Mr D at 2.5% per month for a three-month term, and never paid either principal or interest. [Para 11][Para 12] On 26 July 2016 the parties signed a debt-repayment agreement rescheduling the sum over 27 months, payable on the 10th of each month, which Mr C also failed to honour. [Para 15][Para 16] Mr D sued on 20 March 2019 for the principal, 2.5% monthly interest from 29 August 2014, and USD 3,000 in damages and lawyer's fees. [Para 6][Para 7][Para 8]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a contractual claim for 2.5% monthly interest has a legal basis when it exceeds the interest rate capped by Prakas No. 128 on capped interest rates? [Para 21][Para 26]
2. Whether a claim for damages and lawyer's fees of USD 3,000 is justified, and in what amount? [Para 21][Para 28]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr D, creditor):** the loan contract of 29 August 2014 provided 2.5% per month over three months; nothing was ever repaid, so he claimed the principal, interest running from the date of the loan, and USD 3,000 for damage and counsel. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 17]
**Defendant (Mr C, debtor):** he admitted the USD 3,000 principal and offered to repay USD 50 a month until paid in full and to pay interest at 18% per year from 29 August 2014, while asking the court to dismiss the claim for moral damages and the USD 3,000 of lawyer's fees. [Para 18][Para 19]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** Mr C is ordered to pay Mr D USD 3,000 (three thousand US dollars) principal, interest at 18% per year on that principal from 29 August 2014 until payment in full, and 1,000,000 (one million) riel in damages and lawyer's fees; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal and costs fall on the defendant. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38]
The court found both counsel's statements consistent with the two documents on file — the loan contract of 29 August 2014 and the repayment agreement — and held that the latter confirmed the defendant truly owed the USD 3,000, a point he conceded, leaving no dispute as to the principal. [Para 25][Para 20] On interest it held that 2.5% per month was higher than the rate capped by Prakas No. 128 K.Y.Prak/11 of 21 December 2011, which under Article 17 of the Law on the Implementation of the Civil Code fixes the maximum at 18% per year, and it therefore applied the lawful rate from 29 August 2014 rather than the contractual one. [Para 26][Para 27] On damages it awarded only a reasonable sum, relying on Articles 398(a) paragraph 1 and 401(a) paragraph 1 of the Civil Code for the failure to perform an obligation. [Para 28] Provisional enforcement was granted under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure because the defendant had acknowledged the claim, and costs were charged to him under Article 64. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A contractual rate of interest above the 18% per annum ceiling fixed by Prakas No. 128 is not enforceable; the court substitutes the capped rate and runs it from the date of the loan, while a claim for damages and lawyer's fees is compensatory only and is reduced to the sum the court considers reasonable rather than the amount pleaded. [Para 26][Para 35][Para 28]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that provisional enforcement may be ordered where the defendant has acknowledged the claim, under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 31][Para 32]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The repayment agreement is dated 26 July 2016 in the narrative and in the court's reasoning but 26 October 2016 in the list of exhibits. [Para 15][Para 22][Para 25] The reasoning twice states that interest runs from 29 August 2019, whereas the facts, the plaint and the operative order all give 29 August 2014. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 35] The judgment switches between "co-plaintiff" and "co-defendant" although one party sits on each side. [Para 18][Para 31] Party names appear only as the initials "ដ" and "ច", with street numbers and ID numbers left blank; they are reported as OCR losses, not expanded. [Para 2][Para 4]
