# Microfinance institution (name redacted) v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 1015-5, Phnom Penh First Instance Court, 23 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Hong Sokun Vathana) · first instance · Doc ID 143264

## 1. Facts
On 31 October 2017 Mr C1 and his wife Ms C2 borrowed USD 23,800 (twenty-three thousand eight hundred US dollars) from the plaintiff microfinance institution under an interest-bearing loan contract at 1.30% per month — USD 309 — over a 60-month term. [Para 13][Para 17][Para 18] They paid interest for two months only, USD 500 in all, and then stopped paying principal and interest entirely, leaving USD 23,800 outstanding. [Para 14][Para 19][Para 15] The institution sued for the principal, 1.30% monthly interest and a USD 2 per day penalty both running from 06 April 2018, plus 4,000,000 riel for loss of time and moral injury. [Para 7][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a debt acknowledged by the borrowers in the preparatory procedure and matching their signed loan contract entitles the lender to principal, contractual interest and the daily penalty as pleaded? [Para 36][Para 38]
2. Whether damages for loss of time and moral injury may be awarded at the sum claimed when no evidence establishes the extent of the damage? [Para 39][Para 40]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the microfinance institution, lender):** the contract of 31 October 2017 stands at USD 23,800 with 1.30% monthly interest and a USD 2 daily penalty; only USD 500 of interest was ever received, and it claimed the full contractual relief plus 4,000,000 riel in damages and provisional enforcement. [Para 17][Para 19][Para 22][Para 26]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** Mr C1 appeared and admitted borrowing the USD 23,800 and paying only two months of interest; he and his wife acknowledged the debt in full but said they had no means to pay, asked to repay the principal only, and asked the court to drop the interest, penalty and damages claims. [Para 29][Para 31][Para 32][Para 34] Ms C2 was absent but had been notified and asked her husband to attend in her place. [Para 28]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is accepted; Mr C1 and Ms C2 are ordered jointly to pay the institution USD 23,800 (twenty-three thousand eight hundred US dollars) principal, interest at 1.30% per month on that principal from 06 April 2018 until payment in full, a penalty of USD 2 (two US dollars) per day from the same date, and 2,000,000 (two million) riel in damages for loss of time and moral injury; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52]
The court found the parties' statements consistent with the loan contract of 31 October 2017 and with the documentary evidence, so that the borrowing was proved, and it noted that the defendants had themselves acknowledged the claim, which removed any dispute of fact. [Para 36][Para 38][Para 35] It therefore granted the principal, the contractual monthly interest and the daily penalty exactly as pleaded. [Para 37][Para 47] On the damages claim it observed that the 4,000,000 riel sought was supported by no evidence establishing the size of the loss, and reduced the award accordingly. [Para 39][Para 40] Provisional enforcement of the principal was ordered under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure on the strength of the defendants' acknowledgment, and costs were charged to the losing party. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the borrowers admit the loan and the debt and their admissions match the signed contract, the court awards principal, contractual interest and the contractual daily penalty as pleaded; damages for loss of time and moral injury, by contrast, are not at the lender's discretion and are cut down where no evidence establishes the extent of the loss. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that an acknowledgment of the principal debt is what justifies provisional enforcement of that principal under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 43][Para 44]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The reasoning states that the 4,000,000 riel damages claim is supported by no evidence of the extent of the damage, yet 2,000,000 riel is nonetheless awarded in the operative order. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 50] The defendants are said to have paid two months of interest, but the amount is given as USD 500 although two instalments at USD 309 would be USD 618. [Para 17][Para 19] The caption is badly garbled — case number, date and party descriptions run together, and the judgment number appears only as "1015 \"5\"". [Para 1][Para 5][Para 6] The plaintiff is called a microfinance institution in the caption and a bank in the party statements, and its name and the representative's name survive only as "ដ" and "ត ដ". [Para 6][Para 17]
