# Company (name redacted) v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 911 K, Phnom Penh First Instance Court, 30 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Say Sarun) · first instance · Doc ID 143265

## 1. Facts
Between 30 September 2015 and 30 December 2018 the plaintiff company and Mr C1 and his wife Ms C2 entered into ten successive loan contracts totalling USD 56,050 (fifty-six thousand and fifty US dollars) at 1.8% per month: six of USD 5,000, then USD 3,250, USD 6,100, USD 8,000 and USD 8,700. [Para 37][Para 42][Para 24] The first loan was secured on 30 September 2015 by a pledge of a transfer-of-possession letter over land and a house dated 11 October 2012, measuring 66.40 m, registered at the commune office. [Para 14][Para 50] The borrowers repaid neither principal nor interest after 01 December 2018. [Para 25]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the parties in fact concluded loan contracts totalling USD 56,050, when the defendants admit only USD 27,000? [Para 42][Para 44]
2. Whether the lender deducted 7% of the loan and one month's interest in advance, and whether the defendants paid eight months of interest at USD 486 a month? [Para 42][Para 45]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the company, lender):** ten contracts were signed in succession from 30 September 2015 to 30 December 2018 and together total USD 56,050 at 1.8% per month; it denied outright any 7% deduction or advance interest and any receipt of USD 486 monthly, and claimed USD 5,000 in damages and moral injury plus forced provisional enforcement. [Para 42][Para 45][Para 7][Para 8]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** they admitted only four contracts and a debt of USD 27,000; they asserted the lender had deducted 7% and one month's interest up front, and that they had paid eight months of interest at USD 486, producing three payment receipts in support. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 46]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the defendants are ordered jointly to pay USD 56,050 (fifty-six thousand and fifty US dollars) principal with interest at 1.8% per month from 03 December 2018 until the principal is repaid, and 2,000,000 (two million) riel in moral damages; all claims beyond those two heads are dismissed, costs fall on the defendants, and the judgment is provisionally enforceable only as to points 1 and 4. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59]
On the first issue the court held that the successive loan contracts, taken with the parties' statements, established that the defendants had borrowed the full USD 56,050, and it recognised the plaintiff's claim on the debt arising from those contracts. [Para 47][Para 42] On the second issue it noted that the defendants had produced evidence of the interest payments but that the evidence was not clear enough to be accepted, so the allegation of a 7% deduction and of USD 486 monthly receipts failed. [Para 48][Para 46] It then fixed the debt at the contractual principal with 1.8% monthly interest running from 03 December 2018, awarded moral damages of 2,000,000 riel in place of the USD 5,000 claimed, and granted provisional enforcement only for the principal and costs because the claim was a money claim on a debt right. [Para 49][Para 52][Para 56][Para 54]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a borrower admits only part of a series of loans, the lender establishes the whole by producing each successive written contract, and the borrower who alleges upfront deductions or part-payments bears the burden of proving them with clear evidence; evidence the court finds unclear does not reduce the contractual debt, though a claim for moral damages in a foreign currency may be scaled down to a reasonable sum in riel. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 56]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that a money claim resting on a debt right is of a kind for which provisional enforcement may be declared necessary. [Para 54]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The tenth loan of USD 8,700 is dated 30 December 2018 in the narrative and 03 December 2018 in the agreed-facts section, and interest is calculated from 03 December 2018 although the contracts continue to that date. [Para 23][Para 40][Para 49] One contract is printed as "៦១.០០$" where the amount is USD 6,100. [Para 38] The defendants admit USD 27,000 although the four contracts they accept total USD 27,800. [Para 43][Para 44] The caption gives the filing of 06 March 2019 in the date line of the judgment itself, and party names survive only as "ដ", "ច១" and "ច២". [Para 2][Para 4]
