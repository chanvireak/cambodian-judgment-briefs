# Bank (name redacted) v Mr C1, Ms C1-2 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 961 D, Phnom Penh First Instance Court, 09 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Thlang Phanet) · first instance · Doc ID 143266

## 1. Facts
On 05 October 2017 the defendants signed a loan agreement for USD 51,000 (fifty-one thousand US dollars) over 84 months at 12% per year, repaying principal in equal monthly instalments of USD 607.14, secured by a hypothec (ប្រាតិភោគ) over land and a house under transfer letter No. 3989 of 09 December 2010, with a third-party guarantee given by Ms C2. [Para 10][Para 11] The borrowers paid only USD 7,892.82 of principal in total and defaulted from 16 October 2018, leaving USD 43,107.18 outstanding. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 5] The bank sued on 21 March 2019 for the balance, 12% annual interest, a 25% annual penalty and USD 4,500 in costs and losses. [Para 6][Para 7][Para 8]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a court may enter judgment in the defendants' absence, treating their non-appearance as an admission of the plaintiff's statement under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure? [Para 29][Para 30]
2. Whether the agreed 12% annual interest and 25% annual penalty are lawful, and whether the USD 4,500 claimed for costs and lost time must be evidenced? [Para 35][Para 38]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the bank, creditor):** the loan agreement of 09 October 2017, the hypothec contract of the same date and the third-party guarantee stood as written; USD 43,107.18 of principal remained unpaid, so it claimed that balance with 12% interest from 17 October 2018, the 25% penalty from 16 October 2018, and USD 4,500 for legal fees, lost time and procedural expenses. [Para 21][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35]
**Defendants (Mr C1, Ms C1-2 and Ms C2, borrowers and guarantor):** all three were served with the summons and none appeared, giving no reason, and they filed no documentary evidence and called no witnesses. [Para 20][Para 29]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the three defendants are ordered jointly to pay the bank USD 43,107.18 (forty-three thousand one hundred and seven US dollars and eighteen cents) principal, interest at 12% per year on that sum from 17 October 2018 to 09 October 2022, a penalty at 25% per year from 16 October 2018 until payment in full, and 4,000,000 (four million) riel for legal fees, lost time and other procedural expenses; points 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 are provisionally enforceable and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46]
The court first found service regular and the defendants' absence unexplained, and on that basis applied Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure, which deems a defendant who fails to appear at the oral argument to have admitted the plaintiff's statement and permits judgment in absentia. [Para 29][Para 30] It then held that the parties had a genuine debt relationship on the documents — the loan agreement, the hypothec contract, the repayment schedule and the guarantee — and that the default was a breach of the contract's clause 6.1 on failure to pay interest and principal when due. [Para 31][Para 16][Para 18] The 12% interest and 25% penalty were found to be within what Articles 584 and 585 of the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128 K.Y.Prak/11 of 21 December 2011 allow, so both were enforced as agreed. [Para 35] On damages the court noted that the bank had produced no evidence of the extent of its loss and awarded a reasonable sum in riel instead of the USD 4,500 claimed, under Article 398 of the Civil Code. [Para 38][Para 36] Provisional enforcement was ordered under Article 196 and costs under Article 64. [Para 39][Para 40]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A defendant who has been duly summoned and fails to appear without reason is deemed to admit the plaintiff's statement under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and the court may then enter judgment in absentia on the documentary record; interest and penalty rates within the ceilings of Prakas No. 128 are enforced exactly as agreed, but damages for lost time and procedural expense are reduced to a reasonable sum where their extent is not proved. [Para 30][Para 35][Para 38]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that the mortgage security and the third-party guarantee support the debt relationship without altering the money award. [Para 31][Para 36]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The loan agreement is dated 05 October 2017 in the narrative but 09 October 2017 in the reasoning and in the exhibit list. [Para 10][Para 16][Para 21] The court describes the agreed rate as "12% in one month" at one point, while the plaint, the facts and the operative order all say 12% per year. [Para 6][Para 35] Interest is capped at 09 October 2022 although the term is 84 months from 2017 and the penalty has no end date. [Para 10][Para 42][Para 43] Default is variously dated 16 October 2018 and 25 November 2018. [Para 15][Para 24] The parties appear only as "ដ", "ច១", "ច១-២" and "ច២", and the guarantor's name is partly left as an unredacted string in one place and an initial in another. [Para 3][Para 11][Para 21]
