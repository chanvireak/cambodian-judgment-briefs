# Bank (name redacted) v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 1319 Th, Phnom Penh First Instance Court, 17 October 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Seng Leang) · first instance · Doc ID 143269

## 1. Facts
On 27 July 2016 the bank granted Mr C1 and his wife Ms C2 a short-term credit facility of USD 100,000 (one hundred thousand US dollars) for 12 months, secured by a mortgage over land registered on 29 July 2016 at the Phnom Penh land management department under a certificate of 24 August 2015. [Para 8][Para 29] The borrowers paid ordinary interest until 26 April 2017 and failed to repay the principal due on 02 August 2017, so interest ran unpaid from 26 May 2017 and principal from 03 August 2017. [Para 25][Para 10] The bank issued repeated notices and demands and sued on 28 March 2019. [Para 11][Para 13]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the borrowers failed to perform the credit contract of 27 July 2016, so that the principal and accrued interest are recoverable? [Para 20][Para 24]
2. Whether the contractual 8% annual interest and 12% annual overdue interest are lawful, whether the mortgage right exists, and whether USD 30,000 in moral damages is justified? [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 30]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the bank, creditor):** the short-term credit contract of 27 July 2016 carried 8% annual interest, ordinary and overdue interest to 27 February 2019 came to USD 21,405, and the mortgage over the land was duly created and registered; it claimed the principal, that interest, 12% annual overdue interest running from 28 February 2019, recognition of the mortgage, USD 30,000 in moral damages and provisional enforcement. [Para 5][Para 6][Para 27][Para 28]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** they were served with the summons in accordance with the law and neither they nor their authorised counsel appeared, giving no reason, and they filed no evidence. [Para 19][Para 22]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** both defendants are ordered jointly to pay USD 100,000 (one hundred thousand US dollars) principal, USD 21,405 (twenty-one thousand four hundred and five US dollars) in ordinary and overdue interest to 27 February 2019, and 12% annual overdue interest on the principal from 28 February 2019 until payment in full; the mortgage right over the certificate of 24 August 2015 is recognised, 8,000,000 (eight million) riel is awarded in moral damages, the rest is dismissed, the principal is provisionally enforceable, and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40]
The court recorded that service had been regular and proceeded to hear the case in the defendants' absence. [Para 22][Para 23] On the credit contract it found the defendants still owed the USD 100,000, that ordinary interest had been paid only to 26 April 2017 and that the principal due on 02 August 2017 had not been repaid, and that their evasion of the bank's requests was a breach of the contract. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26] It held the agreed 8% annual interest and the 12% annual overdue interest consistent with Articles 584 and 585 of the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128 of 21 December 2011 on capped interest rates. [Para 27][Para 28] On the security it found the mortgage over the land under the certificate of 24 August 2015 had been created and registered on 29 July 2016 to secure the debt. [Para 29] The moral damages claim of USD 30,000 was cut to 8,000,000 riel, and provisional enforcement was confined to the principal under Article 196, with costs under Article 64. [Para 30][Para 37][Para 39][Para 34]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a borrower defaults on a short-term credit facility, the court enforces the contractual interest and the contractual overdue interest as agreed where both sit within the ceiling fixed by Prakas No. 128, recognises a mortgage that has been duly created and registered as security for the same debt, and reduces a moral damages claim that is pleaded but not quantified by evidence. [Para 27][Para 29][Para 37]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that the bank's repeated notices and demands, unanswered, evidence the breach rather than a separate head of claim. [Para 11][Para 26]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
No computation of the USD 21,405 is given, and the contract rate is stated as 8% per year although the claim covers both ordinary and overdue interest up to 27 February 2019. [Para 5][Para 27] The courts below fix the principal due date as 02 August 2017 while the narrative also says interest stopped on 26 May 2017 — two different default dates for two different obligations, only one of which is explained. [Para 10][Para 25] Paragraph 35 breaks off into the initials "ច២" mid-sentence, and paragraph 36 ends with the plaintiff's name cut into the middle of the mortgage description. [Para 35][Para 36] The mortgage certificate number, the loan contract numbers and all ID numbers are blank in the OCR, and the parties appear only as "ដ", "ច១" and "ច២". [Para 1][Para 3][Para 21]
