# Bank (name redacted) v Ms C1, Mr C2, Mr C3 and Ms C4 (Civil Judgment No. 839 "S", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 8 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Theam Chan Piseth) · first instance · Doc ID 143271

## 1. Facts
On 12 April 2017 the four defendants took a base credit facility from the plaintiff bank and granted it a hypothec (ប្រាតិភោគ) over land and a house under ownership certificate of 20 March 2009; on 20 November 2017 they signed a revised credit agreement adding USD 4,000 to the existing USD 29,359, for a total of USD 33,359 at 1.2% interest and 2% penalty per month. [Para 11][Para 14][Para 46]
They fell into arrears, and the bank sued on 29 March 2019 for USD 31,835 outstanding as at 26 November 2018, plus interest, penalty interest, USD 6,000 for distress and lost time, and costs. [Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8] One defendant appeared, admitted the debt and asked only for leniency on the penalty and distress claims and for the tax and court-fee claims to be dropped. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 24]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a borrower who admits the outstanding principal and contractual interest must also pay the agreed penalty interest of 2% a month? [Para 45][Para 49]
2. Whether the bank's claim for USD 6,000 in emotional distress and lost time can be granted alongside the contractual penalty? [Para 50]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the bank, represented by Mr T D):** it maintains the plaint of 29 March 2019 — principal of USD 31,835 to 26 November 2018, interest at 1.2% a month from 27 November 2018, penalty at 2% a month from 26 December 2018, USD 6,000 distress, costs on the defendants, and provisional enforcement of the principal. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 47]
**Defendants (borrowers and mortgagors):** they admit owing the principal and interest exactly as pleaded and do not seek to evade responsibility, but ask the bank to reduce the penalty interest and the distress claim, and ask that the tax and other fees be borne by the plaintiff. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 24]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the defendants are ordered jointly to pay USD 31,835 as at 26 November 2018, interest at 1.2% a month from 27 November 2018, and penalty at 2% a month from 26 December 2018, all until full payment; the remaining claims, including the USD 6,000 distress claim, are dismissed; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal only; costs lie on the defendants. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]
The court read the base agreement of 12 April 2017 and the revised agreement of 20 November 2017 together, found the total facility of USD 33,359 proved, and noted that the defendants themselves put the balance at USD 31,835 after partial payments. [Para 45] The hypothec created by Ms C1 and Ms C4 over the certificated land was recorded as valid security for the same debt. [Para 46] The bank's claim to principal and contractual interest was admitted outright by the defendants and therefore allowed as claimed. [Para 48] Penalty interest was allowed as a contractual obligation undertaken in both credit agreements, but the distress and lost-time claim of USD 6,000 was not entertained. [Para 49][Para 50] Provisional enforcement was confined to the principal because that much was admitted outright. [Para 51]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the borrower expressly admits the outstanding principal and the contractual interest, the court enforces the penalty rate the parties themselves stipulated in the credit agreement, but a claim for emotional distress and lost time pleaded in a lump sum without proof is not entertained on top of that contractual penalty. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that interim enforceability may be granted on the admitted principal alone, even where the rest of the claim remains contested. [Para 51]
