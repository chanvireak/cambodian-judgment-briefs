# Mr D v Mr C (Civil Judgment No. 1533 "Nh", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 29 November 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Ly Sok Leng) · first instance · Doc ID 143274

## 1. Facts
Mr D sued Mr C on 21 August 2017 for USD 7,511 (seven thousand five hundred and eleven) said to be owed under a contract leasing an ice factory dated 01 December 2016; the judgment states no rent rate or rental period, only the sum claimed, together with statutory interest from 01 April 2017, USD 1,000 for lost time and expense, provisional enforcement, and costs on the defendant. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9]
Both parties were absent at every stage. Summonses were issued twice and served at the addresses given in the plaint, but notice could not actually be delivered to either party; the preparatory stage was closed and a hearing date fixed. [Para 10] Neither appeared at the hearing either. [Para 11]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a plaintiff's claim must be dismissed where the plaintiff, though lawfully summoned, does not appear at the hearing of the claim he himself brought? [Para 11][Para 12]
2. Whether the defendant's failure to appear or to file any defence alters that consequence? [Para 8][Para 10]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr D, landlord of the ice factory):** no submissions were made, because he never appeared; his case rests entirely on the plaint of 21 August 2017, the lease of 01 December 2016 and the sums pleaded there. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 11]
**Defendant (Mr C, tenant):** no defence was filed and he did not appear; the section of the judgment reserved for the defendant's claims is blank. [Para 2][Para 8]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is dismissed in absentia; the plaintiff bears the costs of the action. [Para 12][Para 13]
The court recorded that summonses had been issued twice and served at the parties' addresses as pleaded, that every practical step at service had been taken, and that neither party appeared at the preparatory stage — so the preparatory procedure was closed at once and a hearing date was set. [Para 10] At the hearing the plaintiff again failed to appear, and the court applied Article 200 of the Code of Civil Procedure to dismiss the claim by default judgment. [Para 11][Para 12] Nothing in the judgment assesses the lease, the debt or the defendant's position, because the case never reached the merits. [Para 9][Para 12]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a plaintiff, having been duly summoned, fails to appear at the hearing, the court dismisses the claim by default judgment under Article 200 of the Code of Civil Procedure and charges the plaintiff with the costs; the defendant's own absence does not revive the claim. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 13]

**Obiter dicta:** none identified — the judgment decides the procedural point and nothing else. [Para 12]
