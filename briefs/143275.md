# Vietnamese construction company (name redacted) v Cambodian company (name redacted) (Civil Judgment No. 847 "Th", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 10 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Seng Leang) · first instance · Doc ID 143275

## 1. Facts
By a contract of 24 February 2016 the defendant company bought from the plaintiff, a Vietnamese construction company with its head office in Vietnam, construction works in a factory project in the Bavet special economic zone for USD 650,000, payable as the contract provided. [Para 10][Para 14][Para 53] The plaintiff completed the works and handed them over in full on 14 September 2016, under a certificate confirming completion and full handover dated that day, but the defendant paid only part of the price, and at a meeting on 24 January 2018 the parties agreed in writing that USD 288,535 was owed, payable in two instalments. [Para 16][Para 18][Para 56][Para 57]
The defendant paid USD 75,000 on 12 March 2018 and then stopped, leaving USD 213,535. [Para 19][Para 58] The plaintiff sued for that sum, statutory interest, penalty at 2% a month from 14 September 2016, USD 20,000 in fees and damages, and provisional enforcement. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 9]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the minutes of the meeting of 24 January 2018 bind the defendant to pay USD 288,535, so that the unpaid balance of USD 213,535 is due? [Para 52][Para 57]
2. Whether a contractual penalty of 0.5% a week — 2% a month, or 24% a year — is enforceable, and whether statutory interest and USD 20,000 in fees may be added to it? [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the construction company, through counsel Chhor Sun Hong, Khom Dina and In Dina):** the works were completed and handed over in full on 14 September 2016 and accepted by the defendant; the defendant then signed the minutes of 24 January 2018 acknowledging USD 288,535, paid only USD 75,000 of it, and ignored the reminder of 10 January 2018. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 16][Para 31][Para 54]
**Defendant (the Cambodian company, through counsel Bun Keo Lim):** the plaint should be dismissed in full; the handover of 14 September 2016 was only provisional, with a one-year warranty, and the roof leaked, causing loss; the plaintiff refused to repair, so the defendant had to engage another contractor at its own cost, and the agreement was that payment would follow the plaintiff's repair of the roof and clearing of accounts. [Para 21][Para 25][Para 26][Para 28][Para 29]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the defendant is ordered to pay USD 213,535 with penalty at 2% a month on that sum from 14 September 2016 until payment; the claims for statutory interest and for USD 20,000 in attorney fees and damages are dismissed; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to point 1(a); costs lie on the defendant. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70][Para 71][Para 72]
The court accepted the contract of 24 February 2016 at USD 650,000 and the full handover of 14 September 2016, both admitted by the defendant, and held the minutes of 24 January 2018 to record a genuine agreement on USD 288,535 — the figure the defendant's own counsel conceded in both the preparatory stage and the hearing. [Para 53][Para 56][Para 57] It found no evidence at all for the alleged leaking roof, the engagement of another contractor or the cost of repairs, although time had been allowed for that evidence; the non-payment was therefore the defendant's own fault. [Para 61] On the penalty, the court read clause 16.13 of the contract, which charges 0.5% a week on late payment — equivalent to 2% a month — and tested it against paragraph 2 of Ministry of Justice Prakas No. 128 K.Y. Prak/11 of 21 December 2011, which caps agreed interest at 1.5 times the 18% statutory rate, that is 27% a year; 2% a month being 24% a year, the penalty was within the cap and was enforced. [Para 63][Para 64] The separate claim for "interest according to law" and the USD 20,000 for fees and losses were refused as having no legal basis. [Para 65]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A written record of a meeting, signed by both companies, in which the debtor acknowledges a specific balance and a schedule of payment, is a binding agreement that fixes the debt and shifts the burden of proof: a debtor who later alleges that payment was conditional on the creditor first doing repair work must prove that condition and its breach, and failing proof is in default. A liquidated penalty of 0.5% a week (24% a year) is upheld where it stays under the 27% annual ceiling of Prakas No. 128, but interest "according to law" cannot be claimed cumulatively with it. [Para 57][Para 61][Para 64][Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that interim enforceability may be ordered where the claim is a money claim that is clear and sufficiently evidenced. [Para 66]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The judgment is dated 10 July 2019 on its face but records the oral reasoning as opened on 26 June 2019, and the plaint's request for interim enforcement refers to "point 1-1" where the order refers to "point 1(a)". [Para 1][Para 9][Para 71] At line 43 a cheque of 02 June 2017 is described as written for "28,535" US dollars while the same passage speaks of it as a payment for materials, and the agreed balance is USD 288,535. [Para 43][Para 56] At line 32 the plaintiff's counsel is recorded as saying the remaining debt was "288,535" immediately after saying only 30% had been received. [Para 32] The plaintiff's own evidence list also carries a notice confirming completion and handover dated 27 February 2016, seven months before the handover the judgment relies on. [Para 11][Para 52] Company names are replaced by "0", "•" and a bare letter, addresses and contract numbers are blank, and line 52 breaks off with "financial report of company 5". [Para 3][Para 10][Para 52]
