# Bank (name redacted, district branch) v Mr C1 and five others (Civil Judgment No. 1492 "V", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 22 November 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Yi Sok Touch) · first instance · Doc ID 143277

## 1. Facts
On 28 October 2016 six co-borrowers — Mr C1, Ms C2, Mr C3, Mr C4, Mr C5 and Ms C6, the last as guarantor (អ្នកធានា) — borrowed USD 15,000 from the plaintiff bank's district branch at 14.40% a year over 72 months, repayable in equal monthly instalments of principal and interest, secured by a hypothec over land under ownership certificate of 05 July 2005 and by a third-party guarantee of the same date. [Para 14][Para 19][Para 52]
They paid USD 3,354.35 in total and then stopped from 15 September 2018 despite repeated notices, leaving USD 11,645.65 of principal. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22] The bank sued on 08 July 2019 for that principal, 14.40% interest, a 25% annual penalty, USD 2,000 in legal fees and costs, and provisional enforcement. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 11][Para 12][Para 23]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the court may decide against co-borrowers who, though lawfully served, do not appear, treating them as having confessed the plaintiff's statements under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure? [Para 47]
2. Whether the bank may recover, on top of principal, 14.40% contractual interest and a 25% annual penalty, a further USD 2,000 for legal fees and lost time? [Para 54][Para 55]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the bank, represented by Mr T D, with counsel Pa Nguon Kea and Eang Sour Sen):** the six borrowers signed the loan of 28 October 2016 and the hypothec, paid only USD 3,354.35, ignored all three notices of September–October 2018 and the final notice of 26 November 2018, and therefore owe the balance with interest and penalty. [Para 17][Para 22][Para 34][Para 43][Para 23]
**Defendants:** Ms C2 alone appeared and admitted the whole claim, saying she accepts responsibility by herself and that the others need not be involved; Mr C1, Mr C3, Mr C4, Mr C5 and Ms C6 did not appear, Ms C6 because of old age and infirmity, though all had notice. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** all six defendants are ordered jointly to pay USD 11,645.65 principal, 14.40% a year interest on it from 16 August 2018, and penalty interest of 25% a year from 15 September 2018 until payment; the USD 2,000 claim for legal fees and lost time is refused; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to point 1-1 only; costs lie on the defendants. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62]
The court found service regular — the summonses reached the defendants' residence and Mr C3 signed for them — held that their unexplained absence allowed the hearing to proceed and that they were to be treated as having confessed the plaintiff's statements under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and cited Article 4 on fairness and good faith in civil proceedings. [Para 47][Para 48] On the documents — the loan contract, the hypothec and the guarantee, all of 28 October 2016 — it found the USD 15,000 loan at 14.40% over 72 months proved and admitted by Ms C2 in full. [Para 51][Para 52] The arrears from 15 September 2018 were established by the hypothec contract and the notices. [Para 50] The 25% penalty was upheld by reference to Ministry of Justice Prakas No. 128 K.Y. Prak/11 of 21 December 2011 on capped interest rates. [Para 54] The USD 2,000 claim was refused precisely because the 25% penalty had already been awarded on the same principal, so the loss was already compensated. [Para 55] Provisional enforcement followed Article 196, limited to the admitted principal. [Para 56][Para 61]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where defendants have been duly served and fail to appear without excuse, Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure permits the court to treat them as having confessed the plaintiff's statements and to adjudicate on the documents alone. One co-debtor's offer to bear the whole debt personally does not release the others or limit the creditor's right to judgment against all of them jointly. A penalty clause already compensating the creditor's loss bars a separate unproven claim for legal fees and lost time on the same principal. [Para 47][Para 28][Para 55]

**Obiter dicta:** the reference to Article 4 of the Code of Civil Procedure, that the court must strive for a fair and prompt resolution while the parties act honestly and in good faith. [Para 48]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The interest is claimed and awarded from 16 August 2018 while the arrears are said to have begun on 15 September 2018, and neither date is reconciled. [Para 10][Para 21][Para 59] Line 15 records that "Ms C2, Mr C3, Mr C4, Mr C5 and Ms C6" consented to the hypothec while line 52 records all six as borrowers, and line 19 treats Ms C6 both as co-borrower and as third-party guarantor. [Para 15][Para 19][Para 52] The lists of parties at lines 16, 49, 52, 62 and 63 are broken, with names dropped mid-sentence and sexes transposed (Ms C1 appears as male at line 18, Ms C6 as male at line 15). [Para 16][Para 49][Para 62][Para 63] The judgment number, the pronouncement date and the registry entries are interleaved in the opening paragraph. [Para 1] All names are reduced to initials, with identity-card numbers, addresses and the bank's name blank throughout.
