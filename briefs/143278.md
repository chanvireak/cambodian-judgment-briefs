# Institution D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 616, Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 10 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Mao Sytha) · first instance · Doc ID 143278

## 1. Facts
By a written loan contract of 30 May 2011 the plaintiff microfinance institution (គ្រឹះស្ថាន ដ) lent Mr C1 and his wife Ms C2 USD 15,000 for 24 months, from 30 May 2011 to 01 June 2013, at 3% per month with a 1% per month late-payment penalty, secured by a mortgage agreement and a land-use transfer certificate of the same date. [Para 10] The borrowers paid back only USD 182.96 of principal, leaving USD 14,817.04 outstanding, and stopped servicing interest from 01 December 2011. [Para 11][Para 14]
The institution sued for the balance, 3% monthly interest from 01 December 2011, the 1% penalty, USD 12,000 in damages and mental distress, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the defendants owe the remaining principal of USD 14,817.04 from 01 December 2011? [Para 29]
2. Whether a contractual rate of 3% per month on that principal is lawful, or must be reduced to the statutory ceiling? [Para 29][Para 38]
3. Whether the 1% per month late-payment penalty and the USD 12,000 damages claim are recoverable? [Para 41][Para 42]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Institution D, creditor):** the contract of 30 May 2011 is written evidence of a USD 15,000 loan at 3% per month, the borrowers received the whole sum and repaid only USD 182.96, and the husband admits the outstanding principal — so principal, contractual interest, penalty, damages and costs all follow. [Para 13][Para 22][Para 37]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** they admit borrowing and owing USD 14,817.04 in principal, but Mr C1 says his wife negotiated the loan and he merely thumbprinted, that he knew nothing of the interest and never made the payments himself; they deny the penalty, the damages, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the defendants are ordered to repay jointly and severally USD 14,817.04 principal; interest at 1.5% per month on that sum from 02 July 2012 until paid in full; the 1% per month penalty from 01 December 2011 until paid; 10,000,000 (ten million) riel in damages and mental distress; the claim for interest above that rate is dismissed; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal only; costs lie on the defendants. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51]
The court accepted the loan contract and the repayment schedule as written evidence, noted the borrowers' admission that they received the full USD 15,000, and fixed the balance at USD 14,817.04 after crediting USD 182.96. [Para 36][Para 37] It then held that the agreed 3% per month — 36% a year — was an excessive profit taken of the borrower's predicament (កេងចំណេញ ច្រើន លើ ស្ថានភាពហួសហេតុ), which the law prohibits, and applied the Ministry of Justice Prakas No. 128 កយ.ប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011 and National Bank of Cambodia Prakas No. B7.017.109 ប្រ.ក of 13 March 2017, both capping credit interest at 18% a year. [Para 39] Claims above that ceiling were struck out, and 1.5% per month was substituted. [Para 40][Para 47] The penalty clause in Article 2 of the contract was enforced as written. [Para 41] The USD 12,000 damages claim was found disproportionate to actual loss, and damages were assessed at a reasonable level instead. [Para 42][Para 43] Provisional enforcement was granted only because the debt was evidenced in writing and admitted as to principal. [Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A contractual interest rate above the 18% annual ceiling set by the Ministry of Justice and National Bank of Cambodia prakases is unenforceable as excessive profit; the court reduces the rate to the ceiling and dismisses the excess, while the principal debt admitted by the borrower and the contractual late-payment penalty stand. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41]

**Obiter dicta:** the court's note that this is a factual case and not a model precedent (សាលក្រម គម្រូ), and its remark that provisional enforcement is available where the obligation is evidenced in writing and admitted. [Para 1][Para 44]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The claim runs interest from 01 December 2011, yet the judgment awards it only from 02 July 2012 without explaining the start date. [Para 15][Para 47] The loan term is stated to end on 01 June 2013 but repayments are said to have been made up to 10 June 2016. [Para 10][Para 14] Paragraph 38 prints the principal as "USD ១,៥០០០" (1,500) where the rest of the judgment says 15,000, and paragraph 44 cites the loan contract as document X9 where elsewhere it is X1; both are OCR slips. [Para 38][Para 44] Damages are claimed in dollars (USD 12,000) but awarded in riel (10,000,000). [Para 7][Para 49] Party names survive only as initials (Mr C1, Ms C2, Institution D) and the institution's address is left as a bare plot number. [Para 1][Para 2]
