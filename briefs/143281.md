# Bank D v Mr C1, Ms C2 and Ms C3 (Civil Judgment No. 1024 "ក១", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 26 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Than Leang) · first instance · Doc ID 143281

## 1. Facts
By a written loan contract of 13 June 2016 Bank D lent Mr C1, his wife Ms C2 and Ms C3 USD 25,000 for 60 months, from 13 July 2016 to 13 May 2021, at 1.20% a month, repayable under the bank's amortisation schedule, and secured by a mortgage of two plots under contracts of 01 September 2014. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 44][Para 47] The borrowers paid for a time and then stopped, defaulting from 13 November 2017 with USD 19,952.45 of principal outstanding. [Para 15][Para 19][Para 20]
The bank sued to rescind the contract and recover that principal, 1.20% monthly interest from 13 November 2017, penalty interest at twice the contractual rate from 13 October 2016, USD 5,000 in legal fees, costs, and provisional enforcement. [Para 6][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10][Para 12]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the loan contract should be rescinded and the admitted balance of USD 19,952.45 with contractual interest ordered repaid? [Para 37][Para 38][Para 48]
2. Whether a penalty clause fixing interest at twice the contractual rate is enforceable against borrowers who are not a bank or financial institution, when the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128 of 21 December 2011 govern the rate? [Para 39][Para 49][Para 52]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Bank D, creditor):** the signed contract and mortgage deeds prove a USD 25,000 loan at 1.20% a month; the borrowers never kept to the schedule, defaulted on both principal and interest, and the contract entitles it to rescission, the balance, default interest at double the contract rate, legal fees and costs. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 22]
**Defendants (Mr C1, Ms C2, Ms C3, borrowers):** they admit the contract, the USD 25,000 received, the USD 19,952.45 balance and the 1.20% monthly interest, but reject the double-rate penalty; Ms C2 explained that the pig farm failed, further loans were taken from three other banks, and the family now rents its house, asking to pay according to their means. [Para 31][Para 33][Para 35][Para 36]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is upheld; the defendants must pay USD 19,952.45 principal, interest of 1.20% a month on that sum from 13 November 2017 until paid in full, penalty interest equal to twice the contractual rate from 13 October 2017 until paid, and 10,000,000 (ten million) riel in legal fees; the claim above that is dismissed; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal; costs lie on the defendants. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62]
The court found the loan and mortgage contracts genuine, the money advanced and the mortgage validly created over two plots. [Para 47] It recorded the admitted principal and the admitted 1.20% monthly rate as undisputed, and treated the double-rate penalty alone as contested. [Para 48][Para 39] On that point it reasoned that because the borrowers are not a bank or financial institution regulated by banking law, the penalty must be measured under the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128 កយ.ប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011, so the claim as pleaded was "not correct in law", and legal fees were to be assessed on the actual facts rather than at the sum claimed. [Para 49][Para 52][Para 53] Provisional enforcement was granted for the principal only under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 54]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan is made by a bank to private individuals, the penalty for late payment is not at large: it is measured under the Civil Code and the 2011 Prakas on interest rates rather than by the contractual multiplier alone, while the principal and the agreed ordinary interest, once admitted, are recoverable in full. [Para 49][Para 52][Para 48]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that the borrowers' financial hardship — a failed pig farm, loans from three other banks, a rented home — explains the default but does not excuse it, and that provisional enforcement attaches to the principal alone. [Para 31][Para 54]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The reasoning holds the double-rate penalty legally incorrect and subject to the Prakas ceiling, yet the operative order awards penalty interest at twice the contractual rate, and the claim itself is "dismissed as to the excess". [Para 49][Para 58][Para 60] The penalty is claimed from 13 October 2016 but awarded from 13 October 2017. [Para 9][Para 58] The 60-month term runs 13 July 2016 to 13 May 2021, which is 58 months, not 60. [Para 13] Legal fees are claimed at USD 5,000 and awarded as 10,000,000 riel. [Para 10][Para 59] The mortgage deed of 01 September 2014 predates the loan contract by nearly two years and the judgment does not reconcile the two. [Para 44][Para 47] Party names survive only as initials and all contract, title and identity-card numbers are blank in the OCR. [Para 3][Para 43]
