# Bank D v Company C (Civil Judgment No. 1552 "8", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 6 December 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Um Vannak) · first instance · Doc ID 143283

## 1. Facts
By a medium- and long-term credit contract of 18 December 2013 Bank D's Phnom Penh branch lent Company C USD 1,000,000 over 60 months at 8.5% a year, repayable in monthly instalments of principal and interest, and secured by a registered hypothec over immovable property held under a certificate of 20 October 2008 and a possession certificate of 28 January 2013 issued in Kandal province. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 43][Para 51] The company paid 43 instalments, USD 799,114 of principal, then defaulted from 26 June 2017, leaving USD 200,886 outstanding. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 44]
The bank sued for the balance, 8.5% interest from 26 June 2017, penalty interest at 150% of that rate (12.75% a year), USD 20,000 in damages and distress, recognition of the hypothec, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the contractual rate of 8.5% a year and the penalty rate of 12.75% a year are lawful under Article 584 of the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128? [Para 49][Para 50]
2. Whether contractual interest runs only to the date the claim was filed and penalty interest from the day after, the contract not having been terminated? [Para 48]
3. Whether the hypothec was validly created and registered, and whether a further USD 20,000 in damages is recoverable? [Para 51][Para 52]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Bank D, creditor):** the credit contract, repayment schedule and mortgage deeds prove a USD 1,000,000 loan at 8.5% with a 12.75% penalty rate; the company stopped paying on 26 June 2017, the hypothec is registered over the two properties, and the breach entitles it to the balance, both rates, USD 20,000 in damages and immediate enforcement. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17]
**Defendant (Company C, borrower):** through its representative and counsel it admitted the contract, the USD 1,000,000 loan, both interest rates and the USD 200,886 balance, but contested the USD 20,000 distress and damages claim and provisional enforcement, saying it had no means to pay. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 24]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the company must pay USD 200,886 with interest at 8.5% a year from 26 June 2017 to 01 August 2018, and interest at 12.75% a year on that sum from 02 August 2018 until paid; the hypothec over the properties under the 2008 certificate and the 2013 possession certificate is recognised as validly registered security; the USD 20,000 claim is dismissed; costs lie on the defendant; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal and the first period of interest. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57][Para 59][Para 61]
The court found the loan, schedule and mortgage documents proved and the debt undisputed. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 46] It held the 8.5% rate lawful under Article 584 of the Civil Code, and treated the 12.75% penalty as late-payment interest governed by Articles 391 and 399, lawful under Article 384 and Article 2 of Prakas No. 128 កយ.ប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011. [Para 49][Para 50] Because the contract had not been terminated, the court held the bank had lost the remaining term only up to the date it filed, 01 August 2018, so contractual interest ran to that date and penalty interest from the following day. [Para 47][Para 48] It confirmed the hypothec as properly registered at the land offices of Phnom Penh and Kandal. [Para 51] The USD 20,000 claim failed because principal, interest and late-payment interest already covered the loss. [Para 52]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan contract has not been terminated, contractual interest runs only to the date the claim is filed and penalty interest, if lawful, from the day after; a penalty set at 150% of the contractual rate is treated as late-payment interest and upheld under Articles 384, 391 and 399 of the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128, and a separate claim for general damages fails once principal, interest and penalty are already awarded. [Para 48][Para 50][Para 52]

**Obiter dicta:** the recognition that the hypothec was duly registered at both land offices, and that provisional enforcement under Article 196 extends to the principal and the first interest period only. [Para 51][Para 54][Para 61]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The mortgage and pledge contracts are dated 13 July 2016 in the evidence list but 13 July 2018 in the court's reasoning, either of which is years after the 2013 loan they secure; a further mortgage contract appears as 25 February 2013. [Para 26][Para 32][Para 33][Para 43][Para 47][Para 51] The judge is named "Um Vannak" (អ៊ុម វណ្ណៈ) on the cover but signs as "Sim Vannak" (ស៊ឹម វណ្ណៈ). [Para 1][Para 63] Paragraph 15 reverses the parties, describing the borrower as lending to "Bank C D". [Para 15] The 43 paid instalments from 18 December 2013 do not reach June 2017 on a monthly schedule, and the judgment gives no reconciliation. [Para 13][Para 44] Party names survive only as initials (Bank D, Company C, Mr T D, Mr T C) and every contract, certificate and title number is blank in the OCR. [Para 2][Para 25][Para 57]
