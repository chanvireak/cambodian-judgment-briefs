# Microfinance Institution D v Mr C1 and Mr C2 (Civil Judgment No. 1561 "ឆ", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 11 December 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Kao Vandy, deputy president) · first instance · Doc ID 143284

## 1. Facts
By a loan agreement of 20 July 2018 the plaintiff microfinance institution (គ្រឹះស្ថាន មីក្រូហិរញ្ញវត្ថុ ដ) lent Mr C1 and Mr C2 USD 78,000 for six months at 0.8% a month, to buy a plot and house in Phnom Penh, with instalments due on the 2nd of each month from 02 August 2018 and the last on 02 January 2019; the borrowers mortgaged the property they had just bought. [Para 19][Para 22][Para 23][Para 57] They then withdrew the title deed in order to transfer ownership, stopped paying from 02 October 2018, and by 12 April 2019 — 192 days — still owed the whole USD 78,000. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 58][Para 59]
The institution sued for the principal, 0.8% monthly interest from default, a penalty of 25% of the principal, USD 30,000 in distress, lost time and legal costs, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 7][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the claim is admissible and the admitted principal of USD 78,000 recoverable with contractual interest? [Para 49][Para 56][Para 63]
2. Whether a penalty of 25% of the principal is enforceable under the contract and Prakas No. 128? [Para 62][Para 64]
3. Whether USD 30,000 in distress, lost time and legal service fees is recoverable where the borrowers admit the debt but ask that this head be waived? [Para 43][Para 66]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Institution D, creditor):** the loan agreement and repayment schedule prove USD 78,000 advanced at 0.8% a month; the borrowers mortgaged the purchased property, then pulled the title deed to transfer it, never paid an instalment, and the contract's clauses 2-2, 3-2-2 and 3-2-3 entitle it to principal, interest, the 25% penalty and USD 30,000 for the expense of enforcing the debt. [Para 33][Para 36][Para 38][Para 40]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Mr C2, borrowers):** through counsel they accepted the debt in full — principal, interest and penalty alike — but asked the court to strip out the USD 30,000 claim for distress, lost time and legal fees. [Para 41][Para 70]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the two borrowers must jointly repay USD 78,000 principal, interest at 0.8% a month on that sum from 02 October 2018 until payment in full, a penalty of 25% of the principal, and 20,000,000 (twenty million) riel for distress, lost time and legal service fees; costs lie on the defendants; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal. [Para 72][Para 73][Para 74][Para 75][Para 76][Para 77]
The court first held the claim admissible under Articles 9 and 23 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 49] It found the agreement and schedule proved the loan, the mortgage and the default, and noted the defendants' admission of the entire debt. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 59][Para 60] The 0.8% monthly rate was upheld as consistent with Prakas No. 128 កយ.ប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011. [Para 63] The 25% penalty was enforced because clauses 3-2-2 and 3-2-3 provided for it on any failure to pay, and acceleration of the whole debt followed from the same clauses. [Para 62][Para 64][Para 65] On the USD 30,000 head the court accepted that the default forced the institution into lawyers' fees, filing and other expenses, and fixed a reasonable sum in riel rather than the figure claimed. [Para 66][Para 75] Provisional enforcement rested on the admitted existence of the debt under Article 196. [Para 67][Para 69]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A borrower who admits the principal, the contractual interest and the penalty is bound by all three, and a penalty of 25% of principal stands where the contract expressly provides it and the monthly rate stays within the Prakas No. 128 ceiling; a claim for the costs of enforcement survives such an admission but is assessed at a reasonable sum rather than the amount pleaded. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 66]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that the borrowers' withdrawal of the title deed to transfer ownership demonstrated bad faith, and that provisional enforcement is appropriate where the debtor admits the obligation and delay would prolong the loss. [Para 58][Para 69]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The claim for USD 30,000 is rendered in words as "three hundred thousand dollars" (បី រយ ពាន់ ដុល្លារ) in the recital of the defendants' position, contradicting the figures throughout. [Para 41][Para 43] The claim is dated 12 April 2019 in the "undisputed issues" section while the file was registered on 07 May 2019. [Para 1][Para 42] The judgment awards damages in riel (20,000,000) against a dollar claim of USD 30,000, and the words written beside the principal are stated as "seventy-eight thousand" against a figure printed as 78,000.000 in the operative order. [Para 72][Para 75] Paragraph 60 reverses the parties, describing the microfinance institution as the borrower and the borrowers as lender. [Para 60] The plaintiff's address, the property title number and the judgment's own case identifiers are blank in the OCR, and the parties appear only as initials. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 19]
