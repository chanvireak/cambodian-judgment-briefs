# Mr D v Ms C1 and Mr C2 (Judgment No. 19 "J", Kampong Thom Court of First Instance, 3 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Heng Chan Sibolrin) · first instance · Doc ID 143287

## 1. Facts
On 11 May 2015 the parties signed a written loan contract (កិច្ចសន្យាខ្ចីបរិភោគ) by which Ms C1 and her husband Mr C1-2 borrowed USD 10,000 (ten thousand) from the lender, an institution whose name is redacted, at 1.80% interest a month and a 3% a month penalty on late payment, repayable over 60 months according to a schedule dated 18 May 2015, with the last instalment due on 12 May 2020. [Para 9] Ms C2 and her husband Mr C2-2 signed a guarantee of repayment in place of the borrowers. [Para 10]
The borrowers repaid 4,010.62 (four thousand and ten dollars and sixty-two cents) of principal, leaving 5,989.38 (five thousand nine hundred and eighty-nine dollars and thirty-eight cents) unpaid since 15 September 2017. [Para 11] The lender sued for the balance, interest at 1.80% a month from 15 September 2017, the 3% penalty, USD 2,500 for lost time, costs and provisional enforcement of the principal. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a written loan contract whose signature and thumbprint the borrowers admit, charging 1.80% interest a month, is valid and enforceable at that rate? [Para 22][Para 25]
2. Whether a contractual penalty of 3% a month on late payment can be awarded before the final repayment date fixed by the contract has arrived? [Para 26][Para 27]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the lender, represented by Mr D):** the contract of 11 May 2015 is in writing and thumbprinted by both sides; USD 10,000 was disbursed, only 4,010.62 was repaid, and 5,989.38 of principal has been outstanding since 15 September 2017 together with 1.80% monthly interest and the 3% penalty; it also asks for USD 2,500 for lost time, costs and immediate enforcement of the principal. [Para 4][Para 7][Para 21]
**Defendants (Ms C1 and Mr C1-2, borrowers; Ms C2 and Mr C2-2, guarantors):** they admit the contract, admit receiving the principal in full, admit the outstanding balance of 5,989.38 with 1.80% monthly interest from 15 September 2017, and admit thumbprinting the guarantee; their only plea is that they are not yet able to repay the whole sum. [Para 16][Para 23][Para 28] Ms C1 and Mr C1-2 did not appear at the preparatory or hearing stages without excuse and sent Ms C1's mother in their place. [Para 12]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the borrowers must pay the institution USD 5,989.38 of principal with 1.80% monthly interest on that sum from 15 September 2017 until full payment, plus 4,000,000 (four million) riel in damages; the claim for the 3% penalty is dismissed; if the borrowers fail to perform, the guarantors must pay the principal, interest and damages in full; item 1 of the order is provisionally enforceable; costs already paid by the plaintiff are refunded and charged to the defendants. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40]
The court began from the absence of any disputed fact: the defendants had confessed the plaintiff's statement of facts, so under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure the court was to record the confession. [Para 18][Para 20] On the contract it held that the agreement was made with the consent and thumbprints of both parties and was therefore valid under Articles 579 and 583 of the Civil Code. [Para 22] The 1.80% monthly rate was accepted as lawful because it conforms to National Bank of Cambodia Prakas No. B7.09-213 of 9 September 2009 liberalising the setting of interest rates. [Para 25] The debt itself was established by the defendants' own acknowledgement, so the principal claim was recognised. [Para 24]
The penalty claim failed on timing: the contract makes the 3% penalty run only after 12 May 2020, and that date had not yet arrived, so the claim was premature and had to be dismissed. [Para 26][Para 27] The guarantee was valid under Article 900 of the Civil Code, and it created in the guarantors an obligation to pay under Article 903, subsidiary to the borrowers' own obligation. [Para 29][Para 30] Damages were awarded under Article 398 for non-performance, assessed on the actual situation: the delay accrues interest against the borrowers and deprives the lender, as an institution, of the use of the money in its business, so 4,000,000 riel was allowed. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 37]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A claim for a contractual late-payment penalty is premature and must be dismissed where the contract fixes the penalty to run only from a final repayment date that has not yet fallen due, even though the principal and ordinary interest are already owed and admitted. [Para 26][Para 27]

**Obiter dicta:** a monthly interest rate agreed in a loan contract is lawful where it conforms to the National Bank of Cambodia's liberalisation of interest-rate setting; and a guarantor's liability under Article 903 is subsidiary, arising only when the principal debtor fails to perform. [Para 25][Para 38]
