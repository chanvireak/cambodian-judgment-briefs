# Bank D v Ms C1 and Mr C2 (Civil Judgment No. 1091 "Ch", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 4 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Ek Polyphil) · first instance · Doc ID 143290

## 1. Facts
On 25 August 2016 Ms C1 and Mr C2 signed a loan agreement with Bank D for USD 240,000.00 (two hundred and forty thousand), at a fixed 8% a year over ten years (120 months) from first disbursement on 29 August 2016, repayable monthly on the 20th, secured by a hypothec over a titled property (certificate of 21 September 2010). [Para 10][Para 12][Para 45] Mr C2 signed as guarantor (អ្នកធានា). [Para 29]
No instalment was paid from 20 May 2017 onward; two notices were issued, on 21 August 2017 and 23 January 2018, and were ignored. [Para 11][Para 13] The outstanding principal stood at USD 226,187.33 (two hundred and twenty-six thousand one hundred and eighty-seven dollars and thirty-three cents) as at 31 July 2018. [Para 14][Para 43] The bank sued on 5 November 2018 to rescind the agreement and recover principal, 8% a year, a 25% a year default rate on principal and interest, USD 100,000.00 for lost time, expenses and legal service, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 9]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a bank may recover the contractual 8% annual interest and the 25% annual default rate where the borrowers admit the contract and the debt but plead that they cannot pay? [Para 19][Para 48][Para 51]
2. Whether the court may rescind the loan agreement at the creditor's request and still order repayment of the principal and interest? [Para 4][Para 62]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Bank D, represented by Mr Ta D and counsel Vong Phakdey):** the agreement of 25 August 2016, the disbursement of USD 240,000 and two unanswered notices prove the debt; the borrowers stopped paying on 20 May 2017 and still owe USD 226,187.33 with 8% interest, the 25% default rate the contract applies when the creditor chooses the courts, and USD 100,000 in damages; it asks for rescission and provisional enforcement of every head. [Para 12][Para 14][Para 40][Para 50][Para 56]
**Defendants (Ms C1, borrower, and Mr C2, guarantor):** they admit signing for USD 240,000 and admit owing USD 226,187.33, and admit they knew of both the 8% rate and the 25% default rate when they signed; they have no means to pay, ask the bank to waive both interest heads, and are trying to sell the house so as to repay. [Para 16][Para 18][Para 19][Para 20][Para 21][Para 22]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the loan agreement of 25 August 2016 is rescinded; Ms C1 and Mr C2 must pay USD 226,187.33, plus 8% a year on that sum from 20 May 2017 until full payment, plus the 25% a year default rate on the unpaid principal and interest from 20 May 2017 until full payment; the claim for USD 100,000 of damages is dismissed; points 1 and 2.1 are provisionally enforceable; costs lie on the defendants. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 64][Para 65]
The court first held the claim admissible under Article 75 of the Code of Civil Procedure and found that the parties had been through conciliation without agreement. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36] On the evidence it found the agreement, box 1, records the loan as "USD 240,000.00 (in words two hundred and forty thousand US dollars)", that the borrowers admitted receiving it and admitted the balance, corroborated by the two notices from the Vina & Partners office. [Para 39][Para 45][Para 46] The 8% rate was proved from box 5 of the same agreement ("the periodic interest rate is the fixed rate of 8.0%") and the borrowers admitted knowing it. [Para 48][Para 49] The 25% rate was proved from box 6 on default, which provides that where default occurs and the borrower chooses the court route the rate changes to 25% a year, and this too was admitted. [Para 51][Para 53]
Non-performance being established, the court applied Articles 389, 407, 408 and 591 of the Civil Code and gave judgment as claimed. [Para 40] It refused the USD 100,000 damages claim: the defendants had admitted the principal but are in economic difficulty with no capacity to pay principal, interest or default interest, so that claim was dismissed. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 63] Provisional enforcement was granted only for points 1 and 2.1, without which the creditor would suffer serious loss, under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 58][Para 59] Costs followed the event under Article 64. [Para 60][Para 61]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a borrower admits the loan agreement and the outstanding balance, the agreed periodic interest and the agreed default rate are enforced as written even against a plea of impecuniosity, because knowledge of the rates at signature binds them; but a separate claim for damages for lost time and legal costs may be refused where the debtors' lack of means is established. [Para 49][Para 53][Para 55]

**Obiter dicta:** a creditor who chooses the court route activates the contractual substitution of the default rate for the periodic rate, and provisional enforcement should be confined to the principal and periodic interest, leaving default interest to the ordinary appeal regime. [Para 51][Para 59]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The bank's name and representative appear only as "Bank D" and "Mr Ta D", and the borrowers only as C1 and C2. [Para 1][Para 3] The default rate is pleaded and ordered from 20 May 2017, but the contract clause quoted in the reasoning makes it run "from the date the complaint is filed until the case is fully decided", which would be 5 November 2018. [Para 6][Para 51][Para 62] One notice is dated 23 January 2018 in the evidence list and 23 January 2017 in the reasoning. [Para 31][Para 39] The principal is printed with a full stop as "226.187,33" in the order and with a comma elsewhere, and paragraph 66 repeats "on the date". [Para 57][Para 62][Para 66]
