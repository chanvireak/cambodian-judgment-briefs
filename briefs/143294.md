# Mr D v Ms C (Civil Judgment No. 1093 K, Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 4 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Than Leng) · first instance · Doc ID 143294

## 1. Facts
On 28 February 2012 Ms C signed a loan contract with Mr D for 35,000$ (thirty-five thousand US dollars) carrying 700$ (seven hundred US dollars) a month in interest, repayable on 28 May 2012. [Para 13] The date fell due without repayment; she asked to pay the 700$ interest alone and to postpone the principal to 28 June 2012 under a further repayment contract dated 06 June 2012, then defaulted on that arrangement as well. [Para 14] The plaintiff filed on 21 August 2017 and supplemented his claim on 04 December 2018, seeking the 35,000$ principal, 700$ monthly interest from 28 May 2012, and 20,000$ (twenty thousand US dollars) for distress, loss of time and other damage. [Para 11] The defendant, served personally and by newspaper publication in Rasmei Kampuchea no. 8005 of 14 August 2019, did not appear. [Para 16]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the defendant in fact failed to repay the 35,000$ principal and the interest, on the parties' statements and the documents examined at the oral hearing? [Para 21]
2. Whether the court may decide the case in the defendant's absence under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure? [Para 24]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr D, creditor):** the loan contract of 28 February 2012 binds the defendant, who thumbprinted it; neither principal nor interest was ever paid, and even the indulgence she asked for — interest only, principal deferred — was breached on the very day the repayment contract was signed. [Para 13][Para 14] He characterises her conduct as deliberate bad faith and evasion (ចេតនាទុច្ចរិត) that should no longer be indulged or delayed. [Para 15]
**Defendant (Ms C, borrower):** absent without excuse; she made no statement in reply, and the court treated her silence as knowledge of the claim, which the documentary evidence then established against her. [Para 16][Para 24]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the defendant is ordered to pay the plaintiff the 35,000$ principal, interest at 18% per annum on that principal running from 28 May 2012 until full payment, and 5,000$ (five thousand US dollars) in damages for distress, loss of time and other damage; the remaining claims, including the balance of the 20,000$ sought, are dismissed, and costs (ប្រាក់ប្រដាប់ក្តី) are borne by the defendant under Article 64 CPC. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 30][Para 31]
The court worked from the documentary file — the loan contract of 28 February 2012, the handover receipt of the same date, the repayment contract of 06 June 2012 and the house sale contract of 28 March 2012 — and from the plaintiff's statement, and found the indebtedness proved as pleaded, so that the claim was well founded and had to be recognised. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 19][Para 20][Para 22][Para 23] Because the defendant had been summoned by due process on 08 August 2019 and by publication, and filed no defence, Article 201 CPC allowed the court to issue judgment in her absence. [Para 24] On the ancillary claim the court measured the loss "according to the facts" and awarded 5,000$ rather than the 20,000$ claimed. [Para 25][Para 30]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a written loan contract, a contemporaneous handover receipt and a later repayment contract establish the debt, and the borrower — duly served personally and by publication — neither appears nor pleads payment, the court will grant the principal with interest and may enter judgment in her absence under Article 201 CPC; damages beyond the proved loss are assessed on the evidence and not at the sum claimed. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25]

**Obiter dicta:** the finding that the borrower's conduct displayed dishonest intent and evasive refusal of responsibility, deserving neither further delay nor indulgence — an observation on her conduct, not an element of the order. [Para 15]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Party names survive only as initials: the plaintiff throughout as "ឈ្មោះ ដ" and the defendant as "ឈ្មោះ ច", and in the dispositive paragraph the defendant's name is replaced by the symbol "☞". [Para 4][Para 27] The claim is pleaded as 700$ a month in interest, yet the award substitutes interest at 18% per annum without explaining the substitution or reconciling the two rates. [Para 6][Para 28] The facts say the principal was deferred to 28 June 2012 while citing a repayment contract dated 06 June 2012. [Para 14] The evidence list describes a 28 February 2012 contract for the sale of a stall (តូប) between "name 1", "name D" and "name 2", a party structure that appears nowhere else in the case. [Para 17] The publication date in the closing formula is corrupted ("ថ្ងៃទី ០៤ ខែ ចោទ"). [Para 32]
