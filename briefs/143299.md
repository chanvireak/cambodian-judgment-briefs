# The plaintiff (name redacted) v Ms C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 24 Ch, Siem Reap Provincial Court, 28 June 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Chan Khung) · first instance · Doc ID 143299

## 1. Facts
On 01 March 2018 Ms C1 borrowed 11,970,000 riel (eleven million nine hundred seventy thousand riel) from the plaintiff under a written interest-bearing loan contract (កិច្ចសន្យាខ្ចីចងការប្រាក់) repayable by 16 June 2018. [Para 11][Para 14] On the same day Ms C2 borrowed 2,991,000 riel (two million nine hundred ninety-one thousand riel) and owed a further 2,549,000 riel (two million five hundred forty-nine thousand riel) for a tontine (តុងទីន) contribution, 5,540,000 riel in all, promised for 01 May 2018. [Para 12][Para 15] Neither repaid anything, despite repeated demands, and both were absent when the case was heard. [Para 13][Para 16][Para 19]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the two loan contracts, being loans for consumption with interest attached but no rate fixed by the parties, carry interest at the statutory rate under Article 584 K (2) of the Civil Code, and for what period? [Para 27][Para 33]
2. Whether the 2,549,000 riel owed as a tontine contribution bears interest, and whether the damages claimed in dollars may be awarded on the evidence? [Para 28][Para 29]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (private lender, female, 32):** she lent C1 11,970,000 riel against a written contract due 16 June 2018 and lent C2 2,991,000 riel while C2 also owed 2,549,000 riel of tontine money, due 01 May 2018; neither debtor performed, both dodged her whenever she sought payment, and their default cost her time, money, tax payments, worry and lost interest, for which she claims 1,500$ (one thousand five hundred US dollars) from C1 and 750$ (seven hundred fifty US dollars) from C2, with costs. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 23]
**Defendants (Ms C1, born 1970, and Ms C2, born 1995, borrowers):** they were duly notified of the hearing date and of the refusal of their adjournment request of 30 May 2018, but did not appear and filed no defence, so under Article 201 K (2) CPC they are taken to have confessed the plaintiff's statement of facts. [Para 19][Para 24]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** C1 must pay 11,970,000 riel principal with interest at 5% (five per cent) a year on that sum from 01 March 2018 to 16 June 2018, and 4,000,000 riel (four million riel) in damages; C2 must pay 5,540,000 riel principal with interest at 5% a year on 2,991,000 riel from 01 March 2018 to 01 May 2018, and 2,000,000 riel (two million riel) in damages; the claims beyond these sums are dismissed, and both defendants bear the costs. [Para 32][Para 33][Para 36][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40][Para 41]
The court first found the claim of 05 April 2019 admissible, then held that because the defendants failed to appear after proper summons and after their adjournment request was refused, Article 201 K (2) CPC required the court to treat them as having confessed the facts, and to uphold the claim if it was well founded. [Para 20][Para 24] The plaintiff's account was in any event corroborated by the two written contracts of 01 March 2018, so her right against the debtors was proved. [Para 25][Para 26] On interest, the court found both instruments to be loans for consumption with interest attached but with no rate stipulated by the parties; Article 584 K (2) therefore required the statutory rate, which Article 318 of the Civil Code fixes at 5% a year, running only to each contract's due date. [Para 27][Para 33][Para 36] Interest was refused on the 2,549,000 riel tontine debt because no contract showed it to be an interest-bearing loan. [Para 28] Damages were reduced from the dollar sums claimed to 4,000,000 and 2,000,000 riel, the court finding no clear evidence of the loss and assessing it on the actual prejudice. [Para 29][Para 38][Para 40]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan for consumption is made with interest attached but the parties have fixed no rate, the court will award interest at the statutory rate of 5% a year under Article 584 K (2) and Article 318 of the Civil Code, running from the date of the loan to the contractual due date and no further; a debt owed as a tontine contribution, not shown by any contract to be interest-bearing, carries no interest, and damages for a defaulting borrower's conduct are assessed on the actual prejudice rather than at the sum claimed. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 33]

**Obiter dicta:** the statement that a defendant who stays away after due summons and after refusal of an adjournment is deemed to confess the claimant's factual statement, with the court still bound to satisfy itself that the claim is well founded. [Para 24]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The plaintiff's name and the first defendant's name are redacted to nothing at all — they appear as "ឈ្មោះ ភេទ ស្រី" and "ឈ្មោះ ភេទ ស្រី កើតឆ្នាំ ១៩៧០" with no initial — and elsewhere as a bare "ដ". [Para 6][Para 7][Para 32] The claim recital is badly scrambled, with the two debtors' amounts and names interleaved ("ឱ្យ ចុងចម្លើយ ឈ្មោះ ច១ លាន កៅសិប ប្រាំពីរ ម៉ឺន រៀល"), so the 11,970,000 and 5,540,000 riel heads are only recoverable from the later paragraphs. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 21] The judgment calls both instruments interest-bearing loan contracts yet holds that no interest rate was agreed. [Para 11][Para 25][Para 27] Damages are claimed in dollars (1,500$ and 750$) and awarded in riel (4,000,000 and 2,000,000), with no exchange rate or conversion stated. [Para 17][Para 38][Para 40] The judgment is headed "24 ច" in the opening recital and signed by "ចាន់ ឃុង" while the bench is named "ថាន់ ឃុង". [Para 1][Para 4][Para 44]
