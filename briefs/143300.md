# Ms D v Ms C (Civil Judgment No. 29, Kampong Cham Provincial Court, 10 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Say Samphos Serei) · first instance · Doc ID 143300

## 1. Facts
In 2014 Ms C borrowed from Ms D a sum recorded as 13,650$ (thirteen thousand six hundred fifty US dollars) at 3% interest a month, and repaid 2,000$ (two thousand US dollars), leaving 11,650$ (eleven thousand six hundred fifty US dollars) outstanding and unpaid despite demands. [Para 8] There is no written loan contract in the file; the plaintiff relies on a record (កំណត់ហេតុ) of 23 June 2017 and minutes taken before the commune chief on 23 July 2018. [Para 9][Para 14] Ms D sued for the 11,650$ principal, statutory interest, 8,000,000 riel (eight million riel) for distress, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether, the debtor admitting the loan but asserting a lower balance of 7,750$, the court should accept the creditor's figure of 11,650$ where the debtor produces no evidence of the further repayments she alleges? [Para 16][Para 17][Para 18]
2. Whether interest and damages may be awarded on a loan for consumption evidenced by a record rather than a contract, and in what amount? [Para 19][Para 22][Para 23]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Ms D, creditor):** she lent 13,650$ at 3% a month in 2014, received only 2,000$ back afterwards, and the debtor has paid nothing since despite being warned, going so far as to say she would not pay; she therefore claims the 11,650$ balance with statutory interest, 8,000,000 riel for distress and provisional enforcement of the principal. [Para 8]
**Defendant (Ms C, borrower):** she borrowed 13,650$ and repaid 2,000$ in 2017, a further 1,000$, and 1,000$ before 2012 — 4,000$ in all — and also guaranteed 1,950$ (one thousand nine hundred fifty US dollars) borrowed by a third person, so she owes only 7,750$ (seven thousand seven hundred fifty US dollars); she has no receipts because payments were made in cash twice and by transfer twice, and she cannot pay. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 12][Para 15]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** Ms C is ordered to pay Ms D 11,650$ principal, interest at 18% (eighteen per cent) a year from 23 July 2017 until full payment, and 1,000,000 riel (one million riel) in costs and damages — the 8,000,000 riel claimed being reduced — with costs on the defendant. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23][Para 24]
The court found that the parties had in fact created a loan for consumption for 11,650$, evidenced by the record of 23 June 2017, and that although Ms C admitted the borrowing she disputed the amount, asserting 7,750$ remained. [Para 17][Para 18] Her assertion carried no evidential weight: she produced nothing to prove the further 2,000$, 1,000$ or 1,950$ payments, and conceded she had no document for any of them. [Para 12][Para 17] The court therefore took the plaintiff's figure as established and ordered the full 11,650$, with interest at 18% a year running from the contract date of 23 July 2017, and cut the distress claim from 8,000,000 to 1,000,000 riel as costs and compensation. [Para 18][Para 21][Para 22][Para 23]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A debtor who admits the loan but asserts that a smaller balance remains, and produces no evidence of the further repayments alleged, fails to displace the creditor's figure; the court will order the sum shown by the written record, with interest at 18% a year from the date of that record, and will assess a distress claim on the actual loss rather than at the sum claimed. [Para 12][Para 17][Para 18][Para 22]

**Obiter dicta:** the court's observation that the plaintiff continued to claim principal, statutory interest and 8,000,000 riel in distress, so that it "should decide according to law" — a general statement of method rather than a finding. [Para 19]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The borrowing is dated "in 2014" in the facts but the record relied on is dated 23 June 2017, the interest runs from a contract dated 23 July 2017, and the commune minutes are dated 23 July 2018; the judgment never reconciles these. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 14][Para 22] The debtor's own account is internally inconsistent — it places a repayment "before 2012", two years before the 2014 loan — and the judgment does not address it. [Para 10] The creditor is recorded as having lent 13,650$ but the operative order and reasoning speak only of 11,650$, with no explanation of the arithmetic beyond the 2,000$ admitted. [Para 8][Para 17][Para 21] The 18% annual rate appears in the order although the parties had agreed 3% a month and no provision is cited for the substitution. [Para 8][Para 22] The damages figure is printed as "ចំនួន១ ០០០ ០០០ ៛" with a broken numeral. [Para 23]
