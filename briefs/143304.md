# Microfinance institution D v Ms C1 and Mr C2 (Civil Judgment No. 11 "ឆ", Takeo Provincial Court, 10 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Kong Phumint) · first instance · Doc ID 143304

## 1. Facts
On 06 September 2018 C1 and her husband C2 borrowed 4,500 USD (four thousand five hundred) from financial institution D for 36 months at 1.5% a month, to buy a motorcycle and pay for land, secured by four immovables — residential land and rice fields. [Para 7][Para 10] They paid two instalments only: 305 USD (three hundred and five) on 16 October 2018 and 256.25 USD (two hundred and fifty-six dollars and twenty-five cents) on 15 November 2018, amortising 250 USD (two hundred and fifty) of principal, and then stopped altogether. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 14]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether borrowers who admit the contract but say they received only half of the stated principal must repay the whole of it? [Para 21][Para 24]
2. Whether the creditor may recover its contractual 1.5% monthly rate and 2,000 USD of expenses, and whether the judgment should be declared provisionally enforceable? [Para 27][Para 31]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (institution D, lender):** the loan contract is dated 23 August 2018 and 4,500 USD was handed over on 06 September 2018 on a 36-month schedule; only two instalments were made, and it claims 4,250 USD (four thousand two hundred and fifty) principal with 1.5% a month, 2,000 USD (two thousand) of expenses and costs. [Para 10][Para 12][Para 14]
**Defendants (the two borrowers):** they admit borrowing 4,500 USD at 1.5% a month but say they actually received only 2,250 USD (two thousand two hundred and fifty) and paid an 85 USD (eighty-five) administration fee; they made the two instalments and then stopped. [Para 17][Para 23]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is partly allowed: the two defendants must pay jointly 4,250 USD principal, interest at 18% a year on that sum from 17 December 2018 until paid, and 3,000,000 riel (three million riel) for expenses and lost time; provisional enforcement is granted on the principal; costs fall on the defendants. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38][Para 39]
The court held the claim admissible under Articles 74 and 75 of the Code of Civil Procedure, found the borrowing proved by the contract of 23 August 2018 and the repayment schedule, and rejected outright the defence that only 2,250 USD was received, because no evidence supported it. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 24] It accordingly found 4,250 USD still owing. [Para 25][Para 26] On interest, the creditor abandoned its 1.5% monthly claim at the hearing, so the court left that head aside and applied instead the statutory rate of 18% a year under Article 17 of the Law on Implementation of the Civil Code and clause 1 of Prakas No. 128 កយប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011, running from 17 December 2018. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29] The 2,000 USD claimed for expenses and lost time was found excessive and unsupported and was reduced to 3,000,000 riel. [Para 30][Para 37] Provisional enforcement was granted on the principal. [Para 32][Para 39] Cost follows the event under Article 64. [Para 33]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A borrower who pleads that less than the contractual principal was actually disbursed bears the burden of proving it; where no evidence is produced, the signed contract and the disbursement schedule establish the full debt and the plea fails. [Para 24][Para 31]

**Obiter dicta:** that a creditor's abandonment of its contractual rate at the hearing confines the award to the statutory rate, an alternative ground only, since the debt itself was established independently of the rate. [Para 27][Para 28]

The case number is dated 19 June 2019 but the complaint is dated 13 June 2019. [Para 1][Para 21][Para 34] The contract is dated 23 August 2018 and the money is said to be handed over on 06 September 2018, yet interest is claimed from 17 June 2018 — before either date — and the court awards from 17 December 2018 instead.
