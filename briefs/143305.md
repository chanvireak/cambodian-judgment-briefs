# Microfinance institution D v Ms 09, Mr 02 and Mr 03 (Civil Judgment No. 10 "ឆ", Takeo Provincial Court, 5 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Kong Phumint) · first instance · Doc ID 143305

## 1. Facts
On 11 May 2017 the defendants' father borrowed 2,000 USD (two thousand) from microfinance institution D under a written loan contract at 18% a year with penalty interest of 27% a year, for a term of two years. [Para 11] He repaid part and then died on 13 July 2018 without settling the debt, leaving a widow and two sons, who are the defendants. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 14] The balance as at 15 March 2019 was 1,086.98 USD (one thousand and eighty-six dollars and ninety-eight cents) of principal and 198.28 USD (one hundred and ninety-eight dollars and twenty-eight cents) of interest and penalty. [Para 6][Para 7]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the heirs of a deceased borrower take over his obligation to repay the debt, and whether the court may adjudicate where they deny any knowledge of the loan and fail to appear? [Para 48][Para 50]
2. Whether the contractual penalty rate of 27% a year is enforceable against them? [Para 54][Para 56]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (institution D, creditor):** the loan contract is written, the arrears are quantified in a statement to 15 March 2019, and the survivors of the borrower must take over the obligation they inherited. [Para 22][Para 26][Para 28]
**Defendants (the widow and two sons, heirs):** they do not know whether their father borrowed the money from the institution at all, and they did not attend the hearing, without any valid reason. [Para 43][Para 44]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed in full: the three defendants must pay jointly 1,086.98 USD principal, 198.28 USD of interest and penalty to 15 March 2019, penalty interest at 27% a year on the principal from 16 March 2019 until paid, and 3,000,000 riel (three million riel) for legal fees and lost time; provisional enforcement is granted on the principal, and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66][Para 67][Para 68]
The court held the claim admissible under Articles 74 and 75 of the Code of Civil Procedure and found the death proved by the death certificate of 18 July 2018 and the family book. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49] It then applied Article 1147(1) of the Civil Code, under which heirs take over all rights and obligations falling within the deceased's estate from the opening of the succession, and held the widow and sons liable to continue the repayment. [Para 50] Their plea that they did not know of the loan was considered and rejected, the contract and the statement of arrears being sufficient proof. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53] On the rates, the court found the 18% interest and 27% penalty compliant with Article 17 of the Law on Implementation of the Civil Code and clauses 1 and 2 of Prakas No. 128 កយប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011, and upheld both the quantified arrears and the continuing penalty. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56] The 2,500 USD (two thousand five hundred) claimed for legal fees, lost time and distress was found excessive and unsupported and was reduced to 3,000,000 riel. [Para 57][Para 66] Because the defendants were absent without valid reason, the court proceeded in default under Article 201(2) of the Code of Civil Procedure, and assessed costs under Article 64. [Para 60][Para 61]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
On the death of a borrower the obligation to repay passes to the heirs as part of the estate under Article 1147(1) of the Civil Code, and the creditor may enforce it against them jointly on the original contract without any fresh agreement with them. [Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** that an heir's denial of knowledge of the deceased's borrowing does not suspend the court's inquiry where the written contract and an arrears statement prove the debt, and that absence without valid reason is answered by judgment in default. [Para 51][Para 60]

Paragraphs 25, 27, 49, 50, 52 and 60 are heavily corrupted, with sentences spliced, truncated and duplicated, and the headnote is inserted mid-sentence at lines 5, 18, 34, 48, 55 and 64. The file number 106 carries no date, and two dates appear for the judgment itself (30 April 2019 and 05 September 2019).
