# Bank D (Kampong Cham branch) v Ms C1 and Mr C2 (Civil Judgment No. 239 "ឆ", Tboung Khmum Provincial Court, 21 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Phon Vanna) · first instance · Doc ID 143306

## 1. Facts
On 05 February 2016 bank D lent C1 and her husband C2 450,000 USD (four hundred and fifty thousand) under a short-term credit contract for 12 months at 10% a year, interest payable on the 25th of each month. [Para 10][Para 12] They serviced the loan only in part, and as at 03 April 2019 the bank computed 425,000 USD (four hundred and twenty-five thousand) of principal, 313.31 USD (three hundred and thirteen dollars and thirty-one cents) of contractual interest, and 61,566.61 USD (sixty-one thousand five hundred and sixty-six dollars and sixty-one cents) of late-payment penalty. [Para 13]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the borrowers' refusal to pay the contractual interest and late-payment penalty, on the ground that they have no means, is justified? [Para 16]
2. Whether the bank may recover 50,000 USD (fifty thousand) in damages for distress, legal costs and lost time? [Para 16][Para 18]
3. Whether the judgment should be declared provisionally enforceable? [Para 16][Para 21]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (bank D, creditor):** clause 2 of the contract fixes interest at 10% a year and a late-payment penalty at 150% of that rate, which produces 15% a year; the contract is the law of the parties, and the bank needs its cash flow and cannot let the debt stagnate. [Para 16][Para 21][Para 23]
**Defendants (the two borrowers):** they do not deny that they failed to pay as alleged, but they do not accept the claim because they have no capacity to pay, being in the midst of a severe economic crisis. [Para 17][Para 19][Para 22]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed in full on the debt: the two defendants must pay jointly 425,000 USD principal, 313.31 USD of interest, and 61,566.61 USD of late-payment penalty computed to 03 April 2019 and continuing at 15% a year from 04 April 2019 until paid, plus 10,000,000 riel (ten million riel) for damages, legal fees and lost time, payable once the judgment becomes final; provisional enforcement is granted on the principal, and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 32][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]
The court held the contract valid and binding and the claim founded on Articles 398 to 406 of the Civil Code, and found the resulting 15% a year to sit below the 27% ceiling in clause 2 of Prakas No. 128 កយប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25] On the 50,000 USD claimed, it accepted that the default had caused loss of legal fees and time over and above the business loss already captured by the penalty, and that this was a separate head, but since no evidence of quantum was produced it decided the amount on equitable grounds and awarded 10,000,000 riel. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 33] Provisional enforcement was granted on the principal because the claim is a money claim on property and the bank's cash flow makes it necessary. [Para 28][Para 36] Cost follows the event under Article 64, the defendants having lost entirely. [Para 31]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A debtor's inability to pay is no answer to a liquidated claim on a loan contract: where the contractual interest and late-payment penalty together stay within the statutory ceiling, the court enforces them as agreed and confines any reduction to the unliquidated claim for damages. [Para 25][Para 27]

**Obiter dicta:** that legal fees and lost time may be claimed in addition to a contractual penalty, because the penalty compensates business loss while costs and time are a distinct head of damage — though here no evidence of quantum was led, so the sum was fixed on equitable grounds. [Para 26][Para 27]
