# Bank D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 292 "ឆ", Tboung Khmum Provincial Court, 17 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Chun Vanna) · first instance · Doc ID 143307

## 1. Facts
There were two loans. On 07 July 2016 the defendants contracted for 35,000 USD (thirty-five thousand) over 60 months at 15% a year, repayable at 7,000 USD (seven thousand) of principal a year with monthly interest; on 13 July 2017 they added 15,000 USD (fifteen thousand) to the 28,000 USD (twenty-eight thousand) still outstanding, consolidating 43,000 USD (forty-three thousand) over 84 months at 13.20% a year, repayable at 6,142.86 USD (six thousand one hundred and forty-two dollars and eighty-six cents) a year. [Para 10][Para 11] They repaid 1.54 USD (one dollar and fifty-four cents) of principal only, leaving 42,998.46 USD (forty-two thousand nine hundred and ninety-eight dollars and forty-six cents), and stopped paying interest from 17 October 2018. [Para 12][Para 13]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a creditor may claim contractual interest and a late-payment penalty cumulatively over the same period? [Para 17][Para 33]
2. Whether a creditor on a money claim may recover 4,500 USD (four thousand five hundred) for legal fees, lost time and distress? [Para 17][Para 36]
3. Whether the judgment should be declared provisionally enforceable? [Para 18][Para 41]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (bank D, creditor):** the 13.20% rate and the 25% penalty are written into the contract of 13 July 2017 and were freely agreed, and the defendants defaulted from October 2018. [Para 19][Para 20]
**Defendants (the two borrowers and mortgagors):** they admit the delay and the non-payment since October 2018 but do not accept the interest and penalty because they have no capacity to pay, and they oppose provisional enforcement because a forced sale would ruin the family — they ask to be allowed to sell the security themselves. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 25][Para 28]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is partly allowed: the defendants must pay 42,998.46 USD principal, interest at 13.20% a year from 17 October 2018 to 08 July 2019, and a penalty at 25% a year on the principal from 09 July 2019 until paid; the 4,500 USD claim is dismissed; provisional enforcement is granted on the principal, and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49]
The court first rejected the plea of hardship: the rates and the damages clause were the parties' agreement, made without vitiated consent, and the creditor was entitled to sue and to claim damages under Articles 385 and 398 to 406 of the Civil Code. [Para 29] It read clause 2(2.2) of the contract as fixing 13.20% a year and clause 2(2.3) as fixing 25% a year for late payment, and found 25% within the 27% ceiling of clause 2 of Prakas No. 128 កយប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32] It then held that interest and the late-payment penalty may not be cumulated, so the claim to run 13.20% until full payment was wrong: interest runs from the default only up to the date the creditor exercised its claim, 08 July 2019, and the penalty runs from that date until payment. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35] The 4,500 USD was refused: Article 400(2) of the Civil Code compensates moral damage to personality interests such as reputation, life and body, whereas a debt claim produces only property damage, which is repaired by the interest and the penalty. [Para 36][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40] Provisional enforcement was granted on the principal because the claim is a money claim. [Para 41] Cost follows the event under Article 64(1). [Para 43]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Contractual interest and a late-payment penalty may not be cumulated over the same period: interest runs only up to the date the creditor brings its claim, and the late-payment penalty runs from that date until payment. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35]

**Obiter dicta:** that a claim for damages for distress under Article 400(2) of the Civil Code does not lie on a purely monetary debt, the loss being fully repaired by interest and the penalty; and that a borrower's wish to sell the mortgaged property himself rather than submit to enforcement is no answer to an order for provisional enforcement on the principal. [Para 40][Para 41]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The judge is named ជុន វណ្ណា at the head of the judgment but signs as ផុន វណ្ណា. The penalty is ordered in the dispositive part to run from 09 July 2018 [Para 46] but from 09 July 2019 in the reasoning [Para 42]; the 2019 date is the one consistent with the filing date and is used above, and both are reported here. The 25% rate is spelled out as "ម្ភៃ បួន" (twenty-four) at [Para 32] while the figure given is 25%. Lines 15 and 16 are shredded, the same sentence broken across both lines and partly duplicated, and page markers and the headnote are spliced into sentences at lines 8, 17, 28 and 39. Party names survive only as C1 and C2.
