# Microfinance institution D v Ms C1 and Mr C2 (Civil Judgment No. 03 "ឃ", Kampong Thom Provincial Court, 2 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Phong Vann) · first instance · Doc ID 143308

## 1. Facts
On 09 October 2015 C1 and her husband C2 borrowed 7,000 USD (seven thousand) from microfinance institution D over 36 months at 1.9% a month. [Para 14] They repaid 2,651.17 USD (two thousand six hundred and fifty-one dollars and seventeen cents) of principal and 1,816.62 USD (one thousand eight hundred and sixteen dollars and sixty-two cents) of interest, leaving 4,348.83 USD (four thousand three hundred and forty-eight dollars and eighty-three cents), and began defaulting from 25 July 2016. [Para 15][Para 21]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the court must award the contractual monthly rate where the debtor admits both the principal and the interest? [Para 30][Para 31]
2. Whether a creditor who proves no separate loss may recover 4,000 USD (four thousand) of expenses on top of a 1.9% monthly rate, and whether the judgment should be declared provisionally enforceable? [Para 32][Para 34]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (institution D, lender):** the contract is written and the defendants defaulted; the bank refuses the offer of 50 USD a month and leaves the 4,000 USD claim to the court to decide. [Para 16][Para 23][Para 24]
**Defendants (the two borrowers):** C1 agrees with the principal and the interest but asks to be excused the further expenses and to pay 50 USD (fifty) a month until the debt is cleared. [Para 22]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed as to principal and interest and dismissed as to expenses: the two defendants must pay jointly 4,348.83 USD principal with interest at 22.8% a year on that sum from 26 June 2018 until paid; the 4,000 USD claim for expenses is dismissed; provisional enforcement is granted on the principal, and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38]
The court found that no issue remained in dispute, C1 having admitted the claim, and held the claim admissible on the written contract. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] It ordered the admitted principal and the contractual rate, converting 1.9% a month into 22.8% a year. [Para 31] On the 4,000 USD, it reasoned that 1.9% a month is itself a high rate and already operates as a late-payment rate, so the further unliquidated claim for expenses could not be added on top of it and was dismissed. [Para 32] Provisional enforcement was granted on the principal under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure, the defendants' request to pay in instalments not displacing the creditor's right. [Para 34] Cost follows the event under Article 64. [Para 33]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the contractual monthly rate already produces a high annual rate, a creditor that proves no separate loss cannot add an unliquidated claim for expenses and lost time on top of it; the contractual rate is treated as covering that ground. [Para 32]

**Obiter dicta:** that a debtor's proposal to discharge the debt by monthly instalments, refused by the creditor, does not bind the court and does not alter the sum ordered. [Para 22][Para 23]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The loan is dated 09 October 2015 in the facts [Para 14] but 05 October 2015 in the reasoning [Para 28]; both are reported. The case number is dated 18 December 2018 at the head of the judgment but 18 December 2019 at [Para 2]. Paragraph 29 is badly corrupted — "២,៦៥១១០០០០" stands for the repaid principal, a stray "ការប្រាក់ ចំនួន ២២៦.៣៨" appears with no antecedent, and the 7,000 USD figure is duplicated mid-sentence — and paragraphs 25 and 26 are spliced together so that "ឈ្មោះ ទាមទារ របស់ ដើមចោទ" reads as nonsense. The headnote is inserted mid-sentence at lines 10, 21, 29, 39 and 40. Interest is ordered from 26 June 2018 although default is pleaded from 25 July 2016 and the arrears statement is computed to 18 December 2018; the judgment does not reconcile the three dates. Party names survive only as C1 and C2.
