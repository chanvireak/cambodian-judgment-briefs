# Bank D (Kampong Thom branch) v Mr C1, Ms C1-2, Mr C2 and Ms C2-2 (Civil Judgment No. 03 "ច", Kampong Thom Provincial Court, 5 June 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Heng Sok Chea) · first instance · Doc ID 143309

## 1. Facts
On 23 November 2015 the first couple, C1 and his wife C1-2, borrowed 25,000 USD (twenty-five thousand) over 84 months at 15.60% a year, repaying 297.62 USD (two hundred and ninety-seven dollars and sixty-two cents) of principal a month with monthly interest. [Para 10] On 20 December 2016 they took a further 35,000 USD (thirty-five thousand) to clear what remained of the 25,000 USD, consolidating the debt over 84 months at 13.20% a year, repaying 416.67 USD (four hundred and sixteen dollars and sixty-seven cents) a month, with mortgages over land and a guarantee given by the second couple, C2 and C2-2. [Para 11][Para 12] They repaid 5,416.71 USD (five thousand four hundred and sixteen dollars and seventy-one cents) of principal and then stopped: principal from 20 February 2018, interest from 23 January 2018. [Para 13][Para 14] After the claim was filed they paid a further 500 USD (five hundred), of which 80.11 USD was principal, reducing the balance to 29,503.18 USD (twenty-nine thousand five hundred and three dollars and eighteen cents). [Para 23][Para 24]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the creditor's contractual rates of 13.20% interest and 25% penalty may be enforced, and whether the 25% penalty breaches the statutory cap the court itself states? [Para 40][Para 41]
2. Whether a creditor who proves no quantum for legal fees and lost time may recover 3,000 USD (three thousand), and whether provisional enforcement should be confined to the principal? [Para 43][Para 44]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (bank D, creditor):** the two contracts, the mortgages and the guarantee are all in writing; the borrowers defaulted, the bank sent notices on 07 February, 14 February, 02 March and 12 July 2018, and it claims the stated balance with contractual interest and penalty. [Para 15][Para 19][Para 20]
**Defendants (two borrowing couples and two guarantors):** they were served but none of the four appeared, either at the preparatory stage or at the hearing, and they filed no evidence; only C2-2 acknowledged service on behalf of the others. [Para 31][Para 46][Para 47]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed in the main: the four defendants must jointly pay 29,503.18 USD principal, interest at 13.20% a year from 03 March 2018 until paid, and a penalty at 25% a year from 20 February 2018 until paid, together with 2,000,000 riel (two million riel) for legal fees and lost time; the rest of the claim is dismissed; provisional enforcement is granted on the principal alone, and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]
The court held the claim admissible under Articles 7, 8 and 9 of the Code of Civil Procedure and found the contracts, the two mortgage contracts and the guarantee genuine, none of it being disputed once the defendants defaulted on appearance. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38] On the principal, it accepted the bank's statement that 5,496.82 USD had been repaid, leaving 29,503.18 USD. [Para 39] It upheld 13.20% as within the rate fixed by Prakas No. 128 កយប្រក/១១ of 21 December 2011, and upheld the 25% penalty as freely agreed. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42] The 3,000 USD claimed for legal fees and lost time was found unsupported as to quantum, and the court assessed it at 2,000,000 riel on the actual circumstances. [Para 43][Para 54] Provisional enforcement was confined to the principal, interest and penalty being variable and incapable of fixation. [Para 44][Para 56] Because the four defendants failed to appear, judgment was given in their absence under Article 201, and costs were assessed under Articles 182 and 64. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where borrowers and their guarantors fail to appear and file no evidence, the court may give judgment in their absence on the written loan, mortgage and guarantee documents alone, and will enforce the contractual rates as agreed. [Para 47][Para 42]

**Obiter dicta:** that provisional enforcement should be confined to the principal where the interest and penalty vary with time and cannot be fixed at the date of judgment. [Para 44][Para 56]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The judgment states at [Para 40] that the Prakas caps interest at 18% a year, yet upholds a 25% a year penalty at [Para 41] and [Para 53]; the sibling judgments in this set state the cap as 27%. The repaid principal is given as 5,416.71 USD at [Para 13] and [Para 21] but as 5,496.82 USD at [Para 39], the difference being the 80.11 USD paid after filing. [Para 23] The headnote is spliced mid-sentence at lines 11, 16, 21, 29, 33, 37, 43 and 51, and paragraphs 32 and 33 are shredded into a bare list of document numbers. Two pronouncement dates appear — 05 June 2019 at the head and in [Para 58], 15 June 2019 at the signature — and the copy date is garbled as "ថ្ងៃទី ០៤ ខែមិនា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩". Party names survive only as C1, C1-2, C2 and C2-2.
