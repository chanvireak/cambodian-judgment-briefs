# Microfinance institution D v Mr C1 and three others (Civil Judgment No. 343 K, Tboung Khmum Provincial Court, 8 October 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Pon Vanna) · first instance · Doc ID 143310

## 1. Facts
On 18 July 2017 Mr C1 and his wife Ms C1-2 borrowed USD 43,000 (forty-three thousand) from microfinance institution D over 84 months at 1.10% interest and 2% penalty per month on late payment, with Ms C2 and her husband Mr C2-2 signing as guarantors. [Para 17][Para 18] The credit was disbursed on 24 July 2017 and repayable by 24 July 2024. [Para 18] The borrowers defaulted; after suit was filed on 07 August 2019 they paid USD 3,000 of principal and USD 9,255.70 of interest, leaving USD 40,000 (forty thousand) principal plus USD 1,986.06 of late interest. [Para 20]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the plaintiff's claims for contractual interest and penalty (ប្រាក់ពិន័យ) are correct in rate and in period? [Para 26]
2. Whether the defendants must pay USD 8,000 in legal service, lost time and distress damages? [Para 26]
3. Whether the judgment should be declared provisionally enforceable? [Para 26]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (microfinance institution D, creditor):** clause 3.1 of the loan contract fixes the penalty, the defendants are in breach, and the penalty will be claimed if they run out the term without paying; as a lending institution it needs cash flow and cannot leave debt stagnant for years, and its figures are documented and unvarying. [Para 26][Para 30][Para 31]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C1-2, borrowers; Ms C2 and Mr C2-2, guarantors):** they admit the arrears and the guarantee but say the loan was taken to plant pepper, the crop died and they have no means to pay; they ask that no provisional enforcement issue, since it would lead to seizure and quick sale of mortgaged land that is now unsaleable, and promise to keep trying to repay. [Para 27][Para 29][Para 32]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the four defendants are ordered jointly to pay USD 40,000 (forty thousand) principal; interest at 1.1% per month on that principal from 08 August 2019 to 24 July 2024; penalty at 2% per month from 25 July 2024 until paid; and USD 1,986.06 late interest; the rest of the claim, including the USD 8,000, is dismissed; costs lie with the defendants; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to point 1.1 only. [Para 49][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54]
The court applied Article 17 of the Law on Implementation of the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128 K.Y. of 21 December 2011, which cap interest at 18% and penalty at 27% a year, and found 1.10% a month (13.2% a year) and 2% a month (24% a year) within those ceilings. [Para 35] It held the periods claimed wrong, however: computed as pleaded, the creditor would recover interest and penalty over the same months, one on top of the other. [Para 36] The rule it applied is that contractual interest runs from the day of default to the contractual maturity date, and the late-payment penalty runs only from the day after maturity until full payment. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40] On the USD 8,000, the court treated the claim as one for moral damages under Article 400 of the Civil Code, which addresses injury to personality interests rather than an unpaid debt; the loss here was economic and already made good by interest and penalty, so no ground was shown. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44] Provisional enforcement was granted for the principal alone, the documentary proof being sufficient and the debt admitted. [Para 45]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan contract provides both interest for default and a penalty for late payment, the two heads of recovery must be sequenced rather than stacked: interest is computed only to the contractual maturity date and the penalty only from the day after it, so that a creditor remaining within the statutory rate caps still cannot recover twice for the same period of delay. [Para 37][Para 39][Para 40]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that Article 400 of the Civil Code is directed at infringements of personality interests — reputation, life and body — and that economic loss is compensated instead through interest and penalty. [Para 42][Para 43]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Late interest is claimed to 07 August 2019 in the prayer and to 14 August 2019 in the statement of facts, at the same figure of USD 1,986.06 in both. [Para 13][Para 20] The contract is dated 18 July 2017, but disbursement and the 84-month term are counted from 24 July 2017, and one recital of the defendants' admission gives the month as "កកដា" (June). [Para 17][Para 18][Para 22] The principal is printed as "៤៣.០០០ (សែ និង ប្តី ឈ្មោះ ច២-២ ... សិប បី ពាន់)", the amount in words interleaved with the next clause, and the damages claim is printed "៨.០០៨ (ប្រាំ) បី ពាន់" where USD 8,000 is meant. [Para 17][Para 28] The watermark "សាលក្រម នេះ ជារឿងក្តី ជាក់ស្តែង..." is spliced into the middle of paras 11, 22 and 28. The institution's name survives only as "ដ" (D) and the parties only as C1, C1-2, C2 and C2-2; these are kept as redactions, not expanded. [Para 5][Para 7]
