# Bank D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 08 "K", Rattanakiri Provincial Court, 28 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Chhun Chanthy Siha) · first instance · Doc ID 143312

## 1. Facts
On 25 December 2015 Mr C1 and his wife Ms C2 borrowed USD 10,000.00 (ten thousand) from bank D, Rattanakiri branch, for 60 months at 1.4% per month, secured by a hypothec (ហ៊ីប៉ូតែក) over three residential plots. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 10][Para 11] By 21 January 2019 they had paid USD 3,442.93 in all — USD 796.73 principal and USD 2,646.20 interest — leaving USD 9,203.27 principal, USD 1,630.21 interest and USD 596.89 penalty, a total of USD 11,430.37. [Para 12][Para 14][Para 15][Para 18] The bank also claimed USD 1,500 for legal consultation and distress, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 6] The defendants never appeared; the summons was served by public posting after all procedural steps were taken. [Para 23][Para 30]

## 2. Legal issues
The court recorded that there was no contested point (គ្មានចំណុចវិវាទ) and decided the following:
1. Whether the court may proceed to judgment where the defendants, duly summoned by public posting, are absent? [Para 19][Para 31]
2. Whether the contractual interest, the penalty of USD 596.89 and the USD 1,500 claim for legal service and distress are recoverable? [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]
3. Whether the judgment should be declared provisionally enforceable? [Para 37]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (bank D, creditor):** the defendants signed the loan contract and the hypothec, defaulted under the repayment schedule, and the bank is therefore entitled to judgment for principal, interest, penalty and procedural costs including legal service, with provisional enforcement. [Para 20][Para 22]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** they entered no appearance and filed no submissions; the file contains only the court's record that service was effected and that their absence was unexplained, so no substantive defence was before the bench. [Para 23][Para 30]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the defendants are ordered to pay USD 9,203.27 (nine thousand two hundred three and 27 cents) principal, USD 1,630.21 interest running to the contract's expiry date, USD 596.89 penalty, and USD 1,000 (one thousand) for legal consultation and distress; costs lie with the defendants under Article 64 of the Code of Civil Procedure; provisional enforcement is refused. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 39]
The court first held that public posting at its own notice board and at the defendants' last known residence, with every step of service completed, meant the defendants knew of the hearing and of the claim, and that their unexplained absence did not prevent adjudication. [Para 30][Para 31] On the merits it found the contract and the agreed penalty clause binding: the parties had agreed at signature to the bank's penalty rate, the amount was reasonable, and the claim was therefore well founded. [Para 34][Para 35] The USD 1,500 was found excessive and unsupported by any document proving the expense, and was reduced to USD 1,000 on the court's view of the actual facts. [Para 36][Para 43] Provisional enforcement was refused for two reasons: the defendants had not taken part in the proceedings, and the mortgaged property was already deposited with the creditor as security, so no necessity arose. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A court may adjudicate a money claim in the defendants' absence once service by public posting has been completed at both the court's notice board and the last known residence, the defendants being thereby fixed with knowledge of the hearing and the claim; and provisional enforcement will be refused where the secured property already lies in the creditor's custody, since the necessity that justifies it is then absent. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 38][Para 39]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that a party's signature on a penalty clause suffices to make the rate binding so long as it is reasonable, no separate proof of the creditor's actual loss being required. [Para 34][Para 35]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The judgment is numbered "08" with a stray letter "K" and the case is numbered simply 09; the hearing was held 20 August 2019 and the judgment pronounced 28 August 2019 out of a case filed 20 February 2019. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 3] The sum in words for USD 3,442.93 is garbled in one passage ("បី ពាន់ បួន រយ រយ សែសិប ប ពីរ") and clean in another, and one recital of the total gives "បួន រយ សែសិប ពីរ" (four hundred and forty-two) for 3,442.93. [Para 14][Para 21] The penalty recital drops the word "សេន" and the operative interest clause speaks of interest from the day interest went unpaid to the contract's expiry, though the USD 1,630.21 figure is computed only to 21 January 2019. [Para 41][Para 43] The reduction of the USD 1,500 claim to USD 1,000 is ordered without reasons beyond the finding that the claim was excessive. [Para 36][Para 43] The watermark "សាលក្រម នេះ ជារឿងក្តី ជាក់ស្តែង..." is spliced into paras 8, 20, 32, 43 and 46. Parties survive only as T1D, T2D, C1 and C2, and the bank as "ដ" (D); kept as redactions. [Para 4][Para 5]
