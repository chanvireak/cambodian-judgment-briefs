# Bank D v Mr C1, Mr C2 and Ms C3 (Civil Judgment No. 64 "N", Kratie Provincial Court, 20 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Nhil Bunly) · first instance · Doc ID 143313

## 1. Facts
On 14 June 2018 Mr C1 borrowed USD 67,000.00 (sixty-seven thousand) from bank D, Kratie branch, over 120 months at 10.50% a year, secured by a hypothec over a plot and a brick house under a title certificate of 28 February 2018 held in the names of Mr C2 and Ms C3. [Para 16][Para 17] By 25 January 2019 he had repaid only USD 921.42 of principal and USD 2,720.25 of interest, leaving USD 66,078.58 principal, USD 1,422.56 interest and USD 109.81 penalty, a total of USD 67,610.95. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 20][Para 21] The bank also claimed USD 5,000 for legal consultation, lost time and distress, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the claim of 10.50% annual interest on the outstanding principal from 17 October 2018 until full payment is correct and lawful? [Para 22]
2. Whether the claim of a 24% annual penalty (ប្រាក់ពិន័យ), apportioned to the days of default from 20 September 2018 until full payment, is correct and lawful? [Para 22]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (bank D, creditor):** the contract of 14 June 2018 fixes the principal, the 10.50% interest from 17 October 2018 and the 24% penalty from 20 September 2018, and those terms were freely agreed and are lawful. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 48][Para 49]
**Defendants (Mr C1 borrower; Mr C2 and Ms C3, mortgagors, through counsel):** counsel admitted the principal of USD 66,078.58 but asked the court to reject the interest and the penalty outright, offering one sentence and no evidence in support. [Para 24][Para 27][Para 45][Para 46]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the three defendants are ordered jointly and severally to pay USD 66,078.58 (sixty-six thousand and 58 cents) principal, interest at 10.50% a year on that principal from 17 October 2018 until full payment, and a penalty of 24% a year on the unpaid amount apportioned to the days of default from 20 September 2018 until full payment; Mr C1 is ordered to pay USD 3,000 (three thousand) for lost time and legal consultation; the excess is dismissed; point (a) is provisionally enforceable and costs lie with the defendants. [Para 60][Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66]
The court found the loan contract and hypothec genuine and the terms clearly written, satisfying Articles 579 and 583 of the Civil Code on loan contracts and interest-bearing debts. [Para 41][Para 42] Because counsel for the defendants admitted the principal, the court accepted the fact and the sum without further proof, as Article 123(2) of the Code of Civil Procedure permits. [Para 43][Para 44] A bare statement rejecting the interest and penalty, unsupported by any fact or document, could not be weighed against the written contract. [Para 45][Para 46] Turning to the caps, the court cited Article 585 of the Civil Code, Article 17 of the Law on Implementation of the Civil Code, and Prakas No. 128 K.Y. of 21 December 2011, under which interest may not exceed 18% a year and a pre-agreed penalty may not exceed 1.5 times that rate — 27% a year; the claimed 10.50% and 24% fell within both ceilings and were upheld. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56] The USD 5,000 was found to reflect a real loss that the bank could not quantify for want of evidence, and on equitable grounds was reduced to USD 3,000. [Para 57][Para 63] Provisional enforcement was granted because the claim is over property rights, the bank needs sound cash flow, the principal is admitted and no security need be lodged under Article 196. [Para 58][Para 59]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A contractual interest rate is enforced where it stays within the 18% annual ceiling fixed by Prakas No. 128, and a pre-agreed penalty for delay is enforced where it stays within 1.5 times that ceiling (27%); a debtor's counsel who merely asks that those rates be struck down, without any fact or document, does not displace terms written into a contract whose principal is admitted. [Para 46][Para 53][Para 55][Para 56]

**Obiter dicta:** where a proven procedural loss cannot be quantified for want of evidence, the court may fix a reduced sum on equitable grounds rather than dismiss the head of claim entirely. [Para 57][Para 63]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The principal of USD 66,078.58 is written out in words as "ហុកសិប ប្រាំ មួយ ពាន់" (sixty-five thousand) in four passages and correctly as "ហុកសិប ប្រាំមួយ ពាន់" elsewhere; the earlier payment of USD 921.42 is glossed "ប្រាំ បួន រយ ម្ភៃ មួយ" (five hundred and twenty-one). [Para 18][Para 19][Para 22][Para 25][Para 43][Para 60] Default dates are given as 20 September 2018 for the penalty and 17 October 2018 for interest, from a contract of 14 June 2018, with no explanation of the gap. [Para 11][Para 12] One evidence recital dates the repayment schedule 15 June 2010. [Para 32][Para 41] The operative order for the USD 3,000 names only Mr C1 yet speaks of the defendants "jointly", and stray names of Mr C2 and Ms C3 sit immediately above it. [Para 63] The heading is printed "ហលក្រម" for សាលក្រម, and the watermark "សាលក្រម នេះ ជារឿងក្តី ជាក់ស្តែង..." is spliced into paras 10, 22, 26, 41, 51, 59 and 68. Parties survive only as T1D, T2D, C1, C2 and C3, and the bank as "ដ" (D); kept as redactions. [Para 3][Para 6][Para 7]
