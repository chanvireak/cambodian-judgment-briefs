# Bank D v Mr C1 and three others (Civil Judgment No. 4.6, Kratie Provincial Court, 19 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Nhil Bunly) · first instance · Doc ID 143314

## 1. Facts
The defendants took two loans from bank D, Kratie branch: USD 130,000 (one hundred thirty thousand) on 26 February 2016 over 96 months at 12% a year, repayable by instalments to 26 January 2024 with a final payment on 26 February 2024, and USD 20,000 (twenty thousand) on 23 July 2016 over 60 months at 12% a year, repayable to 23 June 2021. [Para 15][Para 16] Nothing was paid after mid-2017, leaving USD 116,943.40 outstanding on the first loan and USD 17,443.58 on the second, a claimed principal total of USD 134,386.98. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 17] The bank claimed 12% interest from 26 and 23 June 2017 respectively, a penalty at twice the contract rate, a 1% annual "documentation fee", USD 10,000 for legal service and lost time, costs, and provisional enforcement. [Para 9][Para 11][Para 13]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the defendants' inability to repay the debt in full and on time releases them from their obligation? [Para 23]
2. Whether the 1% documentation fee claimed each year on the outstanding principal, and the penalty at twice the contractual rate, are recoverable? [Para 46][Para 48]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (bank D, creditor):** the defendants admit what they owe; the bank had already sought conciliation and sent repeated reminders, so it asks for the claim as pleaded. [Para 18][Para 19]
**Defendants (Mr C1, Ms C2, Ms C3 and Mr C4; borrowers and mortgagors):** Mr C1 admitted the debt of USD 134,389.98 and the 12% rate but asked the bank to waive everything beyond that and to accept USD 500 a month, which he would pay at once if the bank agreed; the other three, duly summoned, did not appear. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 37]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** Mr C1, Ms C2 and Mr C4 are ordered jointly and severally to pay the principal of USD 134,386.98 (one hundred thirty-four thousand three hundred eighty-six and 98 cents), 12% interest on USD 116,943.40 from 26 June 2017 and on USD 17,443.58 from 23 June 2017, and a penalty of twice the contractual rate (2 × 12%) on each of those sums until paid; the same three are ordered to pay USD 4,000 (four thousand) for legal service and lost time; point (a) is provisionally enforceable, the other claims are dismissed and costs lie with the defendants. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61]
The court held the two interest-bearing loan contracts genuine and the terms clearly written, satisfying Articles 579 and 583 of the Civil Code, and, the principal being admitted, accepted the debt without further proof. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 38] Non-payment was a breach of the debtor's duty to perform in good faith under Article 384, giving the creditor a right to enforce under Article 385; the court could proceed despite the absence of the other three defendants under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 37][Para 39] The 12% rate was within the 18% ceiling of Prakas No. 128 and was upheld. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45] The penalty at twice the contract rate was a valid pre-agreed sum, and the court found that no legal instrument yet caps the rate of such compensation in the banking and finance sector, so the agreement stood. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48] The 1% "documentation fee" was refused: the clause reads "1% for the 96-month period (loan 1) or 60 months (loan 2)", a single charge for preparing the contract, not an annual rate on outstanding principal. [Para 48][Para 49] The USD 10,000 was a real loss that the bank could not quantify, and was reduced to USD 4,000 on equitable grounds. [Para 50][Para 58] Provisional enforcement was granted on the principal, interest and penalty because the debt was admitted and no security need be lodged under Article 196. [Para 51][Para 52]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A clause charging a documentation fee of "1% for the 96-month (or 60-month) period" is a one-off charge for preparing the contract, not a yearly rate, and cannot be recovered as 1% a year on outstanding principal; while in the banking and finance sector, absent any instrument capping a pre-agreed penalty, a penalty fixed at twice the contractual interest rate is enforced as agreed. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49]

**Obiter dicta:** inability to pay does not extinguish the debt, since the debtor's duty to perform in good faith under Article 384 is enforceable under Article 385 whatever the reason for the default. [Para 39]

[Para 11][Para 15][Para 25][Para 34][Para 48] The principal is claimed as USD 134,386.98 but admitted by Mr C1 as USD 134,389.98, and the sum in words is garbled in two passages ("មួយ រយ សាមសិប បួន ពាន បី រយ..."). [Para 9][Para 17][Para 21] The operative paragraphs list the defendants inconsistently: point (a) names C1, C2 and C4 with "C3" spliced in, point (b) names all four, point (c) again omits C3.
