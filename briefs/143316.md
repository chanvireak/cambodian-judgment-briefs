# Bank B D v Ms C1 and Mr C2 (Civil Judgment No. 75 Ch 605, Banteay Meanchey Provincial Court, 21 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Nhim Pisal) · first instance · Doc ID 143316

## 1. Facts
On 25 October 2017 Ms C1 and her husband Mr C2 borrowed USD 21,000 (twenty-one thousand) from bank B D over 60 months at 14.40% a year, repayable at USD 496 a month, secured by a mortgage over 320 m² of land in Banteay Meanchey. [Para 9][Para 13][Para 34] They paid the monthly instalment from 02 December 2017 until 02 August 2018 and then stopped, defaulting from 02 September 2018 and leaving USD 18,794.21 (eighteen thousand seven hundred ninety-four and 21 cents) of principal. [Para 11][Para 39][Para 40] The bank claimed that principal with 14.40% annual interest to full payment, a penalty at twice the contract rate, 12,000,000 riel (twelve million) for distress, lost time and legal service, costs and provisional enforcement of the first three heads. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 8]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the creditor in fact holds a right over the debt against the defendants, and in what amount, and what becomes of the ancillary claims? [Para 15]
2. Whether interest at 14.40% a year and a penalty at twice that rate may both run from the date of default until payment? [Para 53][Para 63]
3. Whether the defendants' offer to surrender the mortgaged land in full satisfaction of the debt extinguishes it? [Para 46][Para 47]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (bank B D, creditor):** through counsel it maintained the claim as pleaded, submitting that the 14.40% rate is lawful because the Ministry of Justice prakas caps interest and that a penalty of twice the rate simply adds a further 14.40%, and that both should run from the day the defendants stopped paying. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 20][Para 21]
**Defendants (Ms C1 and Mr C2, borrowers and mortgagors):** they raised no objection at all, admitted the principal, the interest and the penalty, conceded the default from 02 September 2018, and asked the court to let the bank take the mortgaged land outright, saying that without the land they cannot pay in money. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 24][Para 25]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the two defendants are ordered jointly to pay USD 18,794.21 principal, interest at 14.40% a year on that principal from 02 September 2018 to 02 November 2022 only, and a penalty of 27% a year on the same principal from 02 November 2022 until the principal is repaid; the claim to interest and penalty beyond those periods, and the whole 12,000,000 riel claim for distress, lost time and legal service, are dismissed; costs lie with the defendants; point 1 alone is provisionally enforceable. [Para 75][Para 76][Para 77][Para 78][Para 79][Para 80][Para 81]
The court found the loan contract, the mortgage and the reminders proved the borrowing and the default, and the principal of USD 18,794.21 was admitted and confirmed by the repayment schedule. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 38][Para 42][Para 73] The 14.40% rate was upheld under Article 585 of the Civil Code and Article 1 of Prakas No. 128, which caps interest at 18% a year. [Para 49][Para 50] The court then refused interest "until full payment" as unlawful precisely because the contract carries both interest and penalty: interest belongs to the period of the loan and must stop at maturity, 02 November 2022. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55] The penalty clause at twice the rate, computed at 28.80% a year, exceeded the 27% ceiling in Article 2 of the same prakas and was cut to 27%. [Para 57][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61] It could not run from the default either, since that would order payment twice over for the same period as the interest, and it therefore runs only from the maturity date. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65] The distress claim failed because interest and penalty already absorb that loss. [Para 67][Para 68][Para 69] The offer to hand over the mortgaged land could not take effect without the creditor's consent, and the bank still demanded money, so the unilateral offer was disregarded. [Para 46][Para 47] Only the principal was declared provisionally enforceable; the interest and penalty claims were not yet fixed in amount and would burden debtors unable to pay. [Para 70][Para 71]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan contract provides both interest and a penalty for late payment, they may not run concurrently from the date of default: interest is confined to the period up to the contractual maturity date and the penalty runs only from that date, and a penalty fixed above 27% a year is reduced to 27% under Article 2 of Prakas No. 128 K.Y. of 21 December 2011. [Para 55][Para 61][Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** a debtor's unilateral offer to surrender the mortgaged property in full satisfaction of the debt has no effect without the creditor's agreement. [Para 46][Para 47]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The instalments are said to begin on 25 October 2018 in the facts but are shown on the schedule as paid from 02 December 2017. [Para 10][Para 39] A 60-month term from 25 October 2017 would mature on 25 October 2022, yet the court fixes maturity at 02 November 2022. [Para 9][Para 55][Para 64] Counsel's submission recites a Ministry of Justice prakas capping interest at 15%, while the court applies the 18% cap of Prakas No. 128. [Para 18][Para 50] The principal is written out in one passage as "ប្រាំពីរ រយ" split as "ប្រាំ ពីរ រយ" and the lawyer is named "ហួត សុវណ្ណ" in the heading and "ហូត សរណ" in the operative part. [Para 55][Para 1][Para 76] The judgment is numbered "៧៥ ឆ៦០៥". [Para 1] The watermark "សាលក្រម នេះ ជារឿងក្តី ជាក់ស្តែង..." is spliced into paras 6, 18, 37, 50, 64, 76 and 84. Parties survive only as T D, C1 and C2, and the bank as "ប ដ" (B D); kept as redactions. [Para 1][Para 2]
