# Bank D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 1, Kratie Provincial Court, 28 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Nhil Bunly) · first instance · Doc ID 143317

## 1. Facts
On 04 May 2017 Mr C1 and his wife Ms C2 borrowed USD 15,000 (fifteen thousand) from bank D, Kratie branch, over 72 months at 13.60% a year, repaying principal at USD 208.33 a month, secured by a hypothec over land under a certificate of 15 December 2014 by a mortgage contract of 12 May 2017. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 24] They paid for the first months and then fell into arrears, leaving USD 12,054.61 (twelve thousand fifty-four and 61 cents) of principal, on which the bank claimed 13.60% interest from 07 August 2018, a 25% annual penalty from 06 August 2018, USD 2,500 for legal service and lost time, costs and provisional enforcement. [Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8][Para 42]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the defendants' inability to repay the debt in the amount and at the times fixed relieves them of liability? [Para 15]
2. Whether the defendants are right to contest the claim of USD 2,500 for lost time and legal service? [Para 16]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (bank D, creditor):** it maintains its claim and its statements filed for the preparatory hearing of 14 August 2019 and the hearing of 23 August 2019, relying on clause 6 of the contract on non-performance and clause 7 entitling it to demand immediate repayment of all interest, penalty and damages. [Para 17][Para 40][Para 41]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers and mortgagors):** Ms C2 admitted the principal of USD 12,054.61, the 13.60% interest and the 25% penalty, said she has no money now, would sell the mortgaged land to pay the bank and asked for four or five months to do so, and asked that the USD 2,500 be waived; Mr C1, duly notified, did not appear. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 13][Para 14][Para 20][Para 38]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the two defendants are ordered jointly and severally to pay USD 12,054.61 principal, interest at 13.60% a year on that principal from 07 August 2018 until payment, and a penalty of 25% a year on that principal from 06 August 2018 until payment; they are further ordered to pay USD 800 (eight hundred) for legal service, lost time and procedural costs; the remainder of the claim is dismissed; point (a) is provisionally enforceable and costs lie with the defendants. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62]
The court found the loan contract, the mortgage, the repayment schedule and the letters of reminder proved the borrowing, the default and the sums owed, and the contract's clauses 6 and 7 made the whole debt immediately due. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 40][Para 41] Non-payment was a breach of the debtor's duty to perform in good faith under Article 384, enforceable under Article 385, and the court could proceed against the absent husband under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 43][Para 38] The defendants' acknowledgment, made at the preparatory stage and repeated at the hearing, let the court accept the principal, the rate and the penalty without further proof. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 39] The 13.60% rate was tested against Article 585 of the Civil Code, Article 17 of the Law on Implementation of the Civil Code and Article 1 of Prakas No. 128, which caps interest at 18% a year, and was upheld. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49] The 25% penalty was enforced as a pre-agreed sum within the 27% ceiling, on the strength of the parties' own agreement rather than any further legal limit. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52] The USD 2,500 was a real but unquantified loss, and the defendants had also asked for a waiver, so it was reduced to USD 800 on equitable grounds. [Para 53][Para 59] Provisional enforcement was granted because the debt was admitted and concerned property rights, no security being required under Article 196. [Para 54][Para 55]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A penalty fixed by the parties below the 27% annual ceiling of Article 2 of Prakas No. 128 K.Y. of 21 December 2011 is enforced as written, alongside the contractual interest admitted by the debtor, the court having no further ground to reduce it; and once the debtor admits the principal, rate and penalty, the court accepts them without requiring further evidence. [Para 36][Para 39][Para 51][Para 52]

**Obiter dicta:** a debtor's request for time to sell the mortgaged land and settle has no effect on the judgment where the creditor does not consent to that mode of payment. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 19]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The rate is 13.60% throughout the claim and the operative order, but one passage records the court as recognising interest at "១៣,៨០%" (13.80%). [Para 6][Para 49][Para 57] Prakas No. 128 is quoted as capping interest at "១៨% (ដប់ ប្រាំ បី ភាគរយ)", i.e. eighteen per cent in figures but fifteen in words. [Para 48] The pronouncement is dated 23 August 2018 in one sentence and 28 August 2019 in the next, from a hearing held 23 August 2019. [Para 1][Para 63] Interest is claimed from 07 August 2018 and the penalty from 06 August 2018 with no explanation for the one-day difference. [Para 6][Para 7] The judgment is numbered simply "លេខ ១" and the heading repeats the word សាលក្រម; both defendants are printed with the identical birth date of 06 April 1989. [Para 1][Para 3] A stray "សេន)។" opens the operative paragraph. [Para 56] The watermark "សាលក្រម នេះ ជារឿងក្តី ជាក់ស្តែង..." appears twice in para 23 and again in paras 9, 55 and 64. Parties survive only as T D, C1 and C2, and the bank as "ដ" (D); kept as redactions. [Para 1][Para 3]
