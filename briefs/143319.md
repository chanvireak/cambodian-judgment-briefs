# Ms D v Mr C1 and Madam C2 (Civil Judgment No. 1447 "N", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 14 November 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Ly Sok Leng) · first instance · Doc ID 143319

## 1. Facts
The defendants, a married couple (Mr C1, 45, and Madam C2, 40), borrowed twice from the plaintiff Ms D: 20,000$ (twenty thousand US dollars) on 20 July 2017 under a written loan letter at 2% per month, repayable on three months' notice, and a further 20,000$ on 18 February 2018 with no written instrument, witnessed by a third party — 40,000$ (forty thousand US dollars) in all. [Para 13][Para 15][Para 16] They paid interest for 15 months on the first loan and 8 months on the second, then stopped; no receipts for any interest payment were ever issued. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 20] The plaintiff sued on 8 August 2019 for the 40,000$ principal, 2% monthly interest from 20 October 2018, 10,000$ damages, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the interest paid at 2% per month, being above the statutory ceiling, is to be recharacterised as repayment of principal? [Para 35][Para 52]
2. Whether the second loan, made without any written contract, nonetheless bears interest, and at what rate? [Para 26][Para 44]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Ms D, lender):** she maintained the complaint of 8 August 2019, pleaded the 40,000$ lent in two tranches and the 2% monthly rate agreed, and asked for the principal, continuing interest from 20 October 2018, damages and provisional enforcement. [Para 17][Para 21]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Madam C2, borrowers):** they admitted borrowing 40,000$ in two tranches at 2% a month but pleaded that the plaintiff's excessive rate had exhausted their capacity to pay; they asked that the interest already paid be set off against principal, that the lawful 5% annual rate under Article 318 of the Civil Code be applied to the reduced balance, and that the damages claim, provisional enforcement and costs be refused. [Para 22][Para 24][Para 25][Para 26][Para 29][Para 30]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed in part. The defendants must pay jointly 37,254.4$ (thirty-seven thousand two hundred fifty-four point four US dollars) principal, 18% annual interest on that sum from 20 October 2018 until payment, and 6,000$ (six thousand US dollars) moral damages, the excess above that being dismissed; the judgment is declared provisionally enforceable as to point 1; costs lie on the defendants. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62][Para 63]
Both defendants admitted the 40,000$ debt, so the court accepted the claim of principal as pleaded. [Para 50] The rates were agreed at 2% a month, i.e. 24% a year, which the court found to exceed the ceiling set by Article 585(k), paragraph 2 of the Civil Code and Ministry of Justice Prakas No. 128 KYK.PRK/11, namely 18% a year. [Para 51][Para 52] Because the interest payments were never receipted, the court weighed the parties' competing accounts of how many months were paid and preferred the defendants' figure of 25 months in total, up to November 2018, since it was corroborated by their admission of the loans and the rate. [Para 53] On that footing it computed the interest paid in excess as 2,745.6$ and set it off against principal, leaving 37,254.4$, on which interest at 18% a year runs from 20 October 2018. [Para 52][Para 54][Para 60]
On damages, the court found the defendants at fault for failing to perform the contract and that the breach caused loss, but the plaintiff had proved no measure of it, so damages were awarded "according to what is reasonable" — 6,000$. [Para 55][Para 61] Provisional enforcement was granted for the principal only, under Article 196(k), paragraph 1 of the Code of Civil Procedure, to avoid prejudice to the plaintiff. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan contract fixes interest above the 18% annual ceiling set by Article 585(k) of the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128 KYK.PRK/11, the excess actually paid is not forfeited but is reallocated to the principal, reducing the outstanding debt, and future interest runs at the ceiling on the reduced balance. [Para 52][Para 54][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** the preference for the borrowers' uncorroborated month-count over the lender's, because no payment receipts existed, and the award of damages "according to what is reasonable" where loss was not quantified. [Para 53][Para 55]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The court adopts 25 months of interest paid although the plaintiff pleaded 15 and 8 months (23) and the defendants pleaded 16 and 9 months (25); the discrepancy is left unexplained. [Para 14][Para 16][Para 53] The stated excess interest of 2,745.6$ is given without any computation and cannot be derived from the figures recited. [Para 54] The principal is printed as "40,000 (ពីរ ម៉ឺន)" — the words say twenty thousand against a forty-thousand figure. [Para 54] The Prakas is cited as No. 128 KYK.PRK/11 dated 21 December 2011, fixing an 18% annual ceiling, after the same instrument was relied on earlier in the judgment. [Para 52] Party names survive only as initials and bare digits; addresses and professions are stripped.
