# Ms D v Madam C1 and Madam C2 (Civil Judgment No. 1489 "Ph", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 21 November 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Sam Bun Pow) · first instance · Doc ID 143320

## 1. Facts
On 17 January 2011 the plaintiff Ms D lent 150,000$ (one hundred fifty thousand US dollars) at 2% per month for a twelve-month term, secured by a mortgage over immovable property. [Para 13] The borrower defaulted and died on 22 January 2012. [Para 14][Para 15] Madam C2, for the deceased, and her daughter Madam C1 then signed two acknowledgments of debt — on 4 February 2014 and 1 June 2018 — accepting 150,000$ principal plus 117,250$ interest and promising payment by 30 June 2018, pledging two further title certificates. [Para 16][Para 38][Para 39] Nothing was paid, and the plaintiff sued on 11 June 2018. [Para 13][Para 17]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a later acknowledgment of debt binds the acknowledgers to the principal and accumulated interest of the original borrower? [Para 38][Para 40]
2. Whether a contractual late-payment penalty and general damages may be awarded where the plaintiff cannot state their amount or period? [Para 43][Para 45]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Ms D, creditor):** she maintained her claim for 150,000$ principal, interest which by 30 January 2019 had grown to 126,250$ (one hundred twenty-six thousand two hundred fifty US dollars), the late-payment penalty in clause 7 of the 17 January 2011 agreement, 20,000$ for loss of time and moral injury, costs and provisional enforcement. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 19]
**Respondents (Madam C1 and Madam C2, acknowledgers):** they admitted the 150,000$ principal as truly borrowed and admitted the 126,250$ of accrued interest, offered to pay half of the penalty and of the 20,000$ damages claim, but refused costs and provisional enforcement because they had no money, their business having failed, and enforcement would prejudice them. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22][Para 23][Para 24]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the acknowledgers must pay 150,000$ principal and 126,250$ total interest computed to 30 January 2019; the late-payment penalty and the 20,000$ damages claim are dismissed; costs lie on the respondents, whose own claim that the plaintiff bear them is refused; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal and interest. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54]
The court grounded the award on the acknowledgment and agreement to settle of 1 June 2018 between Madam C2 and Ms D, which showed C2 accepting the debt of the deceased and of her daughter C1 in the sum of 150,000$ plus 117,250$ interest, and pledging two title certificates dated 11 April 2006 and 14 December 2005. [Para 38][Para 39] Both respondents then admitted the principal and the 126,250$ interest figure at the hearing, so no issue remained on those heads. [Para 40] The court found the interest reasonable having regard to the respondents' present situation and the rate the plaintiff had actually received, and ordered it paid to 30 January 2019 only. [Para 41]
The penalty failed for want of particularity: asked at the hearing what the penalty amounted to and for what period, the plaintiff could not state it clearly enough for verification, so the claim was dismissed. [Para 43][Para 44] The 20,000$ claim for costs, lost time and moral injury was refused as unreasonable because the plaintiff had already profited from the monthly interest and the default penalty. [Para 45][Para 46] Provisional enforcement was granted for principal and interest under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure, the sums being computable and admitted, to avoid delay in repayment. [Para 47][Para 48] Costs followed under Article 64. [Para 49]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A person who signs a written acknowledgment of another's debt, fixing both principal and accrued interest and a payment date, is bound by that acknowledgment to the sums acknowledged; but a penalty clause, and general damages for lost time and moral injury, will be refused where the claimant cannot particularise the amount and period, or has already recovered interest and penalties exceeding the loss. [Para 38][Para 40][Para 43][Para 45]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that the interest rate was "reasonable" by reference to the debtors' present financial situation rather than to any statutory ceiling. [Para 41]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The claim for interest is pleaded as "11,725,000$" in the prayer but as 126,250$ in the narrative and in the award. [Para 8][Para 18][Para 51] The interest period is repeatedly printed as running from 30 June 2011 to 30 January 2019, although the acknowledgment fixed payment for 30 June 2018 and the pleading dates the accrual from 30 June 2018; both readings are reported, not reconciled. [Para 16][Para 18][Para 21][Para 51] The deceased borrower's name is redacted to "ឈ្មោះ" with no initial, and the acknowledgers appear only as C1 and C2; the prayer's date of 11 June 2018 differs from the case number's filing date of 20 June 2018. [Para 1][Para 13]
