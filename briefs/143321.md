# Mr T (branch manager) v Madam C1, Mr 01-2, Madam 02 and Mr C2-2 (Civil Judgment No. 1655 "Ng", Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 30 December 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Yich Cheana Vy) · first instance · Doc ID 143321

## 1. Facts
On 16 September 2016 the four defendants borrowed 20,000$ (twenty thousand US dollars) from the plaintiff bank at 14.40% a year over 72 months, secured by a mortgage over land under title certificate of 23 February 2010. [Para 11][Para 14] On 06 May 2017 they signed a fresh agreement for 29,453.48$ (twenty-nine thousand four hundred fifty-three US dollars and thirty-eight cents) at 14% a year over 72 months, expressly to repay the 2016 loan, with a second mortgage contract of 07 June 2017 and third-party guarantees. [Para 12][Para 14] Payments stopped on 11 December 2018; the bank computed the outstanding principal at 23,952.87$ and sued on 8 July 2019 for that sum, 14% interest, a 25% annual penalty and 3,000$ in legal and procedural costs. [Para 6][Para 7][Para 8][Para 9][Para 15]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the borrowers' admission of the principal, the 14% interest and the 25% penalty entitles the bank to judgment on those heads? [Para 23][Para 47]
2. Whether the 3,000$ claimed for legal service fees, lost time and procedural expenses is legally founded where no evidence supports it? [Para 24][Para 51]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr T, bank branch representative):** it relied on the 2016 and 2017 loan agreements, the two mortgage contracts, the guarantees, the repayment schedules and its demand letters of 24, 25, 31 December 2018 and 28 January 2019, and pleaded that 1,000$ had been received in August, October and December 2019, leaving 23,952.47$ due. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 40]
**Defendants (Madam C1, Mr 01-2, Madam 02, Mr C2-2, borrowers and mortgagors):** they admitted the 23,952.87$ principal — net of the 1,000$ paid in 2019 — and admitted both the 14% annual interest and the 25% annual penalty, but asked that the 3,000$ legal and procedural costs claim be dismissed and counter-claimed 1,000$ of such costs against the bank. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 20][Para 21]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the defendants must jointly pay 23,952.87$ principal, 14% annual interest on it from 5 February 2019 to 8 July 2019, a 25% annual penalty on it from 9 July 2019 until payment, and 1,000$ (one thousand US dollars) for legal service fees, lost time and procedural expenses; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal only; costs lie on the defendants. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 64]
The court took the debt as established by the two loan agreements, the two mortgage contracts, the two third-party guarantees, both repayment schedules, the title certificate and the four demand letters, all of which showed a loan of 29,453.48$ at 14% a year with a 25% annual penalty. [Para 40] Part had been repaid, leaving 23,952.87$ plus interest from 5 February 2019. [Para 41] The defendants admitted the debt, the rate and the penalty in their statements, and Madam 02 admitted them again at the preparatory stage, so the court accepted those heads without further proof. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 46][Para 47] The two defendants who failed to appear were treated as having admitted the plaintiff's factual allegations, the court having summoned them regularly, and judgment was entered against them in absentia under Article 201(2) of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 55][Para 57]
Interest at 14% was, however, cut off at the date the complaint was filed — 8 July 2019 — under Article 583(k), paragraph 3 of the Civil Code, and the 25% penalty was made to run from 9 July 2019, after that date, under Articles 389(a), 398 and 399 of the Civil Code and clause 2 of the Prakas on capped interest of 21 December 2011. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 60][Para 61] On the costs claim, the bank proved nothing, so the court awarded only the 1,000$ the defendants themselves had offered. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 62] Provisional enforcement was confined to the principal because the defendants had admitted it. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 63]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a borrower admits the principal, the contractual interest and the penalty, the court enters judgment on those heads without further proof; but contractual interest stops at the date the claim is filed and the penalty runs only from the day after, and a claim for legal and procedural expenses that is unsupported by any evidence fails save to the extent the defendant has offered to pay it. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 51][Para 53]

**Obiter dicta:** the application of Article 201(2) of the Code of Civil Procedure to treat non-appearing defendants as admitting the plaintiff's factual allegations. [Para 55]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The principal is printed as 23,952.87$ in the prayer and award but 23,952.47$ where the 1,000$ repayment is deducted. [Para 15][Para 17][Para 59] The penalty is claimed from 11 December 2018 in the prayer but awarded only from 9 July 2019. [Para 8][Para 61] The counter-claim is written as "1.000 (បី ពាន់)" — the figure says one thousand while the words say three thousand. [Para 21] The first loan's rate is given as 14.40% and the refinancing rate as 14%. [Para 11][Para 12] Party names are heavily corrupted: several appear as CJK or Arabic glyphs, and the defendants are identified only by digit strings; these are OCR losses, not names.
