# Microfinance Institution D v Madam C1, Mr C2, Mr C3 and Mr C4 (Civil Judgment No. 10 "Kh", Preah Vihear Provincial Court, 12 December 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Chien Sros) · first instance · Doc ID 143328

## 1. Facts
Madam C1 and her husband Mr C2 borrowed 50,000$ (fifty thousand US dollars) from the plaintiff microfinance institution under a loan agreement of 10 August 2017, at 1.15% interest per month over 84 months, repayable monthly according to a schedule dated 14 August 2017, with Mr C3 and Mr C4 standing as guarantors. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 28] The borrowers repaid 18,049.87$ in all — 9,536$ principal and 8,513.87$ interest — and then defaulted, leaving 40,464$ (forty thousand four hundred sixty-four US dollars) of principal and 3,481.67$ of accrued interest unpaid to 30 July 2019. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 35] The institution sued on 16 August 2019 for the principal, 1.15% monthly interest from 31 July 2019, a 2% monthly penalty from 14 August 2024, an early-settlement service fee of 8,125.54$, the overdue interest, 10,000$ damages, costs and provisional enforcement. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 9]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether interest may be awarded until full payment, or only to the last day of the contractual term? [Para 29][Para 37]
2. Whether the 2% monthly penalty, the 8,125.54$ early-settlement fee and the 3,481.67$ overdue interest are recoverable, or duplicative of the interest already claimed? [Para 32][Para 34][Para 36]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Microfinance Institution D, lender):** it claimed the outstanding principal with contractual interest and a penal clause in clause 3.1 of the agreement, an early-settlement fee because the borrowers had agreed to use the facility for a minimum of 36 months but performed only 20, the accumulated overdue interest, 10,000$ for lost time, and provisional enforcement to stop interest mounting. [Para 21][Para 26][Para 33]
**Defendants (Madam C1, Mr C2, Mr C3 and Mr C4, borrowers and guarantors):** they admitted signing the agreement and owing the debt, but pleaded that they had no means to pay, produced no evidence, and objected to every head of claim including costs and provisional enforcement, asking for indulgence. [Para 18][Para 20][Para 22][Para 31]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** all four defendants must pay 40,464$ principal with 1.15% monthly interest from 31 July 2019 to 14 August 2024, and a 2% monthly penalty on the same principal from 14 August 2024 until payment; 500$ (five hundred US dollars) for loss of time, the excess refused; the 8,125.54$ early-settlement fee and the 3,481.67$ overdue interest are refused; provisional enforcement is refused; costs lie on the defendants. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45]
The court took the debt as admitted: the defendants acknowledged both the loan and the contract, and the guarantors were bound with them. [Para 29][Para 31] It then trimmed the claim. Interest at 1.15% a month was allowed only to the final day of the contractual term, 14 August 2024, not "until full payment" as pleaded, because the contract itself fixed that end date. [Para 29][Para 37] The 2% monthly penalty was upheld although not provided for in the Civil Code, being a pre-agreed liquidated compensation for breach, valid under clause 3.1 of the agreement. [Para 30][Para 32][Para 38]
The 8,125.54$ early-settlement fee was refused as in substance interest during the contract period, which the court was already awarding, so that granting both would duplicate recovery. [Para 33][Para 34] The 3,481.67$ of overdue interest was refused for the same reason: it was interest accruing during the contract term, not interest claimable after termination. [Para 35][Para 36] On damages, the 10,000$ claim was unsupported by evidence, so the court awarded 500$ as the actual loss. [Para 23][Para 42] Provisional enforcement was refused for want of necessity. [Para 27][Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan agreement fixes a term, contractual interest runs only to the last day of that term and not until payment; a penalty clause may be enforced as pre-agreed compensation for breach even absent an express Civil Code provision; but fees and charges that in substance duplicate interest already awarded — an early-settlement fee and accrued overdue interest — are refused. [Para 29][Para 32][Para 34][Para 36]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that provisional enforcement is refused where the claim discloses no necessity for it. [Para 27]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The loan is dated 8 January 2019 in the summary of facts but 10 August 2017 in the reasoning and in the 84-month term, which alone reconciles with the 14 August 2024 end date. [Para 10][Para 28] Principal repaid is given as 9,536$ in the facts and 9,336$ in the reasoning. [Para 12][Para 28] The judgment is dated 12 December 2019 in the heading but pronounced 17 December 2019 and copied out on 25 December 2019. [Para 1][Para 46][Para 48] C1 is described as female yet paired with a "husband" (ប្តី); party names appear only as C1–C4 and the lender as "ដ".
