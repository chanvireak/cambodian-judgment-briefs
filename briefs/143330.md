# Microfinance Institution D v Ms C1, Mr C1-2, Ms C2 and Mr C2-2 (Civil Judgment No. 25 K, Pailin Provincial Court, 20 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Kim Meng) · first instance · Doc ID 143330

## 1. Facts
On 16 June 2016 Ms C1 and her husband Mr C1-2 borrowed 3,500 (three thousand five hundred) US dollars from Microfinance Institution D at 1.8% interest per month, by a written loan contract creating a hypothec (បង្កើត ហ៊ីប៉ូតែក), with Ms C2 and her husband Mr C2-2 pledging their land title as guarantors. [Para 10][Para 18][Para 19]
The borrowers repaid 1,270 (one thousand two hundred seventy) US dollars, leaving 2,723 (two thousand seven hundred twenty-three) US dollars, and stopped paying altogether from 06 March 2017. [Para 11][Para 21] The claim as filed was for that principal, 1.8% monthly interest, daily penalties of 0.1% on principal and 0.2% on interest, and 6,000,000 (six million) riel in lost-time and expense damages. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether, the loan contract being admitted and no point of dispute raised, the two borrowers and the two guarantors are jointly liable for the outstanding principal together with contractual interest and daily penalties? [Para 22][Para 25][Para 26]
2. Whether the claimed 1.8% monthly interest and 6,000,000 riel damages are awarded as pleaded, or reduced by the court to 18% per annum and 2,000,000 riel? [Para 27][Para 28][Para 32][Para 35]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr T D, representative of Microfinance Institution D):** the contract of 16 June 2016 is in writing, secured by a hypothec over the guarantors' land title; 1,270 US dollars was repaid and 2,723 US dollars remains unpaid since 06 March 2017, so the full contractual principal, interest, penalties and 6,000,000 riel damages are due. [Para 10][Para 12]
**Defendants (Mr C1-2, borrower; Ms C2, guarantor):** Mr C1-2 admits borrowing 3,500 US dollars and repaying 1,270, but says he stopped because he and Ms C1 divorced, and asks to pay 100 US dollars a month with relief on interest; Ms C2 admits the claim outright, she and her husband being the guarantors. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15][Para 16][Para 17] Ms C1 and Mr C2-2 were duly summoned and did not appear. [Para 29]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is partly allowed and partly dismissed. All four defendants are ordered jointly to pay the institution: 2,723 US dollars principal; interest of 18 (eighteen) per cent per annum from 06 March 2017 until payment in full; a penalty of 0.1% per day on principal; a penalty of 0.2% per day on interest from 06 March 2017; and 2,000,000 (two million) riel in lost-time and expense damages. The remainder of the claim is dismissed, costs lie on the defendants, and the judgment is declared provisionally enforceable. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38]
The court recorded that there was no contested point at all ("គ្មាន"), the complaint being regular and admissible under Article 75 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 22][Para 24] It then read the loan-and-hypothec contract as proving that all four defendants borrowed from the institution, and found on the evidence that the defendants admitted owing the principal claimed and that the repayment date had passed. [Para 25][Para 26] The principal and interest claim was therefore granted as acknowledged by the parties, damages were left to the court's assessment, and the two defendants who stayed away after proper summons were taken to accept the claim. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29] Costs followed under Article 64(a)(1) of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 30]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where defendants admit a written loan contract and the outstanding balance, the court will enter judgment against borrowers and guarantors jointly for the contractual principal and penalties, but will itself fix the rate of interest and the quantum of unliquidated damages instead of awarding them as pleaded — a contractual monthly rate being replaced by the 18% annual rate and a pleaded damages figure being cut to an assessed sum. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 32][Para 35]

**Obiter dicta:** that a defendant duly summoned who fails to appear without excuse is treated as accepting the plaintiff's claim, and that the successful plaintiff's costs are borne by the defendants under Article 64(a)(1). [Para 29][Para 30]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The court awarded 18% per annum although the pleaded rate was 1.8% per month (about 21.6% a year) and gave no reason for the substitution. [Para 5][Para 32] Damages were pleaded at 6,000,000 riel and awarded at 2,000,000 riel, again without stated reasons. [Para 8][Para 35] The sentence at [Para 28] breaks off mid-clause ("ច១-២ និង ឈ្មោះ ច២ ដូចនេះ គប្បី តុលាការ") and the reasoning for joint liability of the guarantors is not fully legible there. [Para 28] Stray OCR characters ("ដ", "3") interrupt the operative paragraphs. [Para 32][Para 33] Party names survive only as initials and digits (ច១, ច១-២, ច២, ច២-២) and the lender as "ដ"; these are OCR redactions, not names, and are not expanded here.
