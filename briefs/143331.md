# Bank D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 40 "K", Pailin Provincial Court, 21 November 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Li Meng) · first instance · Doc ID 143331

## 1. Facts
On 28 March 2018 Mr C1 (born 1960) and his wife Ms C2 (born 1956) signed a loan contract with Bank D for 51,600 (fifty-one thousand six hundred) US dollars at 15.60 (fifteen point six zero) per cent a year for 60 months — interest monthly, principal in instalments of 8,000 (eight thousand) US dollars every twelve months from 05 April 2018 to 05 September 2022 — with a pledge agreement of the same date and automatic deduction from their account. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 19][Para 20]
The borrowers stopped paying interest and principal for several months, and the bank sued on 31 July 2019 for the whole principal, contractual interest and penalty interest from 05 July 2018, plus 5,000 (five thousand) US dollars for legal fees, lost time and mental distress. [Para 12][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 1]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a borrower couple who admit the debt in full but plead that they have no means to pay (ពុំ មាន លទ្ធភាព សង ប្រាក់) are liable for the entire accelerated principal, contractual interest and penalty interest at twice the contract rate? [Para 23][Para 24][Para 36][Para 39]
2. Whether provisional enforcement may be ordered over all heads of the claim, or only over principal and ordinary interest? [Para 10][Para 40]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Bank D, lending bank, represented by attorney Huot Sovann):** the defendants defaulted for many months and ignored repeated reminders and warnings, so the bank claims the 51,600 US dollars principal, 15.60% annual interest, penalty interest at twice the contractual rate, and 5,000 US dollars in service fees, lost time and mental distress, supported by the loan contract, the interest agreement and the pledge contract. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 16][Para 17][Para 18]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** they acknowledge the bank's claim "entirely" (ទទួល ស្គាល់ ទាំងស្រុង), contest nothing, and ask only that the bank restructure the outstanding principal and interest as fresh credit so that repayment becomes manageable. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 24]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** judgment for the bank. Both defendants are ordered jointly to pay 51,600 US dollars principal, interest at 15.60% a year from 05 July 2018 until full payment, and penalty interest from 05 July 2018 at twice the contractual rate; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal and the ordinary interest only; costs lie on the defendants. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40][Para 41]
The court found the complaint regular and admissible under Article 75 of the Code of Civil Procedure, then held that the loan contract, the interest agreement and the pledge contract proved the existence of the bank's right of claim against the borrowers, which they had admitted without reservation; it therefore accepted the claim and decided it as pleaded. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] Absent defendants duly notified were taken to admit the facts and judgment was given in absentia under Articles 95, 96 and 201. [Para 28][Para 29] The 5,000 US dollars of service fees, lost time and mental distress was reserved for consideration but no sum was awarded in the disposition. [Para 30][Para 37] The clause allowing the bank to sell the pledged property after 30 days was noted, and provisional enforcement was granted only for points (a) and (b) under Article 196. [Para 31][Para 35][Para 40]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where defendants admit a bank's loan claim in full and contest no fact, the court grants the contractual principal, the contractual interest and penalty interest at twice the contract rate as pleaded; an assertion that the defendants cannot afford to pay raises no issue of liability and does not reduce the debt. [Para 24][Para 26][Para 27][Para 39]

**Obiter dicta:** that a request for credit restructuring or rescheduling is a matter between the parties and not a ground of defence; and that provisional enforcement, though claimed over heads 1 to 3, is confined to principal and ordinary interest and withheld from the penalty interest. [Para 22][Para 35][Para 40]

[Para 30][Para 37] Party names are redacted to initials (ច១, ច២) and the bank to "ដ", and the attorney's name is garbled across paragraphs. [Para 1][Para 26]
