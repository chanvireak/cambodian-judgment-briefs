# Microfinance Institution D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 13 "K", Pailin Provincial Court, 23 May 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Li Meng) · first instance · Doc ID 143332

## 1. Facts
On 23 May 2017 Mr C1 (aged 35) and his wife Ms C2 (aged 36) applied to the Pailin branch of Microfinance Institution D for a loan of 15,500 (fifteen thousand five hundred) US dollars; on 31 May 2017 the institution approved it at 1.5 (one point five) per cent a month over 36 months, interest monthly and principal by an annexed repayment schedule. [Para 9][Para 10]
Nothing was paid from 09 February 2018. The institution sued on 19 October 2018 for the 15,500 US dollars principal, 1.5% monthly interest and 1 US dollar a day penalty from that date, 4,000,000 (four million) riel for mental distress, provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 13]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether interest of 1.5% a month is enforceable, given that Article 17 of the Law on Implementation of the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128 cap interest at 18% a year? [Para 29][Para 34]
2. Whether a daily penalty of 1 US dollar and a claim of 4,000,000 riel for mental distress survive the defendants' admission of the principal and their plea that the plaintiff reconsider those heads? [Para 21][Para 25][Para 35]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Mr T D, representative of the institution):** the written contract of 31 May 2017 fixes the principal at 15,500 US dollars and interest at 1.5% a month for 36 months, and the defendants stopped paying in February 2018, so the principal, the contractual interest, the 1 US dollar daily penalty and 4,000,000 riel for mental distress are all due. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15][Para 23]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrowers):** they admit outright that they owe the 15,500 US dollars principal, and ask only that the plaintiff be asked to reconsider the penalty and the mental-distress damages. [Para 20][Para 21] They did not attend the hearing despite summons served by the court. [Para 28]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** judgment for the institution. The defendants must pay 15,500 US dollars principal; interest at 1.5% a month on that principal from 09 February 2018 until payment in full; a penalty of 1 US dollar a day from 09 February 2018 until payment in full; and costs. The judgment is provisionally enforceable as to heads 1, 2 and 4 only — principal, interest and costs, not the penalty. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36][Para 37]
The court found the complaint of 19 October 2018 regular under Article 75 of the Code of Civil Procedure; read the loan contract of 31 May 2017 as proving the existence of the institution's right of claim; and recorded the defendants' admission of the principal both in the first preparatory hearing and in the second. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 26][Para 27] On the rate it applied Article 1 of Prakas No. 128 (21 December 2011) and Article 17 of the Law on Implementation of the Civil Code, which forbid interest above 18% a year, and Article 384 of the Civil Code requiring performance in good faith according to the contract — 1.5% a month being exactly 18% a year, the agreed rate stood. [Para 29][Para 30] Because the claim was a money claim, provisional enforcement was ordered, costs following Article 64. [Para 31][Para 32] The 4,000,000 riel mental-distress head was never ruled on. [Para 15][Para 33]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A contractual rate of 1.5% per month equals the 18% annual ceiling set by Article 17 of the Law on Implementation of the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128 and is therefore enforceable without reduction; a distinct daily penalty clause is separately enforceable, but provisional enforcement will be refused for it even where granted for principal, interest and costs. [Para 29][Para 34][Para 37]

**Obiter dicta:** that a debtor's plea for indulgence on penalty and damages is addressed to the creditor rather than raising a defence, and that admission of the principal in the preparatory minutes binds the admitting party. [Para 21][Para 27]
