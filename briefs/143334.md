# Ms D v Ms C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 826, Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 3 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (president: Ros Samdy) · first instance · Doc ID 143334

## 1. Facts
By a loan contract dated 11 May 2016 Ms C1 (born 1957) and Ms C2 (born 1984) borrowed 30,000 (thirty thousand) US dollars from Ms D for six months at 1.8 (one point eight) per cent a month, secured by a hypothec over a 68 m² house-and-land plot at village 21, Sangkat Srah Chak, Khan Daun Penh, registered under a title certificate of 24 October 2011. [Para 11][Para 13]
The principal fell due on 11 November 2016 and was never repaid; the debtors kept paying interest until 11 May 2017 and then stopped altogether. [Para 12][Para 14] Ms D claimed the principal, 1.8% monthly interest from 11 May 2017, damages and mental distress of 30% of the principal, compulsory provisional enforcement and costs. [Para 6][Para 7][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the agreed 1.8% monthly interest is recoverable in full, or only at the 1.5% monthly rate the court in fact awarded? [Para 7][Para 19]
2. Whether damages and mental distress of 30% of the principal may be awarded on top of that interest under Articles 400 and 401 of the Civil Code, and whether provisional enforcement should be ordered over the principal? [Para 8][Para 16][Para 21]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Ms D, lender, represented by attorney Seng Sovanna):** the debtors signed for 30,000 US dollars for six months at 1.8% a month and pledged their title as security; they never repaid the principal and stopped paying interest on 11 May 2017, so principal, interest, 30% damages and compulsory provisional enforcement are all claimed. [Para 13][Para 14]
**Defendants (Ms C1 and Ms C2, borrowers, represented by attorney Vith Sovannara):** they admit borrowing the 30,000 US dollars and offer to negotiate repayment of the principal, but ask the plaintiff to waive the 1.8% interest, the 30% damages, the provisional enforcement and the fees, because they are old, have no income, and have already paid interest for a period. [Para 15][Para 16]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed. Ms C1 and Ms C2 must pay Ms D 30,000 (thirty thousand) US dollars principal; interest at 1.5 (one point five) per cent a month on that principal from 11 May 2017 until full payment; and damages and mental distress of 30 (thirty) per cent of the principal; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to head 1, and the debtors bear the costs. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 20][Para 21][Para 22]
The court worked from the admission of the loan, the written contract of 11 May 2016, the hypothec registered on 06 May 2016 over the 68 m² plot, and the payment history it recorded: principal due 11 November 2016 and unpaid, interest paid only to 11 May 2017. [Para 11][Para 13][Para 14] On damages it held that an appropriate sum should be fixed under Articles 400 and 401 of the Civil Code; on provisional enforcement it applied Articles 196 and 197 of the Code of Civil Procedure; and costs followed Article 64 against the losing party. [Para 16][Para 17] The operative order then awarded interest at 1.5% a month rather than the 1.8% contracted, without saying why. [Para 19]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the contractual monthly rate exceeds the statutory ceiling, the court enforces the loan at 1.5% a month — the rate that equals 18% a year — from the date interest stopped, and may in addition award damages and mental distress of 30% of the principal under Articles 400 and 401 of the Civil Code, the two heads being cumulative rather than alternatives. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 16]

**Obiter dicta:** that inability to pay, old age and lack of income are pleas for indulgence addressed to the creditor and do not reduce the debt; and that a money judgment on a clear principal may be made provisionally enforceable under Articles 196 and 197 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 16][Para 21]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Interest is claimed at 1.8% a month and awarded at 1.5% a month with no reason given anywhere in the judgment, the reduction appearing only in the disposition. [Para 7][Para 19] The principal in the disposition is printed as "ចំ ដ ០០០ (បី ម៉ឺន)", the digits being lost, so the 30,000 figure is taken from the pleadings. [Para 18] The hypothec contract is dated 06 May 2016, five days before the loan contract of 11 May 2016 that it secures. [Para 11][Para 13] The debtors' argument is cut off mid-sentence ("បាន បង់ ការប្រាក់ ជូន ដើមចោទ បាន រយៈ") where the page-header disclaimer bleeds into the text, and the court's own reasoning on damages is scrambled at the same point ("៣០% ទីរ ពាក់ ដើម"). [Para 16] The page-header disclaimer recurs at [Para 11] and [Para 16]. [Para 11][Para 16] Party names are redacted to initials throughout and are not expanded here.
