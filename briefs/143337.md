# Ms D v Mr C (Civil Judgment No. 45 (3)/593, Banteay Meanchey Provincial Court, 8 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Khlok Piseth) · first instance · Doc ID 143337

## 1. Facts
On 22 February 2016 Mr C borrowed 57,000 baht (fifty-seven thousand baht) from Ms D, promising interest of 5,700 baht a month — 10% a month — recorded at the time only by a handwritten entry in a book. [Para 5][Para 11] The debt was restated in a written acknowledgment of indebtedness signed at the police station on 22 July 2017. [Para 11]
Mr C admits the 57,000 baht principal but says he paid interest for four months only and has paid nothing since. [Para 15] Ms D sued for the principal, 10% monthly interest running from the date he stopped paying, and 5,000,000 riel in moral damages (ជំងឺចិត្ត). [Para 3][Para 4]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a contractual rate of 10% per month — 120% a year — can be enforced, or must be cut to the statutory ceiling? [Para 22]
2. Whether the defendant's admission of the principal, together with the acknowledgment of debt of 22 July 2017, is sufficient proof to order repayment on the plaintiff's terms? [Para 18][Para 19]
3. Whether damages for delay lie where the debt itself is admitted and only the rate is contested? [Para 20][Para 29]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Ms D, creditor):** she advanced 57,000 baht on 22 February 2016 at 5,700 baht a month interest; the defendant paid four months and stopped, and signed a further acknowledgment at the police station on 22 July 2017; she claims principal, 10% monthly interest from default and 5,000,000 riel damages. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 13][Para 14]
**Defendant (Mr C, debtor):** he admits borrowing the 57,000 baht but rejects both the 10% monthly rate and the 5,000,000 riel damages; he asks to repay the principal at 3,000 baht a month and says he has no capacity beyond that. [Para 15]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed. Mr C is ordered to pay Ms D 57,000 baht principal, interest at 18% a year — 855 baht a month — from the date of non-payment until paid in full, and 4,000,000 riel in damages for delay; the claim for anything beyond that is rejected and costs fall on the defendant. Point 1 of the claim is declared provisionally enforceable. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 31]
The court held the acknowledgment of 22 July 2017 established the debt, and since the defendant conceded owing exactly the sum claimed, Article 400 of the Civil Code (decision on the established facts) allowed it to adjudicate without further proof. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 21] Turning to the rate, it cited Articles 578, 579, 583, 584 and 585 of the Civil Code and Prakas No. 128 K.Y.Prak/11 of 21 December 2011, which caps contractual interest at 18% a year; 10% a month being far above the cap, the court refused the contractual rate and substituted 18% a year, working out to 855 baht a month on 57,000 baht. [Para 22][Para 28] It then awarded 4,000,000 riel as compensation for delay and rejected the balance of the money claim, allocating costs to the losing defendant under Article 64 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 29][Para 23][Para 31]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a loan contract fixes interest above the ceiling set by Prakas No. 128 (18% per annum), the court will not enforce the stipulated rate: it awards interest at 18% per annum from the date of default until payment, and the excess is unenforceable. An admission of the principal by the debtor, corroborated by a later written acknowledgment of debt, is sufficient proof of the debt and permits judgment on the established facts under Article 400 of the Civil Code. [Para 19][Para 21][Para 22]

**Obiter dicta:** that the principal-order in point 1 may be enforced provisionally, and that an appeal must be lodged within one month of service of the judgment. [Para 25][Para 34]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The principal is printed as 57,000 baht in the claim and findings but as "៥,៧០០០" and "៥.៧០០០" in the reasoning and the operative order — a digit transposition in the OCR, not a different sum. [Para 18][Para 27] Ms D claimed 5,000,000 riel and the court records itself as accepting that claim as pleaded, yet the operative order awards 4,000,000 riel; both figures are reported. [Para 16][Para 29] The operative paragraph on damages is truncated, losing the plaintiff's name and merging a rejection clause into the same line. [Para 29] The defendant's name is rendered by a Chinese character (竹) at the interest order. [Para 28] The court orders the principal repaid "counted from 22 February 2016", the date of the loan rather than of default, while interest is ordered from the date of non-payment. [Para 27][Para 28]
