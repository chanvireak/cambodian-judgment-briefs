# Ms D v the defendant (name redacted) (Civil Judgment No. 249, Kampong Cham Provincial Court, 10 September 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Say Samphos Serey) · first instance · Doc ID 143338

## 1. Facts
In 2014 the defendant borrowed 13,650 USD (thirteen thousand six hundred and fifty dollars) from Ms D at 3% interest a month. [Para 7] The defendant repaid 2,000 USD, leaving 11,650 USD (eleven thousand six hundred and fifty dollars) of principal outstanding, and then stopped paying despite being pressed. [Para 8][Para 13]
The loan was recorded in a minute (កំណត់ហេតុ) of 23 June 2017, and a further minute of settlement was drawn before the commune chief on 23 July 2018. [Para 9][Para 14] Ms D sued for the principal, statutory interest and 8,000,000 riel in moral damages (ជម្ងឺចិត្ត), with provisional enforcement of the principal. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a debtor's admission of a smaller sum displaces the written record of the loan, where she produces no evidence of the further repayments she alleges? [Para 16][Para 19]
2. Whether the creditor is entitled to interest at 18% a year, and from which date it runs? [Para 20][Para 23]
3. What quantum of damages for mental distress follows from a debt that is admitted in part? [Para 20][Para 24]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Ms D, creditor):** she lent 13,650 USD at 3% a month in 2014 and has received back only 2,000 USD, so 11,650 USD remains; she expressly refuses the defendant's figure of 7,750 USD and holds to her statement of claim. [Para 7][Para 13]
**Defendant (debtor, name redacted):** she borrowed 13,650 USD but repaid 4,000 USD in all — 2,000 USD in 2017, then 1,000 USD, and 1,000 USD before 2012 — and a further 1,950 USD was lent to a third person for whom she stood guarantor; she admits owing 7,750 USD, says she cannot pay it, and has no receipts because payments were made hand to hand and twice by Wing. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 12]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** judgment for the plaintiff. The defendant is ordered to pay 11,650 USD principal, interest at 18% a year from 23 July 2017 until payment in full, and 1,000,000 riel in costs and damages for mental distress; costs of the action fall on the defendant. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 24][Para 25]
The court identified the single point of dispute as the amount of principal — 11,650 USD claimed against 7,750 USD admitted — and, on the pleadings and the oral hearing, found that the parties had indeed concluded a loan contract for 11,650 USD, evidenced by the minute of 23 June 2017. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 18] The defendant's assertion that only 7,750 USD remained was unsupported by any evidence capable of satisfying the court, so the written record prevailed and the claim as pleaded was upheld. [Para 18][Para 19] On that basis the court awarded the principal, added interest at 18% a year from 23 July 2017 under the contract of that date, and allowed 1,000,000 riel against the 8,000,000 riel claimed, charging costs to the defendant. [Para 20][Para 23][Para 24][Para 21]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the creditor produces a written record of the loan and the debtor admits the borrowing but asserts a lower balance on the strength of alleged repayments for which she has no receipt or other proof, the court will prefer the written record and give judgment for the sum it establishes; an unproved assertion of repayment does not reduce the debt. [Para 18][Para 19]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that a borrower's inability to pay is no answer to the debt, and that the judgment is reasoned in the presence of the parties and pronounced publicly. [Para 11][Para 25]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The minute evidencing the loan is dated 23 June 2017 in the findings and in the reasoning, but the operative order runs interest from 23 July 2017 and describes the contract as dated 23 July 2017; a separate minute of settlement before the commune chief is dated 23 July 2018. Three dates are therefore in play and none has been corrected. [Para 9][Para 14][Para 18][Para 23] The defendant says she repaid 1,000 USD "before 2012" although the loan is placed in 2014, so the repayment chronology as recorded cannot be reconciled. [Para 10] Ms D claimed 8,000,000 riel and was awarded 1,000,000 riel; the paragraph awarding it merges court fees and moral damages into a single figure without itemising them. [Para 4][Para 24] The defendant's name is lost in the OCR, appearing variously as nothing, "B", "D" and "0". [Para 1][Para 10][Para 15][Para 22]
