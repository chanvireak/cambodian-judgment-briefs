# Bank (name redacted) v Ms C1 and Mr C2 (Civil Judgment No. 24 (K), Kampong Thom Provincial Court, 21 August 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Luang Sor) · first instance · Doc ID 143339

## 1. Facts
On 7 May 2016 the bank lent the two defendants 25,000 USD (twenty-five thousand dollars) over 72 months at 14.4% a year, repayable in equal monthly instalments of principal and interest. [Para 15] On 23 June 2017 they signed a further loan of 31,900.07 USD, applied to discharge the 25,000 USD facility, again over 72 months but at 13.20% a year. [Para 16] Both loans were secured by mortgages (ហ៊ីប៉ូតែក) over immovable property certificates dated 13 March 2006, under mortgage deeds of 10 May 2016 and 23 June 2017. [Para 17]
The defendants paid 4,185.96 USD in principal and interest in total, then stopped on 10 August 2018, leaving 27,721.11 USD outstanding. [Para 18][Para 19] The bank sued for that principal, 13.20% annual interest from 12 July 2018, a 25% annual penalty from 10 August 2018, 3,000 USD in costs and provisional enforcement. [Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8][Para 9]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the court may give judgment for the whole claim where the defendants neither appear nor plead any defence? [Para 26][Para 27]
2. Whether the contractual interest and penalty rates may be awarded as claimed, and at what rates? [Para 75][Para 82][Para 83]
3. What sum is recoverable as legal, lawyers' and procedural costs? [Para 8][Para 84]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the bank):** the loans and mortgage deeds are in writing and on file; the defendants drew both facilities, repaid only 4,185.96 USD and defaulted from 10 August 2018, so the full balance, the agreed interest and the agreed penalty are due, together with 3,000 USD of costs. [Para 15][Para 18][Para 19][Para 25]
**Defendants (Ms C1 and Mr C2):** they entered no appearance and filed no defence; the file records only that they knew of and acknowledged the bank's interest and penalty rates but had no capacity to pay and asked the bank to waive its claim. [Para 26][Para 76]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** judgment for the bank. The defendants are ordered to pay 27,721.11 USD principal, interest at 13.20% a year from 12 July 2018, a penalty at 25% a year from 10 August 2018, all until payment in full, plus 1,000 USD in legal service, lawyers' and procedural costs; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal and costs fall on the losing party. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 83][Para 84][Para 85][Para 86]
The court recorded that every head of the claim was disputed but that the defendants were absent without excuse after service had been effected by posting at the court and at the commune hall, so it proceeded on the plaintiff's statements and documents. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] It found the two loan contracts and the mortgage deeds mutually consistent and consistent with the pleaded facts, and held the debt proved. [Para 30][Para 62][Para 79] On the rates it observed that the claimed rate — written as 13.80% in the reasoning against 13.20% in the claim — was below the 18% ceiling fixed by paragraph 1 of the Prakas on capped interest rates of 21 December 2011, and therefore awarded the claim as made. [Para 6][Para 75][Para 82] The penalty clause was enforced at its stated 25%, costs were assessed under the Code of Civil Procedure, and provisional enforcement was granted on the principal only. [Para 83][Para 80][Para 85]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A defendant who, after due service, neither appears nor adduces any evidence cannot put the plaintiff to proof of a documentary case: where written loan and mortgage contracts are consistent with the pleaded facts, the court will give judgment for the contractual balance and enforce the agreed interest and penalty clauses, subject only to the statutory ceiling on interest. [Para 26][Para 79][Para 75]

**Obiter dicta:** that a claimed rate below the 18% ceiling of the Prakas of 21 December 2011 calls for no reduction, and that provisional enforceability is confined to the principal. [Para 75][Para 85]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The interest rate is pleaded and ordered at 13.20% but appears as 13.80% in the reasoning that tests it against the 18% ceiling. [Para 6][Para 75][Para 82] One loan contract is dated 7 May 2016 in the narrative but 7 May 2018 in the list of documents examined. [Para 15][Para 30][Para 62] The figures do not reconcile: 25,000 USD plus the refinancing of 31,900.07 USD, less the 4,185.96 USD repaid, leaves 52,714.11 USD, not the 27,721.11 USD sued for and awarded. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 18][Para 19][Para 81] Interest at 13.20% and a penalty at 25% are claimed and awarded cumulatively — 38.20% a year — without the court addressing whether the total exceeds the 18% ceiling it had just cited. [Para 75][Para 82][Para 83] The defendants are recorded as absent from the hearing and yet also as having acknowledged the rates and pleaded inability to pay. [Para 26][Para 76] This file duplicates the whole judgment three times, with boilerplate ("this is a factual, not a model, judgment") spliced into the middle of sentences and paragraph numbers detached from their text. [Para 33][Para 42][Para 65][Para 73]
