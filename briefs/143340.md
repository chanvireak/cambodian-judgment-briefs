# Bank (name redacted) v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 09 K, Oddar Meanchey Provincial Court, 16 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Try Sophath) · first instance · Doc ID 143340

## 1. Facts
On 26 May 2018 the defendants, husband and wife, signed a loan contract with the plaintiff bank for 30,000 USD (thirty thousand dollars) at 13.2% a year over 60 months, repayable in equal monthly instalments of principal and interest from 26 June 2018 to 26 May 2023, each instalment to be debited automatically from their account under clause 1.2 of the contract. [Para 9][Para 12] The loan was secured by a pledge contract (កិច្ចសន្យា ដាក់បញ្ចាំ) of the same date and copies of both identity cards were filed. [Para 23][Para 24]
The defendants stopped paying interest and principal for many months and refused to come in to settle, so the bank sued on 25 March 2019 for 29,000 USD, contractual interest at 13.2%, penalty interest at twice the contractual rate, 5,000 USD of costs and damages, and provisional enforcement. [Para 5][Para 6][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10][Para 14]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the court may hear the case in the defendants' absence and treat their non-appearance as an admission of the facts alleged? [Para 29][Para 30]
2. Whether the penalty clause — interest at twice the contractual rate — may be enforced as claimed alongside the contractual rate itself? [Para 6][Para 33]
3. Whether provisional enforcement of the principal, interest and penalty heads is available? [Para 27][Para 36]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the bank, by its authorised representative Mr T D and counsel Kea Sakka):** the contract of 26 May 2018 is in writing, the defendants drew the money, defaulted for months and would not negotiate; they hold to the claim exactly as pleaded in the statement of claim of 25 March 2019, supporting it with the final debt summary of 11 February 2019 and the repayment schedule of 12 February 2019. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 21][Para 22][Para 25]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2):** none. They never entered an appearance or filed a defence, and service was effected at their address and by public posting after the occupants said the couple no longer lived there. [Para 11][Para 29]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** judgment for the bank. The defendants are ordered jointly to pay 29,000 USD principal, interest at 13.2% a year from 26 August 2018, penalty interest at twice the contractual rate from the same date, and 3,000,000 riel (three million riel) for legal service costs, lost time and moral damages, all until full payment; heads 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 are provisionally enforceable and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 35][Para 36]
The court found service properly effected — the summons delivered at the address, the recipients denying the couple lived there, and public posting completed — and held that under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure it could sit in the defendants' absence and treat them as having confessed (សារភាព) the facts alleged by the plaintiff. [Para 29][Para 30] It then examined the loan contract and the debt summary and found that the defendants had indeed borrowed from the bank and had genuinely failed to pay interest as required, so the claim was well founded in law. [Para 25][Para 26] Provisional enforcement was granted by reference to Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and costs were charged to the defendants under Article 64. [Para 27][Para 28]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where defendants have been served at their address and by public posting and still fail to appear without excuse, the court may proceed in their absence under Article 201 of the Code of Civil Procedure and treat the facts pleaded as admitted; a bank that proves the written loan contract and the arrears by its own statements is then entitled to judgment for the balance and the contractual interest and penalty. [Para 26][Para 30]

**Obiter dicta:** that provisional enforceability extends to the principal, the contractual interest and the penalty alike, and that costs follow the event under Article 64. [Para 28][Para 36]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The contract was for 30,000 USD but the sum sued for and awarded is 29,000 USD, and nothing on the file explains the reduction. [Para 9][Para 26][Para 31] Costs and moral damages were claimed at 5,000 USD and awarded as 3,000,000 riel, a change of currency the judgment does not explain. [Para 8][Para 35] The penalty is set at "twice the interest stated in the contract" — 26.4% a year — and is awarded cumulatively with the 13.2% contractual rate, giving 39.6% a year, with no reference to the statutory ceiling on interest. [Para 6][Para 32][Para 33] The defendants' names survive only as C1 and C2, and at the operative order as a blank. [Para 3][Para 31] Paragraph 34 is an orphaned fragment reading only "រៀល ។", and the reasoning at paragraph 29 is broken mid-sentence by inserted boilerplate ("this is a factual, not a model, judgment"), which appears six times in the text. [Para 29][Para 34]
