# Bank branch (Kampong Thom) v Mr C1, Ms C2 and the guarantors Mr C3 and Ms C4 (Civil Judgment No. 09 (K), Kampong Thom Provincial Court, 29 May 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Luang Sor) · first instance · Doc ID 143341

## 1. Facts
On 10 June 2017 the plaintiff bank's Kampong Thom branch lent the defendants 50,000 USD (fifty thousand dollars) over 84 months at 12% a year, repayable in monthly instalments from the start of each month, secured by a mortgage (ហ៊ីប៉ូតែក) over immovable property and by two guarantors (អ្នកធានាបំណុល), Mr C3 and Ms C4. [Para 18][Para 22] The defendants repaid 14,285.72 USD (fourteen thousand two hundred and eighty-five dollars and seventy-two cents) and then stopped, leaving 35,714.28 USD outstanding and unpaid since 1 June 2018. [Para 19][Para 23]
The bank sued for that principal, 12% annual interest from 2 June 2018, a 25% annual penalty from 1 June 2018, 3,500 USD of costs and provisional enforcement. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 10][Para 11][Para 12]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the court must give judgment on the admitted principal where the debtor pleads only inability to pay? [Para 24][Para 28]
2. Whether the contractual interest and the penalty may be awarded cumulatively as claimed, and by reference to what ceiling? [Para 40][Para 47][Para 48]
3. Whether the guarantors are liable jointly with the borrowers, and whether the principal head is provisionally enforceable? [Para 46][Para 50]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the bank, by Mr T D and counsel Eang Sosen):** the contract of 10 June 2017 is on file, the borrowers defaulted from 1 June 2018 after repaying 14,285.72 USD, and the bank has dunned them repeatedly without result; it maintains every head of the claim as pleaded. [Para 21][Para 31][Para 33][Para 38]
**Defendants (Mr C1, Ms C2 and guarantors Mr C3 and Ms C4):** they admit the principal of 35,714.28 USD and admit that they knew of and accepted the bank's interest and penalty rates, but say they have no means to pay at present, having spent their money on their business, and ask to repay the principal alone. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26][Para 41]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** judgment for the bank. Mr C1 and Ms C2, together with the guarantors Mr C3 and Mr C4, are ordered jointly to pay 35,714.28 USD principal, interest at 12% a year on that sum from 2 June 2018, a penalty at 25% a year on that sum from 1 June 2018, and 4,000,000 riel (four million riel) for legal service, lawyers' fees, lost time and other procedural costs; the principal is provisionally enforceable and costs fall on the losing party. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51]
The court separated the admitted from the contested: the principal being acknowledged, there was no dispute on it, and the only issues were interest, penalty, costs and provisional enforcement. [Para 28][Para 29] It found the loan contract consistent with the facts pleaded and the arrears, and treated the defendants' plea of impecuniosity as no answer, since they had admitted knowing and accepting the bank's rates. [Para 41][Para 44] On the rates it held that the claimed 12% interest and 25% penalty were below the ceiling of 18% a year fixed by paragraph 1 of the Prakas on capped interest rates of 21 December 2011, and therefore awarded them as claimed. [Para 40][Para 47][Para 48] Provisional enforcement was confined to the principal, and costs were left to the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 43][Para 45][Para 50]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An admission of the principal debt removes it from dispute, and a debtor's plea that he has no money to pay is not a defence: the court will give judgment for the admitted principal together with the contractual interest and penalty, provided the rate claimed does not exceed the statutory ceiling on interest. [Para 28][Para 41][Para 40]

**Obiter dicta:** that provisional enforcement is available on the principal alone, and that a guarantor who signed the security is jointly liable with the borrowers for the whole. [Para 46][Para 50]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The court states that both the 12% interest and the 25% penalty are below the 18% ceiling of the Prakas of 21 December 2011; the penalty alone exceeds that ceiling, and the two together come to 37% a year. Both are nonetheless awarded. [Para 40][Para 47][Para 48] The monthly instalment is printed as 7,142.86 USD, which over 84 months would far exceed the 50,000 USD lent — a decimal error in the OCR. [Para 18][Para 22] The guarantors are recorded as having signed on 10 June 2015, two years before the loan they guarantee, which is dated 10 June 2017. [Para 18][Para 22] Costs were claimed at 3,500 USD, written in words at one point as "two thousand", and awarded as 4,000,000 riel. [Para 11][Para 42][Para 49] Paragraph 7 garbles the parties' particulars, detaching birth years from names, and the boilerplate ("this is a factual, not a model, judgment") is spliced into the middle of sentences at least five times. [Para 7][Para 10][Para 21][Para 34][Para 48]
