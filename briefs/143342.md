# Bank branch (Siem Reap) v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 25 K, Siem Reap Provincial Court, 27 June 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Sin Sovannarath) · first instance · Doc ID 143342

## 1. Facts
The plaintiff bank advanced the defendants a further facility of 100,000 USD (one hundred thousand dollars), secured by mortgages (ហ៊ីប៉ូតែក) dated 9 March 2017, 18 July 2017 and 20 March 2018, with the loan contract itself dated 16 March 2018. [Para 9][Para 37] The defendants repaid only 7,550.75 USD of principal and stopped paying from 6 March 2019. [Para 9][Para 10]
They paid a further 5,733.30 USD between the filing of the claim and 13 June 2019 — 2,046.24 USD of principal and 3,687.06 USD of interest, covering 122 days — leaving 92,449.25 USD, and the bank amended its claim accordingly. [Para 12][Para 20][Para 21][Para 35] It claimed that principal, 11.50% annual interest from 9 June 2019, a 25% annual penalty, 9,500 USD of costs and provisional enforcement. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 24][Para 25]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the defendants breached the loan contract by failing to pay instalments when due? [Para 15][Para 39]
2. Whether the 11.50% interest and the 25% penalty are lawful and recoverable as agreed? [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52]
3. Whether the costs claimed and provisional enforcement should be granted? [Para 54][Para 56][Para 57]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the bank, by Mr K D and counsel Eang Sor Sen):** the defendants defaulted on the schedule, a serious breach causing loss; after the claim was filed they paid 5,733.30 USD, which the bank credited, and it maintains the amended claim for 92,449.25 USD with interest, penalty and 9,500 USD of costs. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 35]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, borrower and mortgagor):** they admit owing 92,449.25 USD and admit the interest claim, but say they cannot pay in full because their business has failed; they have resumed paying and ask to continue on the ordinary schedule and to pay off everything if they can sell the house, and ask the court to waive the penalty, the costs and provisional enforcement. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30][Para 31]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** judgment for the bank. The defendants are ordered to pay 92,449.25 USD principal, interest at 11.50% a year on that sum from 9 June 2019, a penalty at 25% a year on that sum from 6 March 2019, and 2,000,000 riel (two million riel) for legal service, lost time and procedural costs; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal, and costs and taxes fall on the defendants. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 64]
The court applied Article 384 of the Civil Code — the debtor must perform in accordance with the contract and in good faith — and, on the defendants' admission and the contracts and mortgage deeds, found the breach proved and requiring no further inquiry. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 48] It upheld the interest because 11.50% sits within the rate permitted by Prakas No. 128 K.Y.Prak/11 of 21 December 2011 and Articles 583, 586 and 591 of the Civil Code. [Para 50][Para 51] The penalty was enforced as a distinct contractual entitlement under clause 6 and Article 16 of Annex A, by which the defendants had agreed to 25% a year on the outstanding balance in addition to interest. [Para 43][Para 45][Para 52] The court reduced the costs claim of 9,500 USD to 2,000,000 riel on the actual expenses shown, granted provisional enforcement under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure as the claim concerned rights over property, and charged costs under Article 64. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57][Para 58]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A debtor who admits the outstanding balance and the contractual rate is liable for both, and his inability to pay is no defence; interest is upheld where it falls within the ceiling fixed by Prakas No. 128, and a penalty separately agreed as an addition to interest is enforced as a distinct contractual obligation. [Para 45][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52]

**Obiter dicta:** that a claim for procedural costs is assessed on the expenses actually incurred rather than as claimed, and that provisional enforcement follows where the claim concerns rights over property. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The operative order prints the principal as "USD 92.459,25" where the pleading, the findings and the reasoning all give 92,449.25 USD. [Para 21][Para 28][Para 59] Interest is claimed in the original pleading from 7 February 2019 but in the amended claim and the order from 9 June 2019, while the 5,733.30 USD payment credited runs to 13 June 2019, so the periods overlap. [Para 6][Para 22][Para 35] Costs were claimed at 9,500 USD and awarded as 2,000,000 riel without any stated conversion. [Para 24][Para 62] The court finds the 11.50% interest compliant with the 18% ceiling and then awards a further 25% penalty, a combined 36.5% a year, without addressing the total. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 61] The defendants' names survive only as C1 and C2, and in the heading as Chinese characters (むち). [Para 4][Para 28]
