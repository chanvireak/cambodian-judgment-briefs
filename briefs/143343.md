# Lending institution (name redacted) v Ms C (name redacted), Ms C2 and the guarantors C3 and T4 (Civil Judgment No. 17 (K), Kampong Thom Provincial Court, 31 July 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (Luang Sor) · first instance · Doc ID 143343

## 1. Facts
On 14 September 2016 the plaintiff lending institution (គ្រឹះស្ថាន) contracted with the defendants for a loan of 45,000 USD carrying interest at 1.2% a month and a penalty of 28.8% a year. [Para 12] Part was repaid, leaving 27,000 USD outstanding, and the defendants defaulted on interest from September 2018 after the institution had allowed them several months' grace. [Para 13][Para 17]
The institution sued for the 27,000 USD balance, interest at 1.2% a month and the 28.8% penalty, both from September 2018, 8,000 USD of legal and procedural costs, and provisional enforcement. [Para 6][Para 7][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the defendant's admission of the principal and interest entitles the court to give judgment for them without further proof? [Para 18][Para 23]
2. Whether a penalty of 28.8% a year may be awarded, and by reference to what ceiling? [Para 33][Para 41]
3. Whether the claim for 8,000 USD of costs should be allowed in full, and whether the principal is provisionally enforceable? [Para 35][Para 36]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (the institution, by Mr K D and counsel Srey Srey Sophan Nara):** the contract of 14 September 2016 is on file, the balance and the two rates are uncontradicted, the defendants were given months of leeway and still did not pay, and every head of the claim should be granted. [Para 15][Para 28][Para 32]
**Defendants (Ms C, Ms C2 and guarantors C3 and T4):** they admit owing the 27,000 USD principal and the 1.2% monthly interest, but ask the court to strike out the penalty, the 8,000 USD of costs and provisional enforcement because they have no capacity to pay, offering the principal alone. [Para 18][Para 20][Para 31]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** judgment for the institution. The defendants and the two guarantors are ordered to pay 27,000 USD principal, interest at 1.2% a month and a penalty at 28.8% a year, both running from September 2018 until payment in full, and 1,000 USD of legal, lawyers' and procedural costs; the claims beyond that are dismissed, the principal is provisionally enforceable and costs fall on the losing party. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44]
The court found the loan contract of 14 September 2016 consistent with the facts pleaded and, the principal and interest being admitted, held that no further evidence was needed on them. [Para 37][Para 38] The defendants' plea of impecuniosity did not defeat the claim, since they had acknowledged knowing and accepting the institution's rates. [Para 34] On the rates the court held that the 1.2% monthly interest and the penalty — written as 25% a year in this passage though claimed and ordered at 28.8% — were below the 18% ceiling fixed by paragraph 1 of the Prakas on capped interest rates of 21 December 2011, and awarded the claim as made. [Para 33][Para 41] Costs were assessed on the actual loss shown rather than as claimed, reducing 8,000 USD to 1,000 USD, with the residue of the claim dismissed. [Para 35][Para 42]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the debtor admits the principal and the contractual interest, the court may give judgment for them on the contract alone, and a plea of inability to pay does not reduce the debt; a claim for procedural costs, however, is assessed on the loss actually proved and not on the sum pleaded. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 38]

**Obiter dicta:** that provisional enforcement is confined to the principal, and that a guarantor who signed the security is liable jointly with the borrowers. [Para 39][Para 43]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The judgment states that the 1.2% monthly interest and the penalty are below the 18% ceiling of the Prakas of 21 December 2011; 1.2% a month is 14.4% a year but the penalty is 28.8%, well above the ceiling, and the two together come to 43.2%. The same passage calls the penalty 25% where the claim and the order say 28.8%. [Para 8][Para 33][Para 41] The outstanding balance is printed as 2,700 USD in the narrative (three times) and as 27,000 USD in the claim and the order; the words accompanying the figure read "twenty-seven thousand", so a digit has been lost. [Para 12][Para 16][Para 23][Para 39] Costs are pleaded at 8,000 USD while the parenthetical words say "two thousand five hundred". [Para 9][Para 15] The record is internally inconsistent on whether interest was admitted: the list of disputed points says the defendants denied interest, penalty and costs, while the findings say they admitted the first two. [Para 14][Para 18][Para 23] The defendants' names are blank in the heading and the operative order, and paragraph 22 contains stray non-Khmer characters. [Para 5][Para 22][Para 39]
