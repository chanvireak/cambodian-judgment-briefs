# Bank D v Mr C1 and Ms C2 (Civil Judgment No. 58 K1, Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 17 January 2020)
Bench: 1 judge (Ham Meng Se, Deputy President) · first instance · Doc ID 143346

## 1. Facts
On 10 August 2016 the defendant spouses applied for, and on 24 August 2016 the plaintiff bank advanced, 85,000 USD (eighty-five thousand US dollars) over 96 months at 11% a year, secured by a mortgage over their property. [Para 10][Para 31] They paid three instalments and then stopped; from 05 August 2017 they were in default, ignored three written reminders and a formal demand of 16 January 2019. [Para 11][Para 33][Para 36] Total repayments were 16,022.36 USD (sixteen thousand and twenty-two and thirty-six cents US dollars), leaving 77,980.46 USD of principal, which the defendants admit. [Para 39][Para 24]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the bank's claim is legally well founded where the debtors admit the outstanding principal and the contractual interest, but plead incapacity to pay the penalties, taxes and damages? [Para 28][Para 44]
2. Whether the court must enforce the full contractual penalty interest of 36% a year, and the 10,000 USD claimed for damages and lawyer's fees, as pleaded? [Para 42][Para 43]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Bank D, creditor):** the loan contract, mortgage deed and repayment schedule prove the 85,000 USD advance; the debtors defaulted in August 2017 and ignored every reminder, so it claims 77,980.46 USD principal, 30,349.53 USD in interest and penalty to 05 November 2019, 11% a year thereafter, penalty interest of 36% a year, taxes, 10,000 USD for damage and lawyer's fees, and provisional enforcement. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 9]
**Defendants (Mr C1 and Ms C2, debtors):** they admit borrowing 85,000 USD over 96 months, admit the 77,980.46 USD balance and the interest in the schedule, but say they cannot pay the penalties, taxes or moral damages and ask to resume payment by instalments under the bank's schedule. [Para 22][Para 24][Para 26][Para 27]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed — the defendants must pay 77,980.46 USD (seventy-seven thousand nine hundred and eighty and forty-six cents US dollars) in principal, 30,349.53 USD in interest and penalty to 05 November 2019, interest of 11% a year on the principal from 06 November 2019, penalty interest of 36% a year from 06 November 2019, the filing and other taxes, and 1,000 USD (one thousand US dollars) for damage and lawyer's fees; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal, and costs fall on the defendants. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51][Para 53][Para 54]
The court first upheld the pleading as regular and admitted the documentary evidence — loan application, mortgage registration, credit letter, loan and mortgage contracts and the repayment schedule. [Para 37][Para 28] Because the debtors admitted both the 77,980.46 USD principal and the interest shown in the schedule, it gave effect to the parties' agreement on those heads. [Para 39][Para 44] It then tested the contractual rate against Prakas No. 128 Kay.Prak/11 of 21 December 2011 of the Ministry of Justice, which sets an 18% annual ceiling: at 11% the contract rate was below it, so the 16,594.53 USD of unpaid interest from 05 September 2017 to 05 November 2019 was awarded. [Para 40] The contractual penalty of 13,755.44 USD for the same period was enforced as agreed, and the 36% annual penalty interest running from 06 November 2019 was held to have a proper basis under Article 2 of section 2.7 of the contract. [Para 41][Para 42] The plea of incapacity to pay the penalties, taxes and damages was rejected as unfounded, and the claim for damage and lawyer's fees was held well founded — though the sum awarded was 1,000 USD, not the 10,000 USD claimed. [Para 45][Para 43][Para 53] Provisional enforcement followed, and costs were placed on the defendants. [Para 54][Para 47]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The 18% annual ceiling on credit interest fixes only the lawful rate of interest on the loan; where the contract is admitted, a separately stipulated penalty rate — including penalty interest higher than that ceiling — is enforced as the parties' agreement, and a debtor's inability to pay is no defence to it. [Para 40][Para 42][Para 45]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that the case raised no complexity and could be tried by a single judge, and that provisional enforcement attaches to the admitted principal. [Para 46][Para 54]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The claim and the disposition both state interest plus penalty as 30,349.53 USD, but the components given in the reasoning — 16,594.53 USD of interest and 13,755.44 USD of penalty — add to 30,349.97 USD; the figures are reported as printed. [Para 4][Para 40][Para 41] The claim for damage and lawyer's fees is 10,000 USD in the pleading and reasoning but only 1,000 USD in the disposition. [Para 8][Para 43][Para 53] Default is said to begin on 05 August 2017 while unpaid interest is computed from 05 September 2017. [Para 11][Para 40] The bank's name survives only as "Bank D", the loan-letter and reminder numbers are blank in the OCR, and the defendants appear only as C1 and C2. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 10] The judgment is tried by a single judge although the bench line records a Deputy President. [Para 1][Para 46]
