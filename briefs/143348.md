# Specialised Bank D v Ms C1, Mr C2 and Ms C3 (Civil Judgment No. 38 K, Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 15 January 2020)
Bench: 1 judge (Chhnang Bunnarith) · first instance · Doc ID 143348

## 1. Facts
Under a base credit agreement of 11 August 2017 and an amended agreement of 08 April 2019, the three co-defendants increased an existing 12,948 USD facility by 7,052 USD to a total of 20,000 USD (twenty thousand US dollars), at 1.3% (one point three per cent) interest a month and 24% (twenty-four per cent) penalty a year on outstanding principal, secured by a hypothec over their land and house. [Para 9][Para 16] They paid five months from 16 May to 13 September 2019 — 302 USD of principal and 1,363 USD of interest — leaving 19,698 USD of principal, and then stopped altogether and avoided the bank's reminders. [Para 12][Para 18]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether the court must give effect to the parties' agreement where the debtors admit the principal, the 1.3% monthly interest and the 24% annual penalty, leaving no issue in dispute? [Para 22][Para 27]
2. Whether the 24% annual penalty and the 4,000 USD claim for mental suffering and lost time are lawful and made out? [Para 30][Para 32]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (Specialised Bank D, creditor):** on the base and amended credit agreements and a repayment schedule it claims 19,698 USD of principal, 1.3% monthly interest from 13 September 2019, the 24% annual penalty, 4,000 USD for mental suffering and lost time, provisional enforcement of the principal, and costs. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7]
**Defendants (Ms C1, Mr C2 and Ms C3, debtors):** they admit the debt, admit signing the amended credit agreement with the bank, admit the principal of 19,698 USD, and expressly accept both the 1.3% monthly interest and the 24% annual penalty; they ask only to be excused the 4,000 USD because they are currently unable to pay, and leave provisional enforcement to the court. [Para 19][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28][Para 21]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed — the co-defendants must pay 19,698 USD (nineteen thousand six hundred and ninety-eight US dollars) in principal, interest of 1.3% a month on that sum from 13 September 2019 until the principal is fully repaid, penalty of 24% a year from 14 October 2019 until repayment, and 4,000,000 riel (four million riel) for damage, lawyer's fees and other expenses; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal, and costs fall on the co-defendants. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 38][Para 39]
The court recorded at the outset that the case disclosed no disputed point, and went directly to the evidence. [Para 22] On the base and amended agreements plus the parties' own statements it found that the co-defendants had borrowed the sum and that the bank had actually delivered it, applying Articles 579 and 587 of the Civil Code. [Para 23][Para 24] It found the repayment date long past and the debt unpaid, and took the debtors' admission of the 19,698 USD principal, of the 1.3% monthly interest and of the 24% annual penalty as settling those heads. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] The debtors' plea to be relieved of the 4,000 USD because of incapacity was rejected for want of any evidence in support. [Para 29] The claim for damage and mental suffering was held to have an appropriate character and assessed by the court, which awarded 4,000,000 riel rather than the 4,000 USD claimed. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 38] The 24% penalty was found consistent with Article 5 of National Bank of Cambodia Prakas No. B7-017-109 Prokor of 13 March 2017 and Article 2 of the credit letter's further conditions. [Para 32] Provisional enforcement of the admitted principal followed under Article 196 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and costs under Article 64. [Para 34][Para 35]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the debtor admits the principal, the contractual interest and the stipulated penalty, and the penalty rate is consistent with the National Bank of Cambodia's praksas, the court gives effect to the agreement without further inquiry; but a claim for non-pecuniary damage and lost time, being unsupported by proof, is assessed by the court at what it considers appropriate rather than at the figure pleaded. [Para 27][Para 30][Para 32]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that incapacity to pay is no answer where no evidence of it is produced, and that provisional enforcement is available on an admitted principal under Article 196. [Para 29][Para 34]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The reasoning states that the co-defendants borrowed 19,698 USD, whereas the amended agreement raised the facility to 20,000 USD and 19,698 USD is the balance after repayment; both figures are reported as printed. [Para 9][Para 23] The penalty is claimed from 14 October 2019 but the narrative twice puts the default at 14 November 2019, and interest runs from 13 September 2019. [Para 12][Para 18][Para 37] The claim for mental suffering and lost time is 4,000 USD in the pleading but 4,000,000 riel in the disposition. [Para 6][Para 38] The second co-defendant's sex and year of birth are garbled in the party heading, the third is recorded both as born in 1988 and 1992, and the bank and debtors survive only as initials and numbers. [Para 2][Para 36] The recital that this is a factual and not a model judgment is spliced into the text at six points, and the signature block date line is partly illegible. [Para 43]
