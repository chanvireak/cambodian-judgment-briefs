# Microfinance institution D v Ms 01, Mr C2, Ms C3 and Mr 04 (Civil Judgment No. 117 K, Phnom Penh Municipal Court, 30 January 2020)
Bench: 1 judge (Sam Bun Por) · first instance · Doc ID 143350

## 1. Facts
Under a loan contract of 25 June 2018 the co-defendants borrowed 20,000 USD (twenty thousand US dollars) at 1.15% a month, repayable to 28 June 2024, with a 2% monthly penalty after final maturity; a second advance of 2,000 USD was taken at 1.4% a month, repayable to 02 August 2021. [Para 12][Para 14][Para 15] They repaid 483.34 USD of the first loan and 374.85 USD of the second, leaving 19,516.66 USD and 1,625.15 USD — 21,141.81 USD in all — then fell into repeated arrears and stopped paying. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 11] They were summoned at every stage and never appeared. [Para 19][Para 44]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether contractual interest may be awarded to run until actual payment, or only up to the date the claim was filed? [Para 32][Para 33]
2. Whether the 2% monthly penalty, the early-settlement service fees and the 6,000 USD claim for lost time are recoverable? [Para 34][Para 39][Para 40]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (microfinance institution D, creditor):** four co-borrowers — the daughter, her husband, and her parents — signed for the two loans; they fell into arrears repeatedly and stopped paying, so it claims 21,141.81 USD of principal, the contractual interest running until payment, 2% monthly penalties after maturity, early-settlement service fees of 585.49 USD and 48.75 USD, late-payment interest of 1,987.59 USD and 7.55 USD, 6,000 USD for lost time, costs, and provisional enforcement of the principal. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 9][Para 11][Para 17]
**Defendants (Ms 01, Mr C2, Ms C3 and Mr 04, co-borrowers):** no defence was advanced — they were duly summoned for the preparatory hearing of 29 November 2019 and stayed away without reason, so they are taken to have admitted the plaintiff's statements of claim. [Para 19][Para 44]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the claim is allowed in part — the co-defendants must jointly pay 21,141.81 USD (twenty-one thousand one hundred and forty-one US dollars and eighty-one cents) in principal, interest at 1.15% and 1.4% a month on the two balances from 23 August 2019 up to the date the claim was filed, 27 August 2019, and late-payment interest of 1,987.59 USD and 7.55 USD; the lost-time claim, the early-settlement service fees and the penalty claim are dismissed; the judgment is provisionally enforceable as to the principal, and costs fall on the co-defendants. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 54]
The court verified the two loan contracts and found the co-defendants had signed for 20,000 USD and 2,000 USD respectively, and that the principal balances claimed matched the evidence. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30][Para 31] It then departed from the pleading on interest: an award running from 23 August 2019 until payment was held unreasonable, and the court confined interest to the period from default to the filing of the claim on 27 August 2019, at the agreed rates. [Para 32][Para 33] The 2% monthly penalty was refused outright as a claim of a complicated and unclear character capable of seriously prejudicing the co-defendants. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36] The late-payment interest, by contrast, was supported by the evidence and was granted as claimed. [Para 37][Para 38] Construing clause 2.2 of the 25 June 2018 contract, the court held the 3% early-settlement fee payable only where the borrower repays ahead of schedule — and since the co-defendants never sought early repayment, the 585.49 USD and 48.75 USD claims failed. [Para 39][Para 51] The 6,000 USD lost-time claim was rejected because the creditor already profited from the monthly interest. [Para 40][Para 41] Provisional enforcement of the principal followed under Article 196, the absence was dealt with under Article 201, and costs under Article 64. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A creditor's contractual interest is confined to the period of default up to the filing of the claim, an open-ended award running until payment being unreasonable; a service fee that the contract makes payable only on early repayment is not due where the borrower never sought early repayment; and a penalty clause whose basis is unclear and disproportionately prejudicial will not be enforced. [Para 33][Para 39][Para 36]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that a creditor who already earns monthly interest cannot also recover a large sum for lost time, and that provisional enforcement attaches to a principal fixed by written contracts. [Para 40][Para 42]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The penalty is said to run from 28 June 2014 in one place and 28 June 2015 in another, while the maturity date given is 28 June 2024. [Para 14][Para 34] The second loan's penalty is computed on 1,643.69 USD, not the 1,625.15 USD balance. [Para 6][Para 34] The signatories of the two contracts vary across paragraphs — 01, C4 and C3 in one, "19", C2 and C4 in another — and the plaintiff's representative is named as Mr Kong in one place and Mr T D in another. [Para 28][Para 29] The second loan's contract is dated 30 October 2018 in the evidence but is described as taken after the June contract, and its contract number is blank. [Para 17][Para 22][Para 23] Party names are reduced to numbers and initials, several rendered as stray glyphs, and the disclaimer that this is a factual and not a model judgment is spliced into the text repeatedly. [Para 44][Para 47]
