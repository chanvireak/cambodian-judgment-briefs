# Mr A v Ms B (Civil Judgment No. 427; order sheet No. 40 ក្រVII(ធ), Phnom Penh Court of Appeal, 18 November 2019)
Bench: 3 judges (president: Thou Mony) · appeal · Doc ID 143351

## 1. Facts
On 09 March 2017 the two parties pooled capital (ចូលហ៊ុន) to buy a 21m × 28m plot in Phnom Penh and build five row houses numbered 22 to 26, each contributing 35,000 USD. [Para 8] They agreed the plaintiff would take house 24 and the defendant house 26, that houses 22, 23 and 25 would be sold to fund the construction, and each later withdrew 10,000 USD of profit. [Para 9][Para 13]
Construction was 100% complete and buyers had paid 90%, but a person recorded as "Ling" refused to transfer title to the buyers, so the balances were withheld. [Para 12] On the plaintiff's accounting the venture was 30,542.43 USD short, each venturer owing 15,271.21 USD, and with transfer fees and an over-drawn cash advance the defendant owed 20,203.41 USD. [Para 20]
The Phnom Penh Court of First Instance (Civil Judgment No. 609"K1", 21 May 2019) ordered the defendant to pay 11,104.74 USD, to carry out the transfer of house 24, and to pay 3,000 USD in damages and legal fees, with costs against her. [Para 20][Para 24][Para 25]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a civil appeal must be stayed because a related criminal complaint is pending between the same parties? [Para 43]
2. Whether a venturer who claims construction costs actually incurred can recover more than the court-appointed expert valued those costs at? [Para 34][Para 35]

## 3. Arguments
**Appellant (co-plaintiff, joint venturer):** he advanced the whole of the construction money — over 230,000 USD for the five houses — while the defendant paid nothing yet received a house and withdrew over 10,000 USD; the expert's estimate undercut his real expenditure by slightly more than 10,000 USD, so the 11,104.74 USD awarded should be raised to the 20,203.41 USD claimed, together with 50,000 USD in damages and legal costs because the first-instance award was too low. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 36][Para 37]
**Appellant (co-defendant):** she sought an adjournment of the oral argument in order to deal with criminal case No. 4807 of 13 August 2018, in which she was the complainant against a person connected with this civil file, and maintained that she had always been the one advancing construction money, which is why she refused to pay further sums. [Para 16][Para 39][Para 43]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** both appeals are dismissed, the first-instance judgment No. 609"K1" of 21 May 2019 is upheld as it stands, and costs are borne by each party. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 50][Para 51]
The Appeal Court took jurisdiction under Article 264 of the Code of Civil Procedure and confined itself to points 2 and 3 of the judgment below, the only points appealed. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42] It refused the adjournment: a pending criminal complaint is not the kind of urgent, compelling matter that requires a civil case to be suspended, and the defendant had already obtained one postponement. [Para 43][Para 44] On quantum it accepted that the plaintiff had spent more than 230,000 USD and that the expert's valuation came in about 10,000 USD lower, but held the expert's figure to be the proper measure — which is why the court below awarded just over 10,000 USD rather than the 20,000 USD odd claimed. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36] Finding the judgment below correct, it upheld it in full. [Para 45]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where co-venturers dispute what one of them is owed for construction, the court adopts the valuation of the expert it appointed as the measure of cost, and a party's assertion of higher actual expenditure does not displace it; and a pending criminal complaint between the same parties does not oblige an appellate court to suspend the civil appeal, suspension being reserved for urgent and compelling grounds. [Para 35][Para 43]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that the Appeal Court's jurisdiction rests on Article 264 of the Code of Civil Procedure and that it reviews only the grounds actually appealed. [Para 40][Para 42]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

[Para 20][Para 21][Para 22] The same sum appears as 11,104.7 and as 11,104.74. [Para 21][Para 22] The file number is 427 in the heading but 426 at para 38, and the judgment below is said to have closed oral argument on 21 March 2019 although it is dated and pronounced 21 May 2019. [Para 1][Para 26][Para 38] Party names survive only as initials and bare digits, one name appears in Latin script ("Ling"), and the costs order reads "គួភាគី" for "គូភាគី" (each party); no name has been expanded.
