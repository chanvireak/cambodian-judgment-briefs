# X v Y (Divorce Judgment, Battambang Provincial Court, 12 August 2020)
Bench: 1 judge (name redacted, "J") · first instance · Doc ID 160224

## 1. Facts
The parties married and registered on 03 August 2015 and lived at Y's home in Phnom Penh, where X had no relatives; they have a son, (name redacted), born 11 August 2016, and a daughter, (name redacted), born 22 July 2018. [Para 32][Para 40][Para 41] On 04 January 2019 (year truncated) X took both children back to her native Battambang, where they have remained with her and her parents. [Para 42][Para 92]
At the second preparatory hearing both said they no longer wished to continue the marriage. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 114] X claimed divorce, sole parental authority, 150 USD a month per child, 100 USD a month in alimony until she remarried, and division of the joint property — two plots, a car, a motorcycle and savings. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 18][Para 21][Para 61]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether parental authority over both children should be granted to the mother who has cared for them throughout, rather than to the father whose work keeps him out until late? [Para 103]
2. Whether funds contributed from a spouse's separate pre-marital property must be restored to that spouse before the jointly acquired assets are divided equally? [Para 135][Para 144]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (X, wife):** she alone has raised both children since birth and since the separation, with no cooperation from Y, who returns home late and never helped with their care; the marriage is broken beyond repair and not through her fault, so she should hold parental authority, receive 150 USD a month per child, alimony of 100 USD a month until she remarries, and one half of the joint property. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 61][Para 66]
**Defendant (Y, husband):** he accepts the divorce but rejects maintenance and alimony, offers at most 100 USD a month per child because Battambang is cheaper than Phnom Penh, and says the first plot, the car and the savings were his before the marriage and remain his; he earns about 7,000 USD a year. [Para 64][Para 69][Para 76][Para 80][Para 105]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** the marriage is dissolved; X is granted parental authority over both children; Y must pay 100 USD a month for each child from the date the judgment becomes final until each reaches majority; the claim for alimony is dismissed; the two plots are held in undivided halves, the car and the savings go to Y and the motorcycle to X, and Y must pay X 1,462.5 USD to equalise the shares; costs are borne half by each. [Para 161][Para 162][Para 163][Para 164][Para 165][Para 171][Para 172][Para 173]
Divorce followed from the breakdown found under Article 978(1)(g) of the Civil Code, both parties having stated they would not continue. [Para 49][Para 116] On custody the court applied the best-interests standard of Article 1037(3) and found X had managed both children continuously, including after the separation, while Y's late returns meant he had not shown how he would in practice care for them; his allegation that X was hot-tempered was accepted only as far as ordinary quarrels and not as affecting the children. [Para 97][Para 100][Para 101][Para 102][Para 103] On maintenance it took Y's income of about 7,000 USD a year, estimated the children's cost in Battambang at 80% of the Phnom Penh figure — about 200 USD a month for both — and fixed 100 USD per child. [Para 105][Para 112][Para 113] Alimony was refused because the breakdown was not shown to be Y's fault alone. [Para 117][Para 118] On property, everything acquired during the marriage is joint under Article 973, but where separate funds were put in, that share is returned first: 20% (1,200 USD) of plot L1, 10% (1,000 USD) of plot L2 each, 60% (6,000 USD) of the car and 75% (1,125 USD) of the motorcycle, leaving 17,675 USD to divide equally at 8,837.5 USD each. [Para 129][Para 135][Para 136][Para 138][Para 141][Para 143][Para 144][Para 145] Because the actual allocation gave Y 10,300 USD and X 7,375 USD, Y pays the 1,462.5 USD difference. [Para 150][Para 151][Para 154][Para 155] The mortgaged plots were not ordered sold, the mortgage ranking ahead of any division. [Para 146][Para 147]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Parental authority is awarded by reference to the child's best interests, and a parent who has in fact provided continuous day-to-day care has the stronger claim over one whose working hours leave the care to the other. On division, assets acquired during the marriage are joint, but the value of each spouse's separate pre-marital funds traced into them is returned to that spouse before the residue is halved. [Para 103][Para 135][Para 144]

**Obiter dicta:** alimony (អាហារកិច្ច) is refused where the breakdown cannot be attributed to one spouse alone, and costs may be split where the successful party is not wholly without fault. [Para 118][Para 159][Para 160]
