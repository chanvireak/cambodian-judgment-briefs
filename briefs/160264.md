# X v Y (Civil Judgment, Phnom Penh Court of First Instance, 30 April 2019)
Bench: 1 judge (name redacted, "J") · first instance · Doc ID 160264

## 1. Facts
On 01 August 2018 X bought "land L" from Y for 200,000 USD (two hundred thousand dollars) by private written contract, paying a deposit (ប្រាក់កក់) of 20,000 USD (twenty thousand dollars) at signature. [Para 16][Para 25] On 22 August 2018 Y came to X's house and offered him 40,000 USD (forty thousand dollars) in cash — twice the deposit — stating that he wished to rescind the sale. [Para 26]
X nevertheless sued for delivery of the land and for registration of the whole transfer in his favour. [Para 15] Y's position was simply that the contract had been undone by that offer. [Para 27]

## 2. Legal issues
1. Whether a seller who has taken a deposit may rescind the sale unilaterally by offering the buyer twice the deposit received? [Para 27]
2. Whether the buyer may still demand delivery of the land and registration of title once that rescission has been effected? [Para 28]

## 3. Arguments
**Plaintiff (X, buyer):** the sale contract is valid and binding, he paid the 20,000 USD deposit as agreed, and he is therefore entitled to have the land conveyed and the ownership transfer registered in his name. [Para 15][Para 25]
**Defendant (Y, seller):** rather than perform, he tendered 40,000 USD in cash at X's home on 22 August 2018 — double the deposit — and declared his intention to rescind, which he says brought the contract to an end. [Para 26][Para 27]

## 4. Judgment and reasoning
**Outcome:** all of X's claims are dismissed and costs fall on X. [Para 36][Para 37]
The court held the sale contract valid as between the parties but found that Y, as seller, had rescinded it by making an actual offer of 40,000 USD, twice the deposit handed over at signature — the mechanism expressly allowed by Article 518 of the Civil Code. [Para 27] Once the contract was thus undone there was no longer any basis for an order of delivery or of registration of the transfer, and the claim failed under Articles 414(1) and 411(1) of the Civil Code. [Para 28] Because every claim failed, the whole of the costs was charged to X under Article 64 of the Code of Civil Procedure. [Para 35]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A contract of sale concluded against a deposit may be rescinded by the seller on tendering the buyer twice the amount of the deposit, and the rescission so effected extinguishes the buyer's right to demand conveyance and registration of title. [Para 27][Para 28]

**Obiter dicta:** the formal observations that a judgment must reproduce the matters listed in Article 189 of the Code of Civil Procedure and that an appendix forms part of the judgment, not a mere attachment. [Para 17][Para 21]
