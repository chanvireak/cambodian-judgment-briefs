## Synthesis — the law this corpus actually applies

**What it is.** 108 briefs, 16 courts, 94,173 words, 5,602 pin cites. 100 of the 108 judgments are
from 2019; 61 come from Phnom Penh; 106 are first instance, 1 appeal, 1 cassation. So this is a body of
*trial-level* reasoning, not authority: only [143352](../briefs/143352.md) (Supreme Court, remand) and
[143351](../briefs/143351.md) (Appeal Court) bind anyone, and 143352 decided nothing on the merits.
Outcomes: 93 money judgments for the claimant, 8 partly allowed, 5 dismissed, 1 allowed, 1 remand.
The mix is overwhelmingly bank and microfinance debt recovery, with a tail of land, sale, family and
construction cases.

### Thread 1 — the interest ceiling, and what to do with the excess (55 cases)

Every court accepts a statutory ceiling; they do not agree on the number or the remedy.

| Proposition | Cases |
|---|---|
| 18% a year, Prakas No. 128 of 21 December 2011 + Art. 585(k) Civil Code | [143249](../briefs/143249.md), [143263](../briefs/143263.md), [143337](../briefs/143337.md), [143345](../briefs/143345.md) |
| 27% a year, same Prakas Art. 2 | [143317](../briefs/143317.md) (and [143307](../briefs/143307.md) tests the penalty against 27%) |
| 1.5% a month **is** 18% a year, so lawful without reduction | [143273](../briefs/143273.md), [143332](../briefs/143332.md) |
| 3% a month = 36% a year → replaced by 18% | [143349](../briefs/143349.md), [143333](../briefs/143333.md), [143334](../briefs/143334.md) |
| No rate agreed → statutory 5% a year, Art. 584 | [143299](../briefs/143299.md) |

Three different remedies for the same defect:

1. **Strike the excess and substitute the cap** — the majority: [143249](../briefs/143249.md),
   [143256](../briefs/143256.md), [143260](../briefs/143260.md), [143263](../briefs/143263.md),
   [143322](../briefs/143322.md), [143337](../briefs/143337.md), [143349](../briefs/143349.md).
2. **Only the excess is void, the clause survives** under Art. 585(a): [143252](../briefs/143252.md), and
   [143278](../briefs/143278.md) reaching the same result through "excessive profit".
3. **Excess already paid is credited against principal** rather than forfeited:
   [143289](../briefs/143289.md), [143319](../briefs/143319.md).

### Thread 2 — interest and late-payment penalty may not be stacked (the sharpest split)

The majority sequences the two heads instead of cumulating them: contractual interest runs only to the
date the claim is filed (or to contractual maturity), and the penalty runs from the day after until
payment — [143307](../briefs/143307.md), [143310](../briefs/143310.md), [143311](../briefs/143311.md),
[143316](../briefs/143316.md), [143328](../briefs/143328.md), [143350](../briefs/143350.md),
[143283](../briefs/143283.md), [143254](../briefs/143254.md), [143321](../briefs/143321.md).

A minority enforces both concurrently, capped:

| Cap applied | Case |
|---|---|
| penalty within 1.5× the statutory ceiling | [143313](../briefs/143313.md) |
| penalty at twice the contractual rate | [143331](../briefs/143331.md), [143347](../briefs/143347.md) |
| penalty within the 27% ceiling, beside admitted contractual interest | [143317](../briefs/143317.md) |
| ceiling limits *interest* only; a distinct penalty is outside it | [143346](../briefs/143346.md), [143322](../briefs/143322.md) |

### Thread 3 — a penalty dressed as something else is still interest

Courts look through the label: post-maturity "interest" and percentage "damages" are treated as interest
and capped ([143279](../briefs/143279.md)); a penalty is measured under the Civil Code and the Prakas
rather than left at large ([143281](../briefs/143281.md)); a liquidated penalty above the maximum is
reduced, not voided ([143303](../briefs/143303.md)). A "1% for the 96-month period" documentation fee is
a one-off charge, not an annual rate, and cannot be re-charged yearly ([143314](../briefs/143314.md)).
An unliquidated claim for expenses and lost time cannot be added on top of a high contractual rate
without proof of separate loss ([143308](../briefs/143308.md)).

### Thread 4 — admissions and absent defendants carry most of the docket

Two procedural engines produce most of the 93 money judgments:

- **Admission.** Where borrower and guarantor admit the contract, the balance and the default, the court
  decides on those facts without further proof: [143296](../briefs/143296.md), [143297](../briefs/143297.md),
  [143298](../briefs/143298.md), [143326](../briefs/143326.md), [143330](../briefs/143330.md),
  [143341](../briefs/143341.md), [143343](../briefs/143343.md), [143348](../briefs/143348.md),
  [143323](../briefs/143323.md). A plea of inability to pay never reduces the debt:
  [143290](../briefs/143290.md), [143306](../briefs/143306.md), [143341](../briefs/143341.md),
  [143345](../briefs/143345.md).
- **Absence after service.** Article 201 CPC treats a duly served defendant who does not appear as having
  confessed the pleaded facts: [143266](../briefs/143266.md), [143277](../briefs/143277.md),
  [143282](../briefs/143282.md), [143288](../briefs/143288.md), [143312](../briefs/143312.md),
  [143339](../briefs/143339.md), [143340](../briefs/143340.md), [143344](../briefs/143344.md),
  [143309](../briefs/143309.md). Service by publication plus posting suffices
  ([143248](../briefs/143248.md), [143294](../briefs/143294.md)). The mirror image: a *plaintiff* who
  fails to appear is dismissed by default under Article 200 ([143274](../briefs/143274.md)).

Note the tension in Thread 4 with Thread 5: absence is treated as confession, yet several courts still
require a consistent documentary file before entering judgment ([143339](../briefs/143339.md),
[143309](../briefs/143309.md)).

### Thread 5 — who proves what

- A borrower alleging that part of the principal was never disbursed, or was withheld, bears the burden;
  the signed contract and disbursement record stand until rebutted: [143257](../briefs/143257.md),
  [143265](../briefs/143265.md), [143304](../briefs/143304.md), [143338](../briefs/143338.md),
  [143300](../briefs/143300.md).
- A creditor suing on a running account must produce evidence the debtor acknowledged or that is
  independently verifiable; unilateral documents fail ([143280](../briefs/143280.md)), and a claim above
  the sum the debtor admits and the documents corroborate fails ([143302](../briefs/143302.md)).
- An agreement creating interest must be in a signed writing; an oral or partly written loan yields
  principal only ([143291](../briefs/143291.md)).

### Thread 6 — guarantee, security, and what the judgment may order

- Guarantors are jointly liable on their own contracts and are not released by the borrower's
  impecuniosity: [142328](../briefs/142328.md), [143315](../briefs/143315.md),
  [143341](../briefs/143341.md), [143330](../briefs/143330.md). Co-borrowers stay liable even if another
  co-borrower settled ([143270](../briefs/143270.md)). A third party who undertakes to pay the debt in
  exchange for property is personally bound ([143301](../briefs/143301.md)); on death the debt passes to
  the heirs ([143305](../briefs/143305.md)).
- A registered hypothec is valid and enforceable ([143253](../briefs/143253.md),
  [143250](../briefs/143250.md)), but a pledge does **not** entitle the creditor to an order confiscating
  the pledged property inside the money judgment — realisation belongs to enforcement
  ([143353](../briefs/143353.md)).
- Registered title beats an unregistered sale ([143335](../briefs/143335.md)); a sale signed by a
  non-owner without written authority creates nothing ([143255](../briefs/143255.md)); a seller may
  dissolve a sale for non-payment under Arts 407–408 ([143259](../briefs/143259.md)) or rescind against a
  deposit by tendering double ([160264](../briefs/160264.md)); overlapping possession certificates are
  resolved on the boundary descriptions and the land-office record ([143286](../briefs/143286.md)).

### Thread 7 — prescription

A written acknowledgment restarts a limitation period that has already run
([143285](../briefs/143285.md)); a part-payment acknowledged by the creditor as performance of the debt
interrupts it ([160182](../briefs/160182.md)); a bare "I understand" reply to a request for time varies
nothing and the original date stands ([160204](../briefs/160204.md)).

### What this corpus does not settle

Only one appellate decision exists and it remanded without deciding the merits
([143352](../briefs/143352.md)); the Appeal Court case is about construction valuation
([143351](../briefs/143351.md)). Every divergence above is therefore a split between coordinate
first-instance courts, not resolved law. The corpus also says almost nothing about consumer-credit
licensing, enforcement procedure after judgment, or family property beyond one custody judgment
([160224](../briefs/160224.md)).
